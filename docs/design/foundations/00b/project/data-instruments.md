# Data instruments

The seven instruments below were specified and drawn as board **00b · Data instruments**. Each one is
drawn at true size, in light and dark, with every variant and state, its accessibility labels, its AX5
and 200% forms, a greyscale check and its in-context crops — 57 artboards plus a notes artboard.

The board is the definition: [Pantopus Foundations 00b · Data instruments](https://claude.ai/artifact/QdfWJCxHQNu3EZsm3ggE3v)

## The grammar they share

1. Each instrument states its fact in words and numbers; the graphic repeats the fact and never
   replaces it.
2. Nothing adds up to a grade, score, percentage or verdict. No progress ring, completion ring,
   countdown, streak or confetti. An authority's own band names are allowed even when they contain a
   percentage. "N of M" appears only where a component's contract allows it.
3. Nothing depends on colour alone. The EPA AQI hues are the only sanctioned colour scale, and even
   there the category is printed in words.
4. Three shapes mean provenance and nothing else: a solid disc (Official), a ring (On record, not
   confirmed) and a solid disc with a knocked-out tick (You added this). No position marker, pointer,
   bullet, callout end, segment or badge is a plain disc or ring.
5. A mark, cell or segment is never a tap target on its own. The target is the row, the whole
   instrument or a labelled button.
6. Each instrument has a one-sentence summary and a spoken label, and the same facts appear nearby as
   rows or a list.
7. Every provenance mark keeps at least 3:1 contrast in every state. Staleness is always stated in
   words.
8. Widgets and share cards are standalone surfaces: they print the provenance word next to each mark
   or in a legend line inside the block.

## B. Assumptions

### Board

- Artboard naming override (R1): the exact name is printed as the top line inside each artboard and set as the frame title, because a canvas note above a frame would be a sticky.
- Off-scale micro offsets 6, 3, 2 and 1pt are used only where a component message states them; the medium type weights bodyMedium, bodySmallMedium and captionMedium are additions to the house scale (R3).
- Phone cards are 358 wide, left-aligned at a 16pt frame margin, with right margins of 19pt (iOS 393), 16pt (web-390) and 38pt (Android 412) (R2).
- The dark card edge is decorative border.strong, and dark sunken fills read darker than the card (R2, R5).
- Dark values this board assumes, which the house style does not define: surface.sunken #0F172A, surface.muted #111827, text.strong #F1F5F9, border.strong #374151 (R5).
- Stale marks are drawn as the 00a stale variant, text.primary at 50%, which passes 3:1 (R6, R11). This is the only place the board records the stale treatment; components do not repeat it.
- AX5 and 200% line heights are 1.2× the stated size; the contract gives sizes only.
- Apostrophes are set as ’ (U+2019) throughout.
- 00a is finished, so every component names its 00a parts directly: ProvenanceMark, SourceCaption, FreshnessLine, ScopeChip and KindGlyph carry no stand-in label anywhere on the board.
- 00c and 00d are not finished, so DateRow, FactRow, InlineErrorRow, TextActionRow, WarmingSkeleton and QuietDayReceipt are still stand-ins. Wherever a gutter names one of them it says so; the cells that draw one without naming it are listed in question 21.
