# Evidence and sourcing files

These files contain aggregate observations and government-source links. No owner names, property-level address/PIN pairs, listing photos, personal mailing addresses, raw county records or provider payloads are part of this release. November 5, 2026 is the observation date. A refreshed publisher file can yield a different count.

## How to audit a number

1. Pick a row in `field-dictionary.csv`. Filter `cohort-fields.csv` to that field and sum the observed column. It equals the all-record numerator. To reproduce the class rate, also filter to a class, sum the observed counts and divide by the sum of its cohort accounts. Include unscored cohorts in this all-selected-record denominator.
2. For Florida and MassGIS, filter `jurisdiction-fields.csv` to the same source and class, and sum the relevant field column. It reconciles to the corresponding rows in `cohort-fields.csv`. The government-files inventory supplies the roll, source URL, number read and eligible count for each file. The separate Boston layer stays out of MassGIS subtotals.
3. For any cohort’s mean field count, sum its observed field counts and divide by that cohort’s unique property records. The overall mean also weights by records. An intersection such as beds **and** total baths **and** living area requires the facts on the same account; the percentages of the individual fields cannot produce this number.
4. The five no-observation rows have zero aggregate numerator. Some other fields only qualify with specific meanings, such as confirmed owner-occupancy or measured covered parking area. Read the scoring rule before comparing vendors.

## Source-specific extraction protocol

- **Florida.** Enumerate the 2026 NAL final files for 64 counties and the three available preliminary files for the remainder. Filter state DOR use codes 001, 003, 004, 017 and 018. Deduplicate within county on local parcel ID. Join the public county names to Census FIPS, retaining the state’s tax district and optional county use code separately. Exclude shared condo land and building counts from the unit. The state defines total living or usable area as effective improvement area. [Data portal](https://www.floridarevenue.com/property/Pages/DataPortal_RequestAssessmentRollGISData.aspx) · [Submission standards](https://floridarevenue.com/property/Pages/Cofficial_CompleteSubRollEval.aspx)
- **Massachusetts.** Use each town’s latest published assessor DBF, exclude deleted rows and nonunique/blank local property IDs. Use 101 single-family (explicit townhouse styles split out), 102 condo, 112 apartment and 340/342 office. Resolve county and FIPS from the official town-ID crosswalk. Credit deed area only with explicit acres/square feet. `RES_AREA` is residential living area; `BLD_AREA` varies by town. In Boston, select unique city PIDs instead, separate residential condos with unit numbers from condo main and parking interests, and keep affirmative owner occupancy only. [MassGIS standard](https://www.mass.gov/doc/standard-for-digital-parcels-and-related-data-sets-version-3/download) · [Boston layer](https://gisportal.boston.gov/arcgis/rest/services/Assessing/properties_boston_gov/FeatureServer/0)
- **Cook County.** Use 2026 first/single improvement cards for houses (classes 202–209, 234 and 278), classes 210/295 for townhouses, and residential condos 299 explicitly excluding parking/common parcels. Read same-year situs and parcel-universe records by 14-digit PIN for address, municipal and tax-district fields. Count total baths only when both components exist; the source can default a missing full bath to one. Exterior-measured area and chimney-flue counts are supplemental. The separate address experiment tested normalized street (including unit), city and ZIP against local keys. [Residential characteristics](https://datacatalog.cookcountyil.gov/Property-Taxation/Assessor-Single-and-Multi-Family-Improvement-Chara/x54s-btds) · [Condo characteristics](https://datacatalog.cookcountyil.gov/Property-Taxation/Assessor-Residential-Condominium-Unit-Characterist/3r7i-mrz4)
- **New York City and Philadelphia.** On PLUTO 26v2, use one tax lot per BBL; class A, noncondo C/D with 5+ residences, and O. Keep ordinary gross building area, with separate treatment of condo conventions. Stories describe the tallest building; a NYC tax lot number is not scored as a platted lot. On Philadelphia OPA, use one parcel number per strict category/building-description match (detached, row, unit-designated residential condo, apartment, office). Keep integer bathroom counts, exterior-only condition and garage bays separate from total-bath/overall-condition/total-parking rows. [NYC PLUTO](https://data.cityofnewyork.us/City-Government/Primary-Land-Use-Tax-Lot-Output-PLUTO-/64uk-42ks) · [Philadelphia OPA](https://data.phila.gov/visualizations/property-assessments/)
- **King and Dallas counties.** In King County, join parcel Major/Minor to exactly one residential, apartment-complex or commercial record. Keep single-dwelling residences with present-use 2/6, townhouse plats 29, complexes 11/16/18 with 5+ units, and office 106/118/122. King separately counts full, three-quarter and half baths. In Dallas, require a 2026 Dallas-jurisdiction account, the appropriate tax use and one principal qualifying improvement. A13 condos require a unit ID. B11 apartments require at least five units; F10 offices require a qualifying office building. Measure covered garage/carport area only from identified improvements with positive area. [King County extracts](https://info.kingcounty.gov/assessor/DataDownload/default.aspx) · [Dallas data products](https://www.dallascad.org/DataProducts.aspx)
- **Los Angeles County.** Isolate 2025 in the multiyear official roll. Classify SFR and explicit townhouse-form SFR separately, CND with a specified unit number, income apartments with 5+ units, and commercial office buildings. Require a single improvement line for building facts. Keep main square footage and undifferentiated total bathroom counts as supplemental. Credit only an explicit nine-digit situs ZIP with a nonzero suffix. [LA County data item](https://www.arcgis.com/home/item.html?id=70d93266f45a4080a97b285a471493cd) · [Terms of use](https://egis-lacounty.hub.arcgis.com/pages/terms-of-use)

The saved URLs reproduce **source counts**, not the final policy by themselves. For example, the raw Cook query reports exterior-measured building area, Philadelphia reports an integer bath count, and the raw NYC query reports a tax-lot number. The dictionary explains which were withheld or reported separately. Changes in coverage, fiscal years, classification, upstream licensing or rights should produce a new release and comparison, not an overwrite of these dated results.
