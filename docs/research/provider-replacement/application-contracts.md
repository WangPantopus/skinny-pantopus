# Application and vendor contracts

Code audit at [e49cb33a6a](https://github.com/WangPantopus/skinny-pantopus/tree/e49cb33a6a9281bf19aa9e1dfd5c0dec10a02c25). “Current” describes a reachable code path. Keys, product entitlements, launch flags, live delivery and hosted behavior require separate verification. The free experiment made no ATTOM, Smarty or Lob API calls.

## ATTOM products Pantopus calls

The adapter targets ATTOM Property API `v1.0.0`. For a home it sends a street line with the secondary/unit component and a city/state/ZIP line. The trend call uses `geoid=ZI<ZIP>`. Case-insensitive field paths below follow the county-record serializer. A vendor catalog, an endpoint the app calls, and a populated field on a particular property have different evidentiary value. [Adapter and request parameters](../../../backend/services/ai/propertyIntelligenceService.js) · [ATTOM endpoint documentation](https://api.developer.attomdata.com/docs)

| Endpoint | Returned subject and app use | Replacement decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `/property/detail` | Consolidated physical, legal, use, assessment/tax and sale characteristics for a matched property. Optional owner data is consumed on a separate claim path. Detail also supports prefill, parcel classification, exemptions and the house illustration. | Use the public-record index, store the jurisdiction’s identifier and publish only approved fields. Resolve the unit independently. Household entries carry household provenance. |
| `/attomavm/detail` | Property-specific automated valuation model (AVM). The app reads amount, range, confidence, date and potentially per-area, monthly-change and condition-dependent values. | Suppress model outputs until Pantopus has a licensed eligible estimate or an independently validated model for that asset and market. Label official tax values, assessment dates and recorded sales separately. |
| `/salestrend/snapshot` | ZIP-level sales series. The profile compares current and previous median sale prices and uses a ±2% band for trend. | Derive comparable ZIP medians from rights-cleared recorded transactions when sample size, time windows and disclosure permit. A regional index can support a separately labeled trend. Missing observations yield unknown. |

The **46 screenshot rows** are a subset of the endpoint and display contract. Six headline tiles repeat facts. Other named fields include building/adjusted/standard/attic/basement area, room and bath breakdowns, units, mobile home, framing, foundation, roof shape, heating fuel/water/sewer, frontage, depth, zoning, pool, survey references, assessment components, last-sale amount/date/type/recording document and AVM extras. The existing serializer also traverses eligible additional payload leaves, deduplicates labels and limits text, depth and rows. The replacement should use an allowlist with an explicit subject and purpose for each additional field. **Compatibility traps to fix during migration.** The existing structured AVM confidence row reads `avm.amount.scr`, whereas the normalized profile reads `avm.confidence`. Preserve the semantics of the chosen source and reconcile the two views rather than inventing a score. The current claim call passes the street/address, city, state and ZIP without a dedicated unit field. Carry and verify an explicit unit before using fresh ownership evidence for a condo. The serializer omits many unknown or nonpositive numeric values. Test legitimate zero fireplaces/parking or a studio under the new projector. [Serializer](../../../backend/serializers/countyRecordSerializer.js) · [Claim call](../../../backend/routes/homeOwnership.js)

**Beyond Pantopus today.** ATTOM offers other products such as recorded deed and mortgage history, ownership/transaction intelligence, foreclosure, neighborhood/school and hazard data, and other valuation analytics. The three adapter calls above define this replacement’s actual integration scope. Catalog inclusion does not establish entitlement to a product, coverage of every property type, or permission to redistribute it. A new feature that requires those products needs its own source, rights and accuracy benchmark. [ATTOM data catalog](https://www.attomdata.com/data/) · [API coverage and delivery FAQ](https://www.attomdata.com/solutions/delivery/property-data-api/faqs/)

### If Pantopus later needs more of the ATTOM catalog

These are separate product families and acquisition decisions. They were outside the 46-field test and the three network calls found in the app.

| ATTOM capability | Possible independent route | What remains to establish |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Deed, transfer, sales history and comparables | Recorder/transfer files plus normalized sale arm’s-length/type, geography and dates | Recording fees, commercial display, nondisclosure markets, chain-of-title completeness and quality of comparable selection. A last-sale row may already be available through property detail. |
| Mortgages, liens, distressed properties and foreclosure events | Recorder/court/trustee sources, or an explicitly licensed feed | Satisfactions, event chronology, protected records, currentness, and allowed use. Keep these outside the shared Home/property UI unless separately designed. |
| Rental AVMs, valuation analytics and comparable sales | Authorized rent/transaction inputs and a separately validated, class-specific model | Confidence calibration, temporal holdouts, eligibility, suppression, and rights to inputs/outputs. Unit advertised rent, actual rent and an estimated market rent are different observations. |
| Building permits and parcel/boundary products | Permitting departments, state/local GIS, legal parcel/condo regimes | Geographic coverage, permit vs completion, topology, vintages and derivatives. Polygon location alone cannot identify a leased suite. |
| Neighborhood, school, environmental and hazard products | Census, authorized districts and official hazard layers; qualify third-party analytics separately | Attribution, spatial/temporal precision and meaning of each statistic. Area-level facts should not populate household fields. |

An ATTOM-vetted or modeled derivative may have no exact independent equivalent even when its underlying records are public. Validate feature-level parity against independent ground truth and the actual Pantopus contract. No current account entitlements or live ATTOM response completeness were claimed by this study. [ATTOM product catalog](https://www.attomdata.com/data/) · [API dictionary and endpoint groups](https://api.developer.attomdata.com/docs) · [Valuation products](https://www.attomdata.com/solutions/ai-powered/valuation-analytics/avm/)

## Smarty and the Add Home decision

| Flow | Current owning code | Replacement behavior |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Search and resolve a suggestion | Shared `/api/geo/autocomplete` and `/api/geo/resolve` use the geo provider. `/api/homes/check-address` checks existing homes and canonical hashes. [Geo providers](../../../backend/services/geo/index.js) · [Home routes](../../../backend/routes/home.js) | Preserve suggestion and resolution behavior, including typed units, manual input, budgets and private home-conflict responses. The national address index can augment this separately from removing Smarty. |
| Validate street/unit and classify | `POST /api/v1/address/validate` and `/validate/unit` combine Google normalization/geocoding with Smarty US Street postal results. Smarty parses DPV, RDI (residential/commercial indicator), vacancy, commercial mail receiving agency (CMRA), secondary hints and footnotes. A provider failure can produce `SERVICE_ERROR`. [Smarty adapter](../../../backend/services/addressValidation/smartyProvider.js) · [Decision engine](../../../backend/services/addressValidation/addressDecisionEngine.js) | Google already requests USPS CASS processing and reads DPV, vacancy, CMRA and carrier route. Qualify its actual upstream coverage, rights and failure modes before using it as the postal adapter. Preserve unknown or missing residential/business and secondary evidence explicitly. [Google adapter](../../../backend/services/addressValidation/googleProvider.js) · [Google policies](https://developers.google.com/maps/documentation/address-validation/policies) |
| Secondary list/count | A separately gated Smarty enrichment adapter can request a secondary count and list and check a submitted unit. It is distinct from street validation and optional Google Places classification. [Secondary adapter](../../../backend/services/addressValidation/secondaryAddressProvider.js) · [Rollout configuration](../../../backend/config/addressVerification.js) | Use a permitted unit-level assessor/manager source or a licensed secondary service. A count alone cannot validate a submitted unit. Preserve the retry/manual path. |
| Create, claim and Mailbox Check | Home create validates the address or takes a constrained recent-match outage path. The address claim route has an `OK` / 0.8 auto-verification threshold for an address claim. Mailbox Check reads stored postal flags and the caller’s postcard. [Validation routes](../../../backend/routes/addressValidation.js) · [Mailbox Check](../../../backend/services/mailboxCheckService.js) | Separate physical existence, postal delivery, residence and household authority. A successful postal result can establish address quality. Membership, ownership and other people’s private records need their own proof. |

**Specific regression to catch before cutover.** A synthetic adapter/decision check reproduced on the audited baseline found that `D` with footnote `N1` reached `OK` at 0.85 and blank DPV reached `OK` at 0.75 on its fixture. The parser currently derives its missing-secondary boolean from `CC`. Treat a required missing unit as a correction, unknown deliverability as pending, and unconfirmed secondary as a different outcome. Re-run the real parser, engine, claim and create routes plus all clients under the proposed adapter. No user privilege or live mail was exercised by that fixture. [Smarty US Street field definitions](https://www.smarty.com/docs/cloud/us-street-api) · [Original synthetic check](studies/provider-replacement-review.pdf)

## Lob and physical mail

| Flow | Current owning code | Replacement behavior |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Address verification postcard | `/api/v1/address/verify/mail/start`, status, resend and confirm. Pantopus owns the attempts and codes. Lob accepts the print job and returns a receipt. [Validation routes](../../../backend/routes/addressValidation.js) · [Mail verification](../../../backend/services/addressValidation/mailVerificationService.js) | Admit a durable attempt, send to the exact eligible unit, persist the receipt, and reconcile an unknown send before retry/failover. Enforce expiry, replay limits and private responses. |
| Home postcard and ownership/residency flows | `/api/homes/:id/postcard-requests`, its status/cancel operations, and legacy `request-postcard` / `verify-postcard` share Home-specific admission and redemption rules. [Home postcard requests](../../../backend/services/homePostcardRequestService.js) · [Home postcard processing](../../../backend/services/homePostcardService.js) | Preserve request limits, authorization, durable state, code attempts, address-change and revoked-access checks. A carrier scan cannot replace successful redemption. |
| Block Founders invitations | The separate custom-postcard path handles invitation content, spend/reservations, recipient opt-out and neighborhood attribution. [Block Founders service](../../../backend/services/blockFoundersService.js) · [Custom/verification dispatch](../../../backend/services/addressValidation/lobMailProvider.js) | Qualify both templates, idempotent reservation/send/accounting, recipient opt-out and no-mail rules. It must be possible to disable invitation traffic separately from verification mail. |
| Tracking, failure and retries | The raw-body signed webhook deduplicates provider events and routes Home-card events by metadata. Home-specific processing currently acknowledges only selected events. The address flow has different terminal handling. [Webhook](../../../backend/routes/lobWebhook.js) · [Mail vendor service](../../../backend/services/addressValidation/mailVendorService.js) | Normalize events, preserve both old IDs and provider namespace on in-flight pieces, and define failed, returned and rerouted behavior across both flows. A tracking event describes a piece in the mailstream. [Lob tracking semantics](https://help.lob.com/print-and-mail/getting-data-and-results/tracking-your-mail) |

Digital Mail, neighbor messaging, residency letters, household actions and certain Plans features consume the resulting trust state. Review both participants’ views when changing that state. Physical verification, marketing invitations and ordinary digital messages have separate fulfillment/permission boundaries. [Exit tests and sequencing](delivery-and-cost.md)
