## Nationwide discovery without repeating settled work

1. For each unserved FIPS, consult the portable route and the public evidence files national-routes-by-county.csv, missing-facts-by-county.csv and statewide-inventory.csv. Track a source by issuer, endpoint, table, field grain and vintage. Reopen a settled lead only for new evidence such as a newer release, newly documented fields or newly obtained permission.
2. Check county assessor and GIS bulk-download catalogs, state assessor/equalization extracts, statewide address/E911 programs, regional councils, state GIS clearinghouses and municipal CAMA files. Search government ArcGIS Hub/REST, Socrata, CKAN and Data.gov. Verify the publisher and its rights at the originating agency even when the file is mirrored. A normal HTTP status and a populated field name establish neither semantics nor coverage.
3. Use federal NAD and open state/city address points for addresses; preserve unit, building, alias and ZIP distinctions. Use Census/NCES geographies, FEMA and other original public layers for area-level facts when appropriate. A federal address point or map footprint supplies no private unit interiors. Prioritize official alternate publishers before drafting a records request. [USDOT National Address Database](https://www.transportation.gov/gis/national-address-database) · [MassGIS parcel definitions](https://www.mass.gov/info-details/massgis-data-property-tax-parcels)
4. Dedicated sweep slices include the 300 largest no-file counties with only a letter or resident-supplied lead; state and regional open-data catalogs; regional councils around major metros; and statewide files previously thought to require a request. Among the 50 states, there was no property release at all for AL, AR, AZ, DE, HI, IN, KY, LA, MS, ND, NH, SD or WY. Puerto Rico had 78 unserved property jurisdictions; AS, GU, MP and VI are represented separately as well.
5. For each qualifying source, stage a full county/municipal report. For each failure, name the missing fields, denominator and reason, source URL/date, effective license, and exact evidence that would reopen it. Keep private household and source files off Git and avoid running the identical survey again.
