# Read-only review of PR #253 (branch claude/stream1-poster-fault-fee @ be92a0f71), 2026-09-23 ~07:30 UTC

Reviewer: a read-only coordinator review agent (no DB, Stripe or code changes). Treat each finding as a lead to
reproduce, not as proven.

## 1. HIGH — the fee can be captured at Stripe but never recorded, and the payment is stuck
- **The race.** The no-show reservation does not fence other gig writes.
  - The poster's worker-no-show path (`backend/routes/gigs.js:6998-7016`, hold release `:7035-7043`) updates the Gig
    without checking the payment.
  - The capture (`gigStopService.js:298-307`) runs without a re-check.
  - `record_gig_fee_capture` refuses once the gig changed (migration `:126-128`).
- **Scenario:**
  1. The worker reports a poster no-show, and the reservation sets `capture_pending`.
  2. While the worker's request is at Stripe, the poster reports a worker no-show. The update still matches
     `status='assigned'`, so the gig becomes cancelled (`no_show_worker`). `cancelAuthorization` throws on
     `capture_pending`, but the poster gets 200 with `holdRelease:'pending'`.
  3. The worker's capture succeeds: 25% is taken, and Stripe releases the rest.
  4. `record_gig_fee_capture` returns `PAYMENT_CHANGED`. The partial-capture webhook hits the same error and
     swallows it (`stripeWebhooks.js:625-631`), so Stripe won't redeliver.
- **End state:**
  - The poster is charged on a task recorded as the worker's no-show.
  - The payment stays `capture_pending` with the fee `pending` forever, and nothing settles.
  - The worker's retry gets 400.
- **Also:** the worker's own Start Work can commit in that window. Start Work checks the payment status only in app
  code (`stripeService.js:568-590`), and `bindGigPaymentSnapshot` (`gigs.js:91-98`) doesn't bind `payment_status`.
- **Suggested fix:**
  - Fence gig status/start/assignment changes in the DB while the payment's fee is `pending` (a trigger with a flag
    set only by the record function).
  - Reject the poster path when `payment_status='capture_pending'`.
  - In `record_gig_fee_capture`, always persist a valid fee-capture proof on the Payment. Only skip the gig
    cancellation, and flag it for review.

## 2. HIGH — fee refunds can't be processed or recorded, and the worker share still settles
- **Where:**
  - `paymentRefundService.js:105-106` requires `amount_received === amount_total`.
  - That check is used by `reconcile()` (149-157), which both `create()` (181-182) and the `charge.refunded` webhook
    (`reconcileEvent`, 230-238) call.
  - Fee settlement checks only local refund rows (migration `:384-390`).
  - The default refundable amount is `amount_total` (`20260922020300:140`).
- **Effect:**
  - A support/admin in-app refund of a captured fee returns 409.
  - A Stripe-dashboard refund fails reconciliation on every webhook delivery and is never recorded.
  - Within the 48 h cooling-off, `settleFee` still pays the worker, and after settlement there is no clawback.
  - Even once refunds can be recorded, the full `amount_total` default exceeds the capture, a full fee refund never
    reaches `refunded_full`, and `settleFee` returns `PAYMENT_STATE` hourly.
- **Suggested fix:**
  - Accept an exact recorded fee capture in `verifyProvider`/`reconcile`.
  - Cap the refundable amount at the captured fee, and treat a full fee refund as full.
  - Skip or zero fee settlement when the fee was refunded.

## 3. HIGH — a worker can charge 25% before the scheduled start
- **Where:** eligibility checks only `accepted_at` + 24 h (`gigs.js:6855-6863`; migration `:72`).
  `gigStopRoute.test.js:95,122` accepts a report while `scheduled_start` is still in the future.
- **Existing UI copy:** "Only report after the agreed start time has passed" (Android sheet).
- **Suggested fix:** when `scheduled_start` is set, also require `now > scheduled_start + buffer`, in both the route
  and `prepare_gig_fee_capture`. Reuse the poster path's existing grace if there is one.

## 4. MEDIUM-HIGH — a stalled no-show reservation never recovers and blocks the gig
- **Where:**
  - `payment_intent.canceled` (`stripeWebhooks.js:803-825`) moves `capture_pending` to `canceled`
    (`paymentStateMachine.js:60-63`).
  - `record_gig_fee_capture` accepts only `capture_pending`.
  - `reconcileNoShowFee` (`gigStopService.js:311-319`) requires `capture_pending` too, and runs only from the
    succeeded and dispute webhooks.
  - No job reconciles reservations. Hold expiry skips them (`20260922020800:49` needs `capture_attempts=0`).
- **Effect:**
  - The owner can't cancel (`PAYMENT_REVIEW`) and the worker can't start.
  - After hold expiry, the payment becomes `canceled` with the fee still `pending`.
  - Retries then return 409 `NO_SHOW_PAYMENT_CHANGED`, and the gig stays `assigned` forever.
- **Suggested fix:**
  - The not-charged branch accepts `canceled`.
  - The canceled handler routes pending-fee payments to `reconcileNoShowFee`.
  - Add a scheduled reconciler that replays the same capture.
  - Rethrow transient errors in the webhooks.

## 5. MEDIUM — history and pending earnings show the full task amount
- **Where:** `pays.js:704-713` and `810-815`; `wallet.js:170-190`.
- **Effect:** the poster sees a $100 debit on a cancelled task. The worker sees ~$85 "in review", then ~$21 lands.
- **Suggested fix:** use `publicGigFee` / `capturedFeeCents` in these readers.

## 6. MEDIUM (unverified) — fees under $0.50
- **Which fees:** a late-cancel fee is under 50¢ for standard tasks below $10 and strict tasks below $5; a no-show
  fee for tasks below $2.
- **Risk:** Stripe's 50¢ minimum may refuse a small partial capture.
  - `captureFee` swallows the error (`gigStopService.js:136-143`).
  - A late cancel would then stay `fee_pending` until the hold expires; a no-show falls into #4.
- **Next step:** check in Stripe TEST. If Stripe refuses it, treat fees under 50¢ as not charged before reserving.

## 7. LOW-MEDIUM — settlement routes on the existence of `metadata.gig_fee`
- **Where:** `processPendingTransfers.js:163-164`.
- **Effect:** a leftover `pending` fee on a later fully captured payment goes to `settleFee`, fails with
  `FEE_PROOF_REQUIRED` hourly, and the worker is never paid.
- **Suggested fix:** route on `capturedFeeCents(payment) !== null`.

## 8. LOW
- **Webhook-recorded fees send no notice:** the poster gets no `no_show_reported` notice (`gigs.js:7085-7095`).
- **Duplicate incidents:** the incident lookup/insert isn't atomic.
- **Reliability rule:** a retry after a failed gig update applies it zero times in the not-charged path
  (`createdIncident`, `gigs.js:7049`).
- **Misleading errors:** `NO_SHOW_STOP_ACTIVE` and post-capture `PAYMENT_CHANGED` both say "needs review before a
  no-show can be reported".
- **Rollout:** shipped clients reject the new stop shapes (web `gigStopRecovery.ts:63,76`, iOS
  `GigStopDTOs.swift:45-46,156`, Android `GigStopValidation.kt:50,74`). This fails closed, but owners get "could not
  be verified" until clients update.
