app has a place for it. This folder is the whole picture, so the build can

## How it came about

- November 11: the founder asked for a business catalog and claim flow that is
  the best in the world. The business file plan was written, and step 1 was
  built and went live the same day.
- November 11, evening: pointing at the Plans PR, the founder said the app
  should connect as one graph and businesses should do business wherever they
  can, and asked how far that is. The four shared pieces were proposed.
- The founder approved them, and said: no building yet; all the designs, the
  architecture and the plans first, so it can be done at once when ready.

## What is decided and what is not

- **Said by the founder:** the four pieces; the one new table for the customer
  record; no build until the founder says go; step 1 of the business file.
- **Taken as the plan answers them, not confirmed one by one:** a customer
  first met through an invoice or booking is the business's own in Plans; the
  statement covers every feature from its first day.
- **Open:** the shared contract's calls S1 to S9; the business file contract's
  calls B1 to B9, among them the second new table, `PublicBusinessRecord`.
- **Not done:** the screens (one sample comes first); the lawyer's and
  accountant's reads; the by-hand test with crews; any build.

The founder's steps are in [NEXT_STEPS](../../../NEXT_STEPS.md), "The business
graph".

## The promises the build must keep

1. With every new switch off, the app is exactly today's app.
2. A person's name, address and home stay private. A business's staff see a
   first name and what was bought.
3. A business never pays twice for a customer it already had, and can see why
   it paid for each one.
4. Every public fact about a business shows its source and its date.
5. Nobody pays to be seen.
