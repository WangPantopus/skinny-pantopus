## Done

- **S1 rule cleanups** (server half): [PR 2259](https://github.com/WangPantopus/skinny-pantopus/pull/2259), merged, verified on web, iOS and Android. Heads-ups about crime and suspicion (`theft`, `vandalism`, `suspicious`, `scam`, `crime_incident`) are refused at creation with `400 ALERT_KIND_NOT_ALLOWED`; `official_notice` and `public_safety` only from Official or curator accounts, each with `sourceUrl` (stored as `post_metadata.source_url`); an edit can't switch a post to a refused kind; businesses can't post to the Place feed (`403 BUSINESS_CANNOT_POST`, also from `POST /api/businesses/:id/posts`). Old posts of those kinds stay readable and editable. The seeder now sends each alert's source. Contract §1, §2.5 and §8 updated.
- **S4 topics** (server half): [PR 2264](https://github.com/WangPantopus/skinny-pantopus/pull/2264), merged, verified on web, iOS and Android: the twelve topics in `Post_topic_check`, `?topic=` on the legacy Place feed, topic mutes with `areaId` (listed in `GET /api/posts/mute` as `topic_mutes`, undone by area), `PostMute.area_id`. Contract §2.9 and §4.1 updated.
- **One read check (S5, part 1):** [PR 2268](https://github.com/WangPantopus/skinny-pantopus/pull/2268), merged, verified on web, iOS and Android. `canReadPost` (the post page's rule) lives in `backend/services/pulse/readAccess.js` with `readablePosts` and `POST_READ_SELECT`; other people's profile post lists and the business Mentions list use it (both showed posts their readers couldn't open). Local-profile activity uses it too: [PR 2270](https://github.com/WangPantopus/skinny-pantopus/pull/2270), merged (signed out, it listed neighborhood posts the post page refuses). Global pins, which head every feed's first page, use it as well, with the viewer's blocks, mutes and hides: [PR 2276](https://github.com/WangPantopus/skinny-pantopus/pull/2276) (a pinned post skipped all of them).
- **Adult attestation (S5.6, server):** [PR 2269](https://github.com/WangPantopus/skinny-pantopus/pull/2269), merged: `POST /api/pulse/adult-attestation`, `User.adult_attested_at`, `403 AGE_NOT_ELIGIBLE` for an under-18 date of birth. New `backend/routes/pulse.js`, mounted at `/api/pulse`.
- **S7 lifecycle, part 1 (columns):** [PR 2273](https://github.com/WangPantopus/skinny-pantopus/pull/2273), merged, verified on web, iOS and Android. `Post.expires_at`, `Post.resolution`, `Post.claim_mode` (default `choose`); `share` and `offer` are valid in `Post_post_type_check` (nothing creates them until S8); `PostCategoryTTL` gains Share 3 days and Offer 7. Legacy post rows carry the three fields, and marking an Ask solved now also sets `resolution: answered`. Contract §4.1 updated.
- **S7 lifecycle, part 2 (endings):** [PR 2274](https://github.com/WangPantopus/skinny-pantopus/pull/2274), merged, verified on web, iOS and Android. The archive job runs hourly and ends each post with its `resolution`, `state` in step: an Ask `closed` or `closed_unanswered` (replies from someone other than the asker), Share and Offer `expired`, a Heads-up at its `expires_at` or after 48 hours, an Event `happened` 24 hours after it ends (or after it starts, when it has no end), Lost and found `closed`; an answered Ask stays `answered`, and Asks solved before part 1 get `answered`. The feed, map, Sports lane and global pins leave out a post past its `expires_at` at once. Restoring a post that ended by time gives it one more window of its type's days. Contract §5 updated.
- **S7 lifecycle, part 3 (day-6 reminder):** [PR 2275](https://github.com/WangPantopus/skinny-pantopus/pull/2275). One `ask_still_open` reminder per Ask in its last day, "Did a reply help?" or "Still need an answer?", held during the asker's quiet hours (hourly job). The asker's two actions: `POST /api/pulse/posts/:postId/keep-open` (7 more days, once) and `POST /api/pulse/posts/:postId/close` (`closed` or `closed_unanswered`). Contract §2.12, §6 and §8 updated.
- **No replies across a block:** [PR 2277](https://github.com/WangPantopus/skinny-pantopus/pull/2277). Replying to a comment by someone you blocked, or who blocked you, now answers `403` ("You can't reply to this comment."); before, the reply went through and its author got a "replied to your comment" notification quoting it, though their comment list hid it. The post-author notification skips a block too.
- **Notices across a block:** [PR 2281](https://github.com/WangPantopus/skinny-pantopus/pull/2281), merged. The notification list, the unread counts and the live badge leave out notices from someone across a block, either direction, including ones from before it; unblocking brings them back. Contract §5 now states the block rules.
- **Radius cap on the legacy feed and map:** [PR 2287](https://github.com/WangPantopus/skinny-pantopus/pull/2287), merged. The Place feed, its Sports lane and the saved-place feed read at most 100 miles around their point (the apps' default; the 1,000-mile and Global choices read 100 miles, and an odd `radiusMiles` reads the default); the map reads Place posts only within 100 miles of its box's center and returns at most 200 markers per layer. Before, `radiusMiles=25000` or a whole-country box read every Place post. Contract §5 updated.

- **Held and removed posts (S11, PB's half, part 1):** [PR 2290](https://github.com/WangPantopus/skinny-pantopus/pull/2290). `Post.moderation_state` (`live`, `held`, `removed`) with `moderation_rule`, `moderation_note` and `moderated_at`. `canReadPost`: a removed post reads only for its author; a held one for its author and staff (admins). The feeds, the map, global pins, the household feed and Today's local updates show live posts only. Nothing writes `held` or `removed` yet (PS's actions and S8's held sales will). Contract §4.1 and §5 updated.
