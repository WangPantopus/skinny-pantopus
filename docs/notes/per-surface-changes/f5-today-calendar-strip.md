# Research-backed changes for f5-today-calendar-strip

- Remove the class hue from the strip and use one ink colour. Provenance is by shape, at least 12pt where it must be told apart; class is carried by the list-row glyphs [a11y, dates]
- Cells are not tap targets on native. The strip is one adjustable accessibility element ('Tuesday 20 October: Recycling and garbage, city schedule, not confirmed') and tapping it scrolls to the rows. List rows are at least 44pt; update f4's 'reached by' line [dates, a11y]
- Weekend tint needs a second cue or should be removed (sunken vs base is 1.10:1) [a11y]
- Holiday move: the mark sits on the moved day with a struck ghost on the usual day and a caption naming the holiday and source [dates]
- Rows further out than tomorrow show 'in 11 days · Fri 30 Oct'; Tonight, Today and Tomorrow stand alone; everything is computed at render [widgets, dates]
- Fix civic rows: 'Property tax, 2nd half — due Sat 31 Oct · Clark County Treasurer'. Show the appeal only as a household-computed date. Remove the Saturday Camas bulk pickup. Voter row reads 'Online or mail — must arrive by Mon 26 Oct' [dates, share]
- Empty after a successful check: drawn cells plus 'Checked — nothing in the next 14 days' plus '+ Add a date'. Error: no strip [onboarding, widgets]
- A summary line, 'Next 14 days: 9 items'; add AX5, greyscale and dark tinted frames [a11y]
