# Research-backed changes for f7-today-widget

- iOS sizes on 393x852: 158x158 / 338x158 / 338x354pt with 16pt margins. Android: responsive, drawn at the 245x115dp minimum and at a comfortable size, with sizes from the documented grid formula [widgets, prompting]
- The medium strip spans the full content width (~306pt, ~21pt pitch) with marks at least 8–12pt; label only 'Today' and week starts, or initials at 11pt or larger [widgets, a11y]
- Small widget: exactly 4 pieces and 1 tap target, opening the hero's own section. Medium: two link regions of at least 44pt/48dp (hero; strip plus air → Today strip). Large: the year strip links to the place file year band. Pass `section=` [widgets]
- Staleness is judged per fact. Pickup and dates render from ISO dates until the snapshot horizon; AQI shows 'observed 7:00 AM' and greys on its own; past the horizon, grey everything with 'As of Mon 7:40 AM' then 'Open to refresh' [widgets]
- Timeline entries at each local midnight for 14 days plus the evening-before-pickup boundary; ages use system relative-date text; no preformatted strings [widgets]
- The AQI micro-bar uses ColorVision Assist with gaps and always prints the category name (short form 'Sensitive groups') [dataviz, widgets]
- Tinted/accented frames show strip and band as well as marks; weekend cells use opacity; marks are widgetAccentable. Add a StandBy frame (no background, larger type, greyscale shapes) plus red low-light, or declare disfavored locations [widgets, a11y]
- Android chrome uses Material dynamic roles on API 31+ with Pantopus fallback; add one non-blue wallpaper frame; system corner radius [widgets]
- AX frames for each size drop the strip, band and lanes and keep the hero line, place label and air as wrapping text. One combined VoiceOver/TalkBack label [a11y]
- Smart Stack relevance: high from 5 PM on the evening before pickup and while AQI is 101 or above; medium for a date within 3 days; 0 on quiet days. Donate an intent on Today open [widgets]
- Place label is the street name only ('Larkspur Loop'); no logo; no keeper overdue text [competitive, companion]
- Fixtures HOME A on TODAY; 19 frames in 4 batches; dark and tinted twins listed explicitly [prompting]
