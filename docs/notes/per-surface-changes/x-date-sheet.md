# Research-backed changes for x-date-sheet

- Choose the control by kind. Lease, notice, insurance and warranty get a typeable field (Android docked picker or INPUT_MODE_TEXT with the keyboard toggle drawn; iOS compact style with a month/year jump; web typed input). Pickup gets a weekday chooser. Show 31 Mar 2027 reached in 2 or 3 taps [dates, a11y]
- Add 'Not sure of the day' (month + year), shown as 'about Dec 2026' everywhere, with the reminder at the start of that month [dates]
- Lease ends asks 'How much notice does your lease require?' with options 20 (WA minimum) / 30 / 60 / Other, plus 'check your lease'. It creates a linked Notice deadline row (Mon 1 Mar 2027) and the reminders attach to that row [dates]
- 'Remind me' draws the full series (chosen lead, the day before, the day of) as three ticks with real dates; fix the sample to 'Warranty ends Sat 12 Dec · remind 30 days → Thu 12 Nov' [dates, notifications]
- Every-other-week pickup shows 'Next recycling' plus a read-only preview of the next 3 dates with holiday moves applied. Frequency is Not set / Weekly / Every other week [dates]
- Validity ranges are per kind, and out-of-range entries get a suggested fix ('That's already passed — remind 7 days before, Thu 24 Sep?'); move-in date may be in the past [dates]
- Tax appeal is seeded as a rule, 'Closes July 1, or 60 days after your value notice was mailed', with a 'When was your notice mailed?' input [dates]
- Voter registration is person-scoped. Its wording is 'Online or by mail — must arrive by Mon 26 Oct' and 'In person until 8:00 PM Tue 3 Nov'. There is an optional plan (Online / Mail / In person) that picks the reminder target, and an 'I did this' that reads 'You marked this done · Only you' [share, dates]
- Every date carries its own visibility control, 'Only you' / 'Household' [onboarding, household]
- Deleting uses a persistent inline 'Removed · Undo' row, not a timed toast [a11y, companion]
- At AX sizes the kind tiles become a one-column list and the sheet may scroll; 'Remind me' becomes radio rows [a11y]
- Reminder copy names the action: 'Tell your landlord in writing by Mon 1 Mar' [dates]
- Use HOME A fixtures only [prompting]
- (research contradiction, topic dates-reminders-pickup: entering dates, ) v1: '60 days must exist in Remind me: it is the only lead anyone ever picks for a lease notice.' Sample: 'Lease ends — 31 Mar 2027, remind 60 days' next to 'Notice to vacate — 31 Jan 2027'. | research: WA tenants owe at least 20 days' written notice, and leases may require more (RCW 59.18.200; WashingtonLawHelp). The action date is the notice deadline. 31 Mar 2027 minus 60 days is Sat 30 Jan 2027, one day before the Sun 31 Jan notice deadline, so the sample reminder arrives too late to act. We found no evidence that 60 days is the lead everyone picks. | do: Keep 60 as an option. Make Lease ends ask for the required notice (20/30/60/other), create the Notice deadline row from it, and anchor reminders to that deadline. Redraw the sample so the leader line lands well before 31 Jan 2027, and show the weekday ('Sun 31 Jan').
- (research contradiction, topic dates-reminders-pickup: entering dates, ) v1: 'Remind me' is a four-segment single choice (60/30/7/1) with one leader line to one labelled day. | research: The design doc (F5 Reminders) fires reminders at the lead, the day before and the day of. Multi-reminder series (7/3/1 days) had strong field effects (Fishbane et al. 2020, 13–21% fewer failures to appear), and calendars support more than one alert. | do: Draw all three ticks with their real dates, or add the caption 'We'll also remind you the day before and on the day.' The control must not understate what will fire.
- (research contradiction, topic dates-reminders-pickup: entering dates, ) v1: The leader-line example labels 'Mon 30 Nov 2026'. | research: Date arithmetic: no sample event minus 60, 30, 7 or 1 days gives Mon 30 Nov 2026. For the warranty ending Sat 12 Dec 2026, 30 days before is Thu 12 Nov and 7 days before is Sat 5 Dec. | do: Use a consistent example, e.g. 'Water heater warranty ends Sat 12 Dec 2026 · remind 30 days → Thu 12 Nov 2026'.
- (research contradiction, topic dates-reminders-pickup: entering dates, ) v1: Every kind is entered through a 'native date control', with no approximate-date path. | research: GOV.UK: allow approximate dates when users may not know the exact one. Warranty end and insurance renewal dates are often not remembered. | do: Add 'Not sure of the day' (month + year) with an 'about' treatment used on every surface, and a reminder at the start of that month.
- (research contradiction, topic dates-reminders-pickup: entering dates, ) v1: Android: 'ModalBottomSheet, M3 date picker' for all kinds, including dates about 2 years out. | research: Material docs: docked pickers suit near and distant dates, and modal date input takes keyboard entry. The m3.material.io snippet (seen via search; the page would not render) advises against the modal calendar picker for distant dates. NN/g: calendars suit dates within less than a year. | do: Specify the docked picker, or the date picker opened in INPUT_MODE_TEXT, for Lease, Notice, Insurance and Warranty. Keep the calendar mode for pickup's first date and dates within a few weeks. Draw the keyboard toggle.
- (research contradiction, topic dates-reminders-pickup: entering dates, ) v1: Validation frame: 'past date 1 Sep 2026' treated as an error in general. The same sheet also has a Move-in date kind. | research: A move-in date is normally in the past (f6-home-basics-rows saves dates more than 60 days ago). NN/g says to suggest corrections rather than reject input. | do: Make validity ranges per kind. Show the past-date error only for future-only kinds (Notice, Insurance, HOA, Lease), and pair it with a suggested fix. For a lead that has already passed, offer the nearest one still possible.
- (research contradiction, topic dates-reminders-pickup: entering dates, ) v1: Seeded: 'Clark County property-tax appeal — closes 1 Jul 2027'. | research: Clark County BOE: the deadline is 'July 1 of the assessment year or within 60 CALENDAR DAYS of when your Notice of Value was mailed', and late appeals are not accepted. | do: Seed it as a rule ('July 1, or 60 days after your value notice was mailed') with an input for the notice's mailing date. The single computed date appears only after that input.
- (research contradiction, topic dates-reminders-pickup: entering dates, ) v1: 'Online or by mail — by Mon 26 Oct 2026'. | research: RCW 29A.08.140: registrations must be received by an election official no later than eight days before the election. The date is right, but 'by mail — by' reads as a postmark date. | do: Reword to 'Online or by mail — must arrive by Mon 26 Oct 2026'.
- (research contradiction, topic accessibility-inclusive: WCAG 2.2 AA (in) v1: 'Ten glyph tiles, all visible without scrolling' in a two-column grid, above a native date control, a four-segment 'Remind me' control and the footer. | research: At AX5 (53pt) or Android 200%, ten labelled tiles plus a date control cannot all fit in one sheet viewport. Apple says to reduce columns and avoid truncation at large sizes. | do: Keep the one-tap tile grid at default sizes. At AX sizes, switch to a one-column list of kinds and allow scrolling. Let the four-segment 'Remind me' control wrap to a 2×2 grid or radio rows. Keep the reminder date printed in words ('Mon 30 Nov 2026') as well as the leader line.
- (research contradiction, topic accessibility-inclusive: WCAG 2.2 AA (in) v1: 'Delete immediately with an Undo toast; do not use a destructive confirm dialog.' | research: NN/g supports undo over confirmation. But WCAG 2.2.1 treats a timed-only way to undo as a time limit unless there is another way to recover. Apple HIG says auto-dismissing controls are hard for people who need more time. Material/Flutter action snackbars don't time out with TalkBack or VoiceOver on. | do: Keep delete-with-undo. Make Undo persist, either as an inline 'Removed · Undo' row on the calling surface or as a toast that stays until acted on while a screen reader runs. Announce the deletion as a status message.
- (research contradiction, topic accessibility-inclusive: WCAG 2.2 AA (in) v1: A native date control that must accept dates about 2 years out; on iOS it is described as the native inline date control. | research: NN/g date-input guidance: calendar pickers suit dates within about a year. For far-off dates, typing is faster, and scrolling pickers are problematic on mobile. NN/g's older-adult research records frustration when typed input isn't allowed. | do: Use a control that jumps straight to a month and year (iOS compact style with the month/year selector, M3 picker with keyboard input mode on Android, a typed date field on web), and accept typed dates in several formats. Keep the spelled-out date and weekday next to the field.
- (research contradiction, topic share-compare-civic: shareable cards, th) v1: "The SCOPE changes only the footer sentence and nothing else", so on a claimed home the voter-registration 'I did this' self-report sits under "Everyone in this household will see this." | research: Voter registration belongs to each individual. A household-visible tick would read as a fact about everyone at the address. Voting-record exposure drew strong backlash (the 2024 'Voting Report Card' mailers were called invasive, and Maryland's AG issued a cease-and-desist). The design doc bars claiming to know registration status. Nickerson & Rogers show that a self-made plan (how and when) is what moves behaviour. | do: Make voter registration a person-scoped kind. The seeded deadline can be household-visible, but the 'I did this' tick and any plan stay 'Only you' and read "You marked this done". Add an optional method picker (Online / Mail / In person) that selects which per-method deadline the reminder uses.
