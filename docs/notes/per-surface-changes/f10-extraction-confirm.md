# Research-backed changes for f10-extraction-confirm

- Before commit, every extracted field is HOLLOW ('read, not yet confirmed'); after 'Add to bills', the piece becomes filled-with-tick [capture]
- Amount and Due date always show a label caption ('Read beside "Amount due"') plus an inline crop of the source region above the editable value; with no coordinates, say 'We can't point to where we read this' [capture]
- Uncertain fields get a text flag, 'We weren't sure — check this'. When the model found alternatives, show them tappable: 'Oct 2 (Due date)' · 'Sep 12 (Statement date)', at most 3; no percentages and no AI banner [capture]
- Tapping a field opens the right control in place (decimal pad with the value selected, date picker); no reason picker [capture]
- The commit button restates the values: 'Add $142.18 due Fri 23 Oct to bills'; the success line restates them plus the reminder; Undo is available [capture]
- Failure frames show placeholders, never $0.00 or today's date; 'Needed to add a bill' appears only after pressing Add; 'Just file it' needs no fields [capture]
- Collapse Account ending and Kind into a secondary row; the piece counter counts pieces, not photos [capture]
- The photo pane shrinks to about 25% with the keyboard up, at AX sizes, or in landscape (beside the fields). The OCR outline is two-tone [a11y]
- Payee suggestions come only from committed bills [capture]
- (research contradiction, topic capture-and-extraction: photographing pa) v1: 'Confidence is expressed PER FIELD and only as the system's provenance marks: a hollow mark beside Due date and Account ending, a filled mark on the confident fields.' Payee, Amount and Kind are drawn with the filled mark before the user has confirmed anything. | research: In the shared encoding (x-provenance-sheet), FILLED means 'official or confirmed'. Before commit, no field is either. Model confidence is not correctness: Nutrient shows a wrong-column value at 97%, and Microsoft's own utility-bill example has amount due at 0.52 while the address is 0.96, so the money field is often the weak one. Reviewers accept wrong values readily (Bias in the Loop). Marking fields as settled lowers checking of exactly the values that cost money, and it breaks house honesty rule 1 (unverified must look unverified). | do: Before commit, every extracted field carries the hollow 'read, not yet confirmed' treatment (or a fourth 'read from your photo' variant defined once in x-provenance-sheet). Uncertain fields add a text flag, 'We weren't sure — check this', with alternatives where the model found them. Amount and Due date always get the source crop and the 'read beside "…"' caption whatever the model's confidence. On 'Add to bills' the whole piece becomes filled-with-tick.
- (research contradiction, topic accessibility-inclusive: WCAG 2.2 AA (in) v1: On a phone, the photo is a pinned pane of about 42% of the viewport that 'never scrolls away', and 'Do not collapse the photo behind a tap-to-expand.' Tapping a field outlines its OCR region with a 2px focus-border rectangle. | research: With the keyboard up (about 40% of height), at 200% or AX text, or in landscape (WCAG 1.3.4), a fixed 42% pane leaves almost no room, so the focused field is hidden (2.4.11) and the form fails to reflow (1.4.10; the image exception doesn't cover the form). A single-colour focus-blue outline (#0284c7) has no guaranteed 3:1 against arbitrary photo content. | do: Keep photo beside fields, but let the pane shrink automatically (for example to about 25%, showing the zoomed OCR region) when the keyboard is up, text is at AX sizes, or the phone is landscape. In landscape, move the pane to the side. Draw the OCR highlight as a two-tone (dark plus white) outline. Add an AX5 frame.
