# Research-backed changes for f1-today-air-band

- Use EPA ColorVision Assist hues with 2px surface gaps, a keyline in both modes, a two-tone halo marker, the band name in text.primary off the fill, and the top segment labelled '301+' [dataviz, a11y]
- Segments are equal width, with the marker placed within its segment; clamp values over 500 and print the number [dataviz]
- The health line is EPA's exact category text plus the pollutant-specific sensitive groups above 100. Pantopus context ('Smoke from the Gorge') goes on a separate line, labelled as ours [dataviz]
- Mark the person's own threshold (101/151/201) with 'Crossed 151 at 4:00 PM'. Draw a 'still unhealthy, no new push' frame [notifications]
- Push: 'Unhealthy air: AQI 176' / 'AirNow, 4:00 PM · Larkspur Loop'; placeholder 'Air quality alert'. Time Sensitive only at 151 or above; a worsening reading updates the existing alert [notifications, competitive]
- Every row shows 'today · observed 7:00 AM' and its age when stale; stale rows grey on their own [widgets, dataviz]
- No reading: a greyed bar with no marker, 'We couldn't reach AirNow just now · Retry' [competitive]
- Spoken label: 'Air quality index 118, Unhealthy for Sensitive Groups, category 3 of 6. AirNow, observed 4 PM'. Under Reduce Motion the landing jumps and fades rather than scrolling; add an AX5 frame [a11y]
- (research contradiction, topic notifications-permissions: push and perm) v1: Push tray card: 'Air is unhealthy for sensitive groups at 2418 NW Lacamas Dr'. The alert landing marks 'the 101 threshold' on the bar. | research: The title is about 59 characters, past Android's 30-character single-line limit, so the address or category is cut. Apple and App Review 4.5.4 say to keep home addresses off the lock screen. Separately, f4-notification-settings gives the person a threshold choice (101/151/201, with 151 selected), so a hard-coded 101 mark can contradict the rule that actually fired. Apple says urgency must be represented accurately. | do: Title 'Unhealthy air: AQI 118' (or the band name). Body 'AirNow, 4:00 PM · Lacamas Dr'. Placeholder 'Air quality alert'. The bar marks the person's selected threshold and its crossing time. Set the iOS level by severity (Time Sensitive only at 151 or above).
- (research contradiction, topic risk-and-place-dataviz: showing environm) v1: 'Hazardous 301+' but 'segment widths proportional to their ranges'. f8-scale-strips spells the ranges out as '(0–50, 51–100, 101–150, 151–200, 201–300, 301–500)'. | research: EPA: 'All AQI values above 300 are part of the Hazardous category. This includes values above 500.' A track capped at 500 cannot place such readings. Proportional widths also give Hazardous 40% and Very Unhealthy 20% of the track, leaving the everyday 0-100 range 20% (about 70px at 390 wide). | do: Use equal-width category segments with the marker placed within its segment, or proportional widths ending in a short open-ended '301+' stub. Either way, clamp values above the end with the number printed. Apply the same spec to f8-scale-strips, f6-place-section-details, f7-today-widget and x-provenance-sheet.
- (research contradiction, topic risk-and-place-dataviz: showing environm) v1: Default health message 'Air quality is satisfactory for everyone.' and Pantopus-written alert lines ('Everyone should limit time outdoors. Keep windows shut tonight.'). | research: AirNow's official Good text is 'Air quality is satisfactory, and air pollution poses little or no risk.' EPA lists 'inaccurate cautionary statements and health information' as a common reporting error. Above AQI 100, reports must name the pollutant-specific sensitive groups (TAD Table 4). | do: Use EPA's exact category description and pollutant-specific sensitive-group statement as the health line. Show Pantopus context ('Smoke from the Gorge', 'Keep windows shut tonight') as a separate, clearly Pantopus line under it.
- (research contradiction, topic risk-and-place-dataviz: showing environm) v1: 'Do not restyle the EPA ramp…' with the six EPA hues as the only exception to the token rule, drawn in light and dark, with no contrast or separation treatment specified. | research: EPA itself allows the ColorVision Assist palette. Computed from EPA's RGB values: adjacent standard hues contrast 1.57-2.37:1, yellow is 1.07:1 and green 1.74:1 on white, and maroon is 1.62:1 on the dark base #0F172A. All are below WCAG 1.4.11's 3:1. Apple HIG asks for visual separation between touching colour areas. | do: Clarify that switching to EPA's ColorVision Assist palette is allowed and does not count as restyling. Require 2px surface gaps between segments, a border.strong keyline in both modes, a two-tone marker ring that reaches 3:1 on every segment, and the band name always printed.
- (research contradiction, topic accessibility-inclusive: WCAG 2.2 AA (in) v1: The six-band bar uses 'the EPA colours users already recognise' (the standard palette) as the one sanctioned exception to the token rule. | research: EPA's May 2026 technical document says the standard colours 'can be difficult to discern', especially red and green, and permits the ColorVision Assist palette (Table 3). Computed: standard yellow 1.07:1 on white, green/yellow 1.62, orange/red 1.57. In dark mode, maroon is 1.62:1 on #0F172A. | do: Name the ColorVision Assist RGB values in the exception. Add at least 3:1 separators between segments and a keyline. Draw a two-tone halo marker. Keep the band name printed in text.primary beside the marker, never on the fill. Label the top band '301+'. Give the whole band a spoken label with index, category and 'n of 6', and add an AX5 frame.
- (research contradiction, topic Competitive and adjacent product teardow) v1: Push tray card: 'Air is unhealthy for sensitive groups at 2418 NW Lacamas Dr'. | research: Same HIG and App Store 4.5.4 guidance on personal information in notifications. HIG also says Time Sensitive is only for notifications relevant in the moment, and urgency must be represented accurately. | do: Use 'Air Unhealthy for Sensitive Groups · Lacamas Dr' (or the saved label), with the AQI value and the source in the body.
