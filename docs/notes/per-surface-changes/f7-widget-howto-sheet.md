# Research-backed changes for f7-widget-howto-sheet

- Android: when isRequestPinAppWidgetSupported() is true, the primary button is 'Add to home screen' (system pin dialog). Fallback steps use the app-icon route: touch and hold Pantopus → Widgets → drag a size. Add 'pin dialog shown' and 'launcher unsupported' frames. Update the job line [widgets, competitive]
- iOS step 2: 'Tap Edit, then Add Widget, and search for Pantopus'. Illustrate it without depending on button shapes; no 'Add it for me' [widgets]
- The preview is a non-interactive illustration captioned 'Your widget'; this widget look is never reused elsewhere in the app [widgets]
- Triggers: after 'That's my day', or after push is declined, at most once per 24h, never as a blocking modal [widgets]
- At AX sizes the sheet may reach the large detent and scroll, with Done pinned and scroll padding; step illustrations have text labels; Reduce Motion gives static steps [a11y]
- (research contradiction, topic glanceable-today-widgets: home-screen wi) v1: 'No public API can place a widget for the user'. DO NOT add an 'Add it for me', 'Install widget' or 'Take me there' button. Android gets the same three gesture steps. | research: On Android 8.0 (API 26) and higher, AppWidgetManager.requestPinAppWidget() asks the launcher to show a system pin dialog (with a preview in Glance), after checking isRequestPinAppWidgetSupported(). Android guidance recommends offering it after a related task. The statement is true only for iOS. | do: Android: the main footer action is 'Add to home screen', which calls the pin API when it is supported. The gesture steps show only when pinning is unsupported. Keep the no-button rule for iOS only.
- (research contradiction, topic glanceable-today-widgets: home-screen wi) v1: iOS step 2: 'Tap the plus in the corner, then look for Pantopus.' | research: Apple Support's current steps: 'touch and hold a widget or an empty area until the apps jiggle. Tap the Edit button in the upper-left corner. Tap Add Widget.' The '+' wording is out of date, which is the exact failure the prompt's own rationale warns about. | do: Step 2 becomes 'Tap Edit, then Add Widget, and search for Pantopus.' Illustrate it without depending on the button's shape, and check the wording against Apple Support at each iOS release.
- (research contradiction, topic glanceable-today-widgets: home-screen wi) v1: Android steps: 'Touch and hold an empty spot… Tap Widgets, then scroll to Pantopus… Touch and hold the size you want and drag it into place.' | research: Pixel Help documents a shorter app-specific route: 'touch and hold the app' then 'tap Widgets', which opens straight to that app's widgets without scrolling the whole picker. | do: In the fallback steps, teach the app-icon route: touch and hold the Pantopus icon, tap Widgets, then drag a size into place. It has fewer steps and needs no scrolling through other apps.
- (research contradiction, topic glanceable-today-widgets: home-screen wi) v1: The top of the sheet is the user's own widget rendered at true size from the real snapshot, so it reads 'as a thing already on a home screen'. | research: HIG: 'Avoid mirroring your widget's appearance within your app. Including an element in your app that looks like your widget but doesn't behave like it can confuse people.' Against that, Duolingo found that in-app animated install guides drove installs. | do: Keep the preview, but make it clearly an illustration: non-interactive, on the home-screen ground patch, with a small caption such as 'Your widget'. Never reuse this widget-look card anywhere else in the app, such as on Today.
- (research contradiction, topic accessibility-inclusive: WCAG 2.2 AA (in) v1: 'Do not let the sheet scroll or grow past the medium detent.' The true-size preview takes about the top 45% and the three steps fit beneath it with no scrolling. | research: Apple's Larger Text criteria: support 200% or more and avoid truncation. HIG Typography: stack and let text use as many lines as needed. Three steps of AX5 text (53pt body) cannot fit under a 170pt preview in about 430pt. | do: Keep 'no scroll' at default sizes only. At accessibility sizes, allow the sheet to reach the large detent and scroll, keep the steps above the fold before the preview if needed, keep Done pinned with scroll padding, and give the step illustrations text labels.
- (research contradiction, topic Competitive and adjacent product teardow) v1: 'No public API can place a widget for the user.' Do not add 'Add it for me', 'Install widget' or 'Take me there', because 'no API can do it'. This applies to both iOS and Android. | research: Android documents in-app widget pinning from Android 8.0 (API 26). The app can ask the launcher to show a pin dialog; the success callback fires only if the launcher supports pinning and the user accepts. The fetched doc is the Glance page (requestPinGlanceAppWidget). The framework method for the RemoteViews widget, AppWidgetManager.requestPinAppWidget, was not fetched and needs checking. No equivalent iOS API was found. | do: On Android, when the launcher supports pinning, show a primary 'Add to home screen' under the live preview that opens the system pin dialog. Keep the three gesture steps as the fallback for unsupported launchers and for iOS. Add frames for 'pin dialog shown' and 'launcher doesn't support pinning'. Update the surface-index job line too.
