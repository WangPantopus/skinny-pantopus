# Research-backed changes for f1-today-tab

- Replace '3 of 6 things on file →' with one 'Next: set your pickup day →' row (no fraction), shown for 7 days or until the set is done or dismissed [onboarding, companion]
- Rename the empty-state CTA to 'Add a place' when it opens the save sheet [onboarding]
- Warming uses content-shaped skeletons in the final slots only for cold loads over 1s. Each section fills as its provider returns [onboarding, widgets]
- The quiet day is a heading plus 4 per-provider checks (ticked / 'Couldn't reach AirNow · Retry' / pending). The heading shows only when all 4 are ticked [widgets, notifications]
- Keep the fixed section order, but the pinned slot shows any active threshold crossing (AQI at or above the threshold, NOAA) for every arrival type, with a link down to the section [widgets]
- Read a `section=` parameter: scroll to that section and highlight it once, moving accessibility focus there too [widgets, a11y]
- The header freshness line reads 'Updating… / Updated just now / 12m ago'; a failure keeps content and shows 'Couldn't refresh · Retry' [widgets]
- When a push arrives in the foreground, no banner: highlight the pickup card [notifications]
- No permission banner for people who declined; at most one neutral text.secondary line, once [notifications]
- The tab bar uses Large Content Viewer; add AX5 and greyscale frames; batch 15 frames into 3 turns; HOME A and PLACE B on TODAY [a11y, prompting]
- (research contradiction, topic onboarding-activation: time-to-value bef) v1: The dense Today frame (a T1 'Saved place · Only you') ends with 'Progress row: "3 of 6 things on file →"'. x-place-file defines this as the Today entry for the first 14 days, while forbidding anything that reads as 'you are 60% complete'. | research: A fraction is a completion meter, and '3 of 6' reads as 50% complete. The design doc's F2 defines a three-question first week and says the banner must never show a step the person cannot complete at their tier. At T1, household requires a claim and verify requires a home. Villar et al.: stalled or slow indicators increase drop-off. Nunes & Drèze: a bounded set that has already started increases completion. Chameleon: 3–4 steps complete best. | do: Either drop the fraction ('Next: set your pickup day →', naming the one next ask), or use a bounded first-week set that follows the activation definition (Save ✓ · Pickup day · Reminder or widget · One date or person), with a denominator of 4 or fewer that counts only tier-reachable steps. Exclude verify, profile and photo. Make x-place-file and f1-today-tab use the same wording.
- (research contradiction, topic glanceable-today-widgets: home-screen wi) v1: Fixed order location → pinned briefing (push arrivals only) → pickup → 14-day strip → weather → air → alerts → signals, where surviving sections never move. | research: NN/g's inverted pyramid puts the most important fact first, and the HIG deep-link rule applies too. The widget's own urgency ladder ranks AQI ≥ 101 above any date, yet on Today an AQI or NOAA crossing sits sixth or seventh for any arrival that is not a push. | do: Keep the fixed order, since a stable layout is a sound goal. Make the pinned slot show any active threshold crossing (AQI ≥ 101, a NOAA alert) for every arrival type (widget, organic, push), with a link down to the full section.
- (research contradiction, topic companion-progress-ethics: companion cha) v1: The Today content list includes 'Progress row: "3 of 6 things on file →"'. | research: Same evidence as for the place file: X-of-Y framing is how endowed progress and goal-gradient effects create completion pressure (Nunes & Drèze 2006; Kivetz et al. 2006). It also contradicts the house rule, repeated across prompts, that nothing may read as a percentage complete. | do: Replace it with a denominator-free invitation row that points to one specific missing fact, and hide it once the first-week facts exist or the user dismisses it.
- (research contradiction, topic claude-design-prompting: how to prompt C) v1: One prompt asks for 15 frames (12 iOS states plus 3 platform frames), and the preamble implies light and dark, up to 30 artboards in one generation. The same pattern appears in f3b-invitation-decision (19), f7-today-widget (19), f3-invite-banner (16), f3b-verify-address-sheet (15) and f3b-owner-attestation (15). | research: Figma: 'The smaller the scope, the more detailed the LLM can be.' Stitch: one or two adjustments per prompt, and long prompts omit components. IFScale: adherence falls as instruction density rises, with a bias toward earlier instructions. Design2Code: models miss visual elements. Anthropic also says to give the complete spec up front. | do: Keep the full brief in one message but deliver in batches of at most about 6 artboards (the dense default and layout-changing states first, then the other states, then platforms, then dark), checking each batch against the manifest.
