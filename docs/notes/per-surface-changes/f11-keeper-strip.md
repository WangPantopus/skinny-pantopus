# Research-backed changes for f11-keeper-strip

- The top two states are helpful, not distressed ('busy' holding a stack; 'needs you' holding up the envelope). No tears, drooping, illness or darker scenery. Rename the internal states so illustrators don't draw anxiety [companion]
- Mood comes only from due and overdue obligations in a rolling 'next 7 days', never from app opens or time away; the thing is the grammatical subject ('The water bill is past its due date') [companion]
- The mood line is the tap target leading to the item, which offers a correction ('Already paid?'); the accessible label is the fact line; the pose is not colour-coded [companion, a11y]
- Replace the ring with '11 on file' plus known-category chips; no outlined empties; no count animation or '+1' toasts [companion, a11y]
- No badge, dot or 'check in'; no push; never shown next to a safety alert; shared keepers compute mood only from data the viewer is permitted to see [companion]
- After Skip the slot closes entirely; remove the 'Unnamed' invitation frame after Skip; offer 'Hide keeper for now' with a named end date next to Remove [companion]
- Idle animation of 5s or less, then still; Reduce Motion gives a static pose; right side hit area at least 44pt; the silhouette in text.secondary [a11y, companion]
- Use HOME A fixtures (resolves the 2416 NE Ingle Rd drift) [prompting]
- (research contradiction, topic companion-progress-ethics: companion cha) v1: The fact count is a five-segment ring. Unknown categories are 'drawn as a border.strong hairline outline'. The T1 frame shows 'money/people/proof as outlined segments', and the prompt claims this 'is deliberately NOT a progress bar' and makes a saved place read as 'a smaller instrument rather than a failing one'. | research: A closed shape with filled and outlined segments carries a visible denominator (4 of 5), which people read as the proportion of the goal remaining (Kivetz et al. 2006; Nunes & Drèze 2006). UbiFit showed absence as a neutral sparse garden, never as empty slots (Consolvo 2008). For T1, three of the outlined segments cannot be filled at that tier. Apple's HIG also warns that ring-like elements get confused with Activity rings, which iOS users associate with closing the ring. That last point is an inference for non-fitness data. | do: Do not outline unknown categories. Leave a plain gap, or switch to an open form such as a row of labelled category pips that appear only when known. For T1, draw only the categories a saved place can hold. Before committing, test the ring with 5 pilot users by asking 'what does this tell you?' and check whether anyone says 'almost done'.
- (research contradiction, topic companion-progress-ethics: companion cha) v1: Each species is drawn in four moods, 'relaxed, attentive, fidgeting, worried', and 'Worried — "A bill is overdue."' is a distinct distressed-sounding pose. | research: In Fish'n'Steps, some participants ignored the display when their fish was unhappy, and the reported reading is that punishment led to avoidance (as reported in Consolvo et al. 2008). UbiFit deliberately used only positive reinforcement. Duolingo's sad-owl messaging is the leading example of guilt-based engagement. | do: Keep four states, but define the top two as helpful urgency, not distress: 'busy', with the keeper holding a stack, and 'needs you', with the keeper holding up the envelope or pointing at the line. Never draw tears, cowering or illness. Rename the internal states so illustrators don't draw anxiety, and add an 'Already paid?' correction on the overdue line.
- (research contradiction, topic accessibility-inclusive: WCAG 2.2 AA (in) v1: The fact-count ring has 36pt with 3px stroke. Known segments are primary.600, and unknown segments are a 'border.strong hairline outline'. Segment order is fixed clockwise, with no labels. The unnamed state uses a silhouette in text.muted, and the ring is the tap target. | research: border.strong on white is 1.47:1, so 'nothing known' segments are invisible to low-vision users (WCAG 1.4.11). Which categories are known exists nowhere else as text. A text.muted silhouette is 2.54:1. A 36pt target is below 44pt/48dp. | do: Draw empty segments as outlines of at least 3:1. Give the ring a spoken label listing the known and missing categories. Make the whole right side at least 44pt/48dp. Use text.secondary for the unnamed silhouette. Keep mood-by-pose, which is good for colour-blind users, since the mood line is already text.
