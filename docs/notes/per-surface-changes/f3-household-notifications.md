# Research-backed changes for f3-household-notifications

- Canonical titles of 30 characters or fewer: 'Unconfirmed: pickup tomorrow', 'Sam marked Clark PUD paid', 'Dana added Chimney sweep'. Replace the calendar pickup reminder example with a real date reminder ('Lease notice due in 14 days'). Pickup arrives only through the evening briefing [notifications, competitive]
- Household activity is Passive / LOW: one thread per home, same-window events collapse into one notification that updates in place ('Sam paid 3 bills'). Draw it on iOS and Android. bill_paid is Active only for members who still see the bill as unpaid and due soon; task_completed goes only to the creator or assignee [notifications, household]
- Push bodies carry no amounts: 'Due Fri 23 Oct · Larkspur Loop'; placeholder 'Household update'; circular initials avatar [notifications, household]
- Resolving an item cancels and replaces its delivered reminders [household]
- Add quiet rows: 'Tova joined Larkspur Loop — can see the calendar and bills', 'Jenna's guest access ended' [household]
- No 'still hasn't paid' wording; no keeper voice [household, companion]
- The unread dot has the accessible label 'Unread'; each notification opens the exact object [a11y, household]
- (research contradiction, topic household-sharing: shared household spac) v1: For push, 'the caveat rides the TITLE', drawn as 'Garbage + recycling is tomorrow (not yet confirmed)' (51 characters) with the caveat at the end. Household row titles such as 'Dana added Chimney sweep to the calendar' (40 characters) are also over 30. | research: Android's notification design guide limits the content title to 30 characters and the content text to 40. A 51-character title with the caveat last is truncated in exactly the place v1 is trying to protect, just as the body tail would be. Apple likewise asks for brief titles readable at a glance. | do: Keep the caveat in the title, but put it first and keep the title at 30 characters or fewer: 'Unconfirmed: pickup tomorrow' (28) with body 'Wed Sep 17 - Camas city schedule' (under 40). Household titles: 'Sam marked Clark PUD paid' (25), 'Dana added Chimney sweep' (24), 'Priya finished HVAC filter' (26). Add a 30/40 character budget to the prompt.
- (research contradiction, topic household-sharing: shared household spac) v1: Each household event is its own notification (in-app row plus push when enabled, per design doc F3). Grouping is specified only as a same-day, same-actor in-app row ('Sam paid 3 bills'). The OS push tray is drawn only for reminders, so no push grouping, coalescing or interruption level is decided for household activity. | research: Zeng and Roesner's field study found co-resident activity notifications were often overwhelming or ignored. The most valued uses were security and confirming automations, and a single event produced several notifications. NN/g says to merge bursts, Apple says not to send several notifications for the same thing and defines Passive for 'view at their leisure', and Android auto-groups four or more and prefers updating an existing notification. | do: In the prompt, set the Household activity channel to deliver quietly by default (iOS Passive, Android Medium importance, no sound), with the in-app row always present. Merge events for the same home within a window into one push that updates in place, and draw that grouped push on iOS and Android. Reserve Active delivery for bill_paid to members who still see that bill as unpaid and due soon, and send task_completed only to the task's creator or assignee.
- (research contradiction, topic household-sharing: shared household spac) v1: Push and row content carries the amount and the street-derived home label: bill reminder push body '$84.00 - Maple St'; bill_paid caption '$84 - due Sep 20 - Maple St' (design doc push body '${amount} - due {date}'). | research: Apple HIG: 'Avoid including sensitive, personal, or confidential information in a notification... private information that could be visible to others.' Amounts owed plus a street name on a lock screen are the kind of detail this warns against. | do: Keep amounts in the in-app row, but default push bodies to 'Due Sunday - Maple St' without the amount. Where the user has named the home (e.g. 'Home'), use that name instead of the street.
- (research contradiction, topic notifications-permissions: push and perm) v1: OS tray reminder title: 'Garbage + recycling is tomorrow (not yet confirmed)'. Same-day grouping is drawn only for the in-app list, and each activity event pushes as it happens. | research: Android cuts titles past about 30 characters on one line, so the bracketed caveat is lost. Apple: Passive is for information to view at leisure. NN/g and Android say to bundle bursts, and to group only complete children or update in place. Fitz et al.: predictable batching improves attention and control. | do: Use the canonical 'Unconfirmed: pickup tomorrow' title. Household activity is Passive / LOW, with one thread or group per home, and a same-day roll-up in the tray too ('Sam paid 3 bills'). Consider delivering activity inside the evening briefing instead of immediately.
- (research contradiction, topic notifications-permissions: push and perm) v1: The settings list shows the channels 'Household activity', 'Security', 'Reminders' and 'Briefing'. | research: Android channels are permanent once shipped: importance cannot change, and Settings shows users a count of deleted channels. The in-app page and the channels must therefore share one stable set of categories. f4-notification-settings defines a different set of four (Briefings, Household activity, Dates and bills, Air and weather alerts) and has no Security group. | do: Settle on one five-group set in both prompts before the Android build ships: Briefings; Air & weather alerts; Dates & bills; Household activity; Account & security.
- (research contradiction, topic Competitive and adjacent product teardow) v1: It draws a separate calendar_date reminder push, 'Garbage + recycling is tomorrow (not yet confirmed)', as one of five notification types, while F4 already delivers the same pickup through the evening briefing. The caveat is a parenthetical at the end of the title. | research: Apple HIG: 'Avoid sending multiple notifications for the same thing'; repeats lead people to turn off all notifications. Ring reviewers complain about two notifications per alert. The design doc's F5 reminder pass covers only the new date kinds (lease, insurance, warranty, HOA, tax appeal), not pickup. | do: Replace the pickup example with a real F5 date reminder, such as 'Lease Notice Deadline in 30 Days'. State that pickup arrives only through the evening briefing. Move any caveat to the front of a short title. Also require grouping at the push level, not only in the in-app list.
