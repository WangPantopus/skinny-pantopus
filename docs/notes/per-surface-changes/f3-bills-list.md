# Research-backed changes for f3-bills-list

- Keep 'Mark paid' in the overflow, with the Undo toast held open while a screen reader is on, and delay the notification [dates, household]
- Header states scope and window: 'Everyone at Larkspur Loop can see these bills · 3 upcoming · $306.17 through Mon 2 Nov' [household, dates]
- The member caption names who can mark bills paid [household]
- A row highlighted from a notification also receives accessibility focus; overdue shows the word, not colour alone [a11y]
- Offline/cached states say what is stale [household]
- (research contradiction, topic dates-reminders-pickup: entering dates, ) v1: Mark paid only in the row overflow menu. No undo is specified. | research: Bill apps reduce mark-paid friction (Chronicle marks from the widget and confirms later, and reviewers complain about multi-step saves). The prompt's reason, that marking notifies the household, is valid. An undo removes the harm of a mis-tap without adding steps. | do: Keep the overflow placement. Add an Undo toast and hold the bill_paid household notification until the undo window closes. On a repeating bill, show 'Next due <date>' after marking.
- (research contradiction, topic household-sharing: shared household spac) v1: Member read-only: 'no Mark paid on the row at all'. The member holds finance.view only, yet the screen's stated job is 'so two people do not pay the same bill'. f3-bill-detail-web likewise draws 'Mark paid' as absent for members. | research: Geeng and Roesner found the person who sets things up ends up holding most of the control, and recommend designing for other household members. Song et al. (CHI 2021) found shared-account conflict comes from members seeing ownership differently. Zeng and Roesner warn that restrictive setups widen the gap between the setup person and everyone else. If a member who actually paid cannot record it, the double-payment problem this screen exists to prevent remains. The papers do not test bills directly; this conflict is between v1's own stated job and the permission it draws. | do: Give members an attributed, undoable 'I paid this' action (in the overflow and on bill detail), even if adding, editing and removing bills stays manager-only. If the product decides against that, the member caption must name who can mark it paid ('Dana or Marcus can mark this paid'), not just say why the member can't.
