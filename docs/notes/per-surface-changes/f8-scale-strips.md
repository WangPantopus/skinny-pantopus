# Research-backed changes for f8-scale-strips

- The Air row is the one coloured track (ColorVision Assist, gaps, keyline, halo marker, '301+'), identical to f1-today-air-band. Flood, wildfire and radon stay neutral [dataviz]
- Flood uses three bands: 'Minimal (X)' · '0.2% a year (shaded X)' · '1% a year or more (A/V zones)', with the real zone code in the label. Add 'FEMA hasn't mapped flood hazard here' [dataviz]
- Approved strings only: 'Zone X — minimal flood hazard'; 'Wildfire hazard potential: Moderate (3 of 5)'; 'Radon Zone 1 — highest potential (county)'. Row names: Flood zone / Wildfire hazard / Air today / Radon zone [dataviz]
- Official datasets are FILLED with a scope word in the caption; no hollow radon [dataviz, trust]
- Band dividers and keyline in ink at least 3:1. The occupied band is shown by the marker plus the printed name plus an outline, not a raised fill [a11y]
- Captions follow 'authority · scope · as-of'. At narrow widths merge the sources, but air keeps 'today · 7:00 AM' [dataviz]
- Each row is one accessible element with value, position, endpoints, authority, date and provenance; the row is the tap target at 48pt; AX5 frame with text above the track [a11y]

- (research contradiction, topic risk-and-place-dataviz: showing environm) v1: Flood value 'Zone X — minimal risk' (the arrival header and compare sheet use the same wording; the OG card shortens it to 'Zone X — minimal'; f6 says 'minimal hazard'). | research: FEMA defines unshaded Zone X as an area of 'minimal flood hazard'. Kelley's label research depends on standard terms. | do: Use one approved string on every surface: 'Zone X — minimal flood hazard' with caption 'FEMA · area zone · effective <date>'. - (research contradiction, topic risk-and-place-dataviz: showing environm) v1: Flood track with '4 segments — X · X shaded · AE · VE'. | research: FEMA's high-risk zones (A, AO, AH, A1-30, AE, A99, AR, V, VE, V1-30) are all defined by the same 1% annual chance. Shaded X lies between the 1% and 0.2% floods; unshaded X is outside the 0.2% flood. So AE and VE are not successive steps of one ordered scale; V zones add coastal wave hazard (standard FEMA meaning, not in the glossary text I retrieved). An address in Zone A, AO or AH has no segment, and an unmapped or undetermined area has no state. | do: Use three bands by annual chance: 'Minimal (X)', '0.2% a year (shaded X)', '1% a year or more (A/V zones)'. Print the actual zone code in the marker label, add a distinct 'FEMA hasn't mapped flood hazard here' state, and mention V-zone wave hazard in words, not as a fourth band.
- (research contradiction, topic risk-and-place-dataviz: showing environm) v1: Radon is drawn with the 'hollow mark, caption "On record, not yet confirmed"', and state 3 draws radon and wildfire hollow. f6-place-section-details and f8-og-compare-card repeat the hollow radon mark. | research: The EPA radon zone and USFS WHP are official published designations. Their limit is scope, not confirmation: EPA says the map 'should not be used to determine if a home … should be tested' and that high-radon homes exist in all three zones, and WRC says only home assessments show a home's susceptibility. The uncertainty framework in van der Bles 2019 treats these as different kinds of uncertainty. x-provenance-sheet defines hollow as 'on record but unconfirmed', which suggests the zone itself might be wrong. | do: Draw official datasets FILLED, with a scope word in the caption ('EPA · county-wide estimate — only a test tells you about this home'). Keep HOLLOW for facts a household can confirm (pickup, seeded civic dates). If radon must stay hollow, change its caption to 'County estimate · not a test of this home' instead of 'not yet confirmed'.
