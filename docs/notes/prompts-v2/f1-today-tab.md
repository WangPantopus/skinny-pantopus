WHERE IT LIVES & HOW PEOPLE ARRIVE: the Today tab, second of Place · Today · Nearby · Mail. Entry points:
- The tab itself: bottom bar (phones, web 390) or left sidebar (web 1440). Web URL /app/today. /app/place/today (including ?rule=…) redirects here with its query intact.
- A briefing push: pantopus://hub-today?deliveryId=…&kind=morning|evening. The evening briefing (6:00 PM) carries pickup and next-day items; the morning briefing (7:00 AM) carries date items. Either one opens this tab, hands over the delivered items, fills the pinned slot by rule (b) below, and highlights the matching section.
- An air or NOAA alert push: pantopus://today?section=air&src=alert. It fills the pinned slot for Today's place. An alert for a different saved place lands on the air band screen (f1-today-air-band) for that place, not on this tab.
- A widget tap: pantopus://today?src=widget&section=pickup|strip|air. Content already on screen stays; Today scrolls to that section and highlights it once.
- A date reminder push: pantopus://today?rule=… or /app/today?rule=…. Today scrolls to that row in the 14-day card (f5 draws the row), then the DateSheet opens in view mode. If the date is outside the 14 days, the link opens the place file's Dates row instead (x-place-file); Today is not the landing. A removed date shows "That date was removed." as a status line under the 14-day card's summary.
- "See Today" on the save confirmation. Today arrives warming, and the location row already shows the new saved place.
- The first open after claiming an address or joining a household shows a one-time notice.
- Removing the last saved place returns here in the no-place state. A briefing push tapped after its place was removed also lands in the no-place state.
Out of Today: the location-row chip opens Your places. FirstWeekRow scrolls to and highlights the control that holds its step. Strip rows and "+ Add a date" open the DateSheet. "Add a place" opens the add-place sheet. The one-time claim notice opens the claim receipt.

LAYOUT & VISUALIZATION: Draw this vertical order, the same at every tier and on every platform:
1 location row · 2 pinned slot · 2b FirstWeekRow (first 7 days only; zero height when complete or hidden) · 3 PickupCard (on a quiet day, QuietDayReceipt takes this slot) · 4 14-day card · 4b keeper slot (phase 2; zero height in this project, and hidden whenever an alert is pinned) · 5 weather · 6 AqiBand · 7 alerts · 8 ranked signals · 9 "Good day to…" tiles · 10 briefing opt-in card.
Each section title is an accessibility heading. A section appears or vanishes with its data, and the sections that stay never move. Claiming an address must read as "the chip changed, and any household sections appeared". Whenever a place exists, the 14-day card never disappears.
The pinned slot holds at most one card, in this precedence for every arrival type:
(a) An active air or NOAA crossing at Today's place: the AqiBand widget-micro variant (name and number beside the track, no text on fills) with "Air quality index (AQI) 118 · Unhealthy for Sensitive Groups", "Crossed 101 at 4:00 PM · AirNow" and "See air quality ↓". A NOAA crossing uses the same form: "Wind advisory until 10:00 PM Tue" · "National Weather Service · Portland forecast office" · "See alerts ↓".
(b) On a briefing-push arrival, list only the delivered items that are NOT in the first viewport, headed "From your 6:00 PM briefing · Also:" (morning: "From your 7:00 AM briefing · Also:") with one "↓" line each, each scrolling to and highlighting its row. Highlight any delivered item that is already in the first viewport in place; never repeat it in the slot. If every item is in the first viewport, the slot takes no space.
(c) The one-time claim or join notice, drawn with LandingBannerSlot geometry.
(d) Otherwise it takes no space.
Quiet day: draw a finished screen, not an empty state. Show the heading only when all four CheckItems are ticked.
Warming: for cold loads over 1s only, draw WarmingSkeleton section shapes at final height in slots 3–6. Each fills in as its provider returns. While warming, the FreshnessLine under the address reads "Setting up Today for Birchfield Ct"; it becomes "Updated just now" once every provider has returned or timed out. After 10s, a provider that still hasn't returned becomes its InlineErrorRow ("We couldn't reach AirNow just now · Retry"); the others stay as they landed. FirstWeekRow shows during warming. Warm refresh: never skeleton or dim anything. The FreshnessLine reads "Updating…" then "Updated just now", with the hairline only if the refresh takes 1s or longer.
Hidden details (the address resolves but weather, air, alerts, signals and tiles are withheld): show the hidden-details line directly above slot 3, full width, in bodySmall text.strong on surface.sunken; the PickupCard (when pickup is tomorrow) and the 14-day card follow in their usual slots.
When data is missing: a failed provider gets an InlineErrorRow in its own slot. A refresh failure keeps the content. With no cache, show the error state. Offline, show cached content with OfflineNotice, and each disabled control carries its reason caption.
Signals never repeat a section's fact. Pickup appears once, as the PickupCard with its KindGlyph, never with a sparkle icon.

INTERACTION, MOTION & HAPTICS: On an arrival from a push, widget or reminder, scroll once to the target and highlight it with the DateRow highlighted wash (fades in under 300ms, then decays); accessibility focus moves there. If a push arrives while Today is open, show no banner; highlight the PickupCard, and the notification bell badge increments; nothing else changes.
FirstWeekRow: the step order is Save ✓ → Pickup day → Reminder or widget → One date or person. The row names only the next unfinished step, with no fraction. It is a whole-row link that scrolls to and highlights the control for that step: (1) pickup day: the PickupCard when it is on screen; otherwise the first "Pickup day" row's "Set your pickup day" button in the 14-day card (f5 draws it); (2) reminder: "Next: get a reminder the night before →" scrolls to and highlights the briefing opt-in card in slot 10, whose widget line covers the widget half; (3) date or person: "Next: add one date that matters →" highlights the 14-day card's "+ Add a date" row; at a claimed home the person half, "Next: add someone to your household →", opens the household block in the place file. At a saved place the person half is not offered and not counted; only steps reachable at the current tier count. The row shows for 7 days from the save and disappears when the reachable set is done or when hidden. Its separate 44pt text button "Hide this" collapses the row to InlineUndo "Hidden · Undo", stored on the server, kept until the person leaves Today; later sections move up once. A member who joined a household never sees FirstWeekRow.
Pull to refresh on native; its alternatives are Retry in the FreshnessLine and a "Refresh" accessibility action, and web refreshes on focus. Sections fill in with a 200ms cross-fade. Under Reduce Motion: jump-scroll without animation, a static highlight that clears on the next interaction, no shimmer. Today fires no haptics of its own (the PickupCard confirm has its own).

INSTEAD OF:
- Instead of a full-page spinner while providers load, draw section skeletons in their final slots — because nothing should jump and an all-clear must not show early.
- Instead of an illustrated "nothing here yet" empty state on a quiet day, draw the heading plus four ticked checks with times — because a receipt is what makes silence believable.
- Instead of "3 of 6 things on file →" at the bottom of the page, draw "Next: set your pickup day →" directly under the pinned slot — because a fraction reads as percent complete, and a new person needs the next step in view.
- Instead of moving air or alerts to the top on a bad-air day, fill the pinned slot and link down — because a stable order is learnable and the crossing still sits above the fold.
- Instead of a pinned briefing line that repeats the PickupCard right under it, highlight the card in place and pin only the items below the fold — because the same fact twice reads as two things.
- Instead of dropping the chip on a claimed home, show "Your household" — because scope is always stated.
- Instead of "Today starts with a place" for anyone who has a location, show the hidden-details line — because it would tell a resident they have no place.
- Instead of silently switching Today after an invite is accepted, draw the one-time join notice — because an unexplained address change reads as a bug.
