WHAT THIS BOARD IS FOR
The board holds the reading instruments: scales, strips, bands, meters, maps and counts. They share one grammar:
(1) Each instrument states a fact in words and numbers. The graphic repeats the fact and never replaces it.
(2) Nothing adds up to a grade, score, percentage or verdict.
(3) Nothing depends on colour alone. The AQI hues are the one sanctioned colour scale, and even there the category is printed in words.
(4) Three shapes mean provenance (00a · ProvenanceMark) and nothing else, including inside these instruments: a filled disc, a ring, and a disc with a knocked-out tick. That is why position markers are never discs or rings; they are vertical capsules or triangle pointers. There is one exception: the bare stroked check in a QuietDayReceipt line. It has no disc, it is always followed by the word "Checked", and it never appears at a mark position. This exception is listed on Notes as an open question for 00d.
(5) A mark or cell is never a tap target by itself. The target is the row, the whole strip, or a labelled button.
(6) Each instrument has a one-sentence summary and a spoken label, and the same facts appear nearby as a list.
(7) Every provenance mark keeps at least 3:1 contrast in every state. Staleness is stated in words and never shown by dimming a mark.

BOARD CONVENTIONS (these apply to every artboard on this board)

C0 · Artboard naming. This board overrides the house artboard pattern. Name every artboard "00b · <Component> · <NN-name> · <light|dark>", exactly as written in the ARTBOARDS manifest. The last artboard is "00b · Notes".
C1 · Canvas. Each artboard is 1440 wide, with whatever height it needs. Use surface.app for light artboards and dark surface.app #020617 for dark ones. Print the artboard name above the artboard in label 13/18 text.strong, exactly as written in the ARTBOARDS manifest.
C2 · Specimen frames. Draw specimens at true size (1pt = 1px on the artboard) inside plain frames: iOS 393 wide, Android 412 wide, web-390 and web-1440, as named.
- Phone frames (iOS 393, Android 412 and web-390): draw the specimen card 358 wide with 16pt padding, which leaves 326pt of content width, and centre the card in the frame. The 16pt side-margin rule describes web-390 only; in the wider frames the leftover space is split evenly. All phone geometry below assumes 326pt content width unless it says otherwise. Widgets use their own sizes.
- Web-1440 card widths: ScaleStrip 640px (608px content); FourteenDayStrip 648px (616px content, so each cell is 44px); YearBand 880px (848px content). The AqiBand instrument row takes the ScaleStrip right column. FactCount keeps its 160pt maximum width.
- Cards use surface.base, radius lg 12, shadow sm and padding 16. In dark mode, cards use dark surface.raised #1E293B with a 1px dark border.strong #374151 edge, because border.default has no contrast on raised.
C3 · Callouts. Draw callouts in an annotation gutter outside the UI, in caption 12/16 text.strong. Leader lines are 1px text.secondary and end in a short perpendicular tick, not a dot, so an annotation is never mistaken for a provenance mark. Each callout names the part, its size in pt/dp/px and its token name, for example "Track · 12pt · fill surface.raised · keyline 1px text.secondary". Callouts appear only on 01-anatomy artboards and where an artboard below asks for them.
C4 · Grids. A variant × state grid has a header row for states and a header column for variants, both in label 13/18 text.strong. Each cell holds one specimen at true size. If a combination has no meaning, the cell prints "n/a — <reason>" in caption text.secondary and draws nothing else. Each n/a cell has exactly one reason, as given below. Never leave a cell blank.
C5 · Absence specimens. When a state renders nothing (fail-closed), draw the host context around the gap. In the gutter, add a dashed 1px text.secondary annotation box reading "Nothing renders here. No space is reserved and no message is shown." The box is an annotation, not UI. Only SlotMeter has absence states. An error state that shows an InlineErrorRow is not an absence state and is not drawn per C5.
C6 · Dark artboards. Use only these dark values:
- Surfaces: base #0F172A · raised #1E293B · app #020617 · sunken #0F172A · muted #111827.
- Text: primary #E5E7EB · secondary #94A3B8 · strong #F1F5F9.
- Borders: default #1F2937 · strong #374151.
- Focus and link: #38BDF8.
- These light tokens keep their light values in dark mode, with their contrast on dark raised #1E293B checked: semantic.error glyph (3.03:1), semantic.warning glyph (4.59:1) and the primary.600 fill (3.57:1). Draw them by token name.
- Every "text.secondary" ink becomes #94A3B8, every "text.primary" becomes #E5E7EB and every "text.strong" becomes #F1F5F9.
- The primary.700 text buttons and links ("+ Add a date", "See the spot", "List cells" and "Retry") become #38BDF8. The SlotMeter taken-segment edge (primary.700 in light) also becomes #38BDF8, because primary.700 is 2.47:1 on #1E293B.
- Dark highlighted DateRow: a dark base #0F172A wash plus a 1px #38BDF8 outline. Its text is #E5E7EB, at 14.4:1. The light infoBg wash is never used on dark.
- On dark cards (raised #1E293B), surface.sunken fills (#0F172A) look darker than the card. That is intended; their keylines and text carry the edges.
- AqiBand segment gaps use the host surface, which is dark raised #1E293B on cards. Marker and pointer halos stay dark base #0F172A, so the two-tone marker keeps one fixed dark tone.
- The AQI hues do not change in dark mode.
- No selection state is drawn on this board.
C7 · AX5 and 200% artboards.
- iOS AX5 sizes to draw: h1 → 58 · h2 → 58 · h3 → 55 · body/bodyMedium → 53 · bodySmall → 49 · label → 44 · caption → 43 · overline → 40.
- Android 200% sizes to draw (Android 14 non-linear scaling): h1 → 38 · h2 → 36 · h3 → 34 · body → 28 · bodySmall → 26 · label → 25 · caption → 24 · overline → 22.
- Web: 200% browser zoom on web-1440, which gives an effective layout of 720 CSS px.
- Graphic geometry keeps its size, and text reflows around it. This covers tracks, cells, segments, markers, glyph-only buttons, and the marks inside strip cells, lanes and tracks. Marks that sit inside text lines (SourceCaption, DateRow, legends) scale with their text.
- Text wraps and never truncates or ellipsises. Pairs that sit side by side stack.
C8 · Greyscale artboards. Redraw the listed specimens at 100% desaturation. Under each one, print a caption in text.secondary that names the non-colour cue that still carries the meaning, for example "Occupied band: 2px outline + printed name".
C9 · Motion. Show motion as static annotated frames, never as a loop. Annotate Reduce Motion wherever it changes something: skeletons become static, and highlights appear without animation.
C10 · Fixtures and clocks. Use the house-style FIXTURES exactly. TODAY is Mon 19 Oct 2026, 6:10 PM Pacific. Air specimens use these clocks, because AirNow readings are hourly and a reading's age decides its state:
- Ready and offline Air specimens (and every instrument or crop that contains one) are drawn at specimen time 7:10 AM on Mon 19 Oct, so "observed 7:00 AM" is a fresh reading.
- Alert and still-above specimens are drawn at specimen time 4:10 PM.
- Stale Air specimens use TODAY, 6:10 PM, which gives "— 11 hours ago".
- Print "specimen time 7:10 AM" or "specimen time 4:10 PM" once in the gutter of every artboard that shows such a specimen.
Any string that is not in the fixtures, the contract or this prompt must be listed on 00b · Notes. When a specimen shows a value that is not tied to a fixture address (for example Zone AE), label it "specimen value" in the gutter.
C11 · Provenance marks inside instruments are 00a · ProvenanceMark, drawn at size XS (12pt) or S (16pt).
- OFFICIAL is a solid disc.
- ON RECORD, NOT CONFIRMED is a ring with a stroke of at least 1.5px.
- YOU ADDED is a solid disc with a knocked-out tick.
- The ink is text.secondary or darker, always at full opacity.
- Stale style on this board: the same shape at full ink, paired with the age words. The contract's 50% opacity is not used, because text.secondary at 50% is 1.9–2.0:1 on base and sunken, below the 3:1 graphics rule. This deviation is listed on Notes.
Source lines are 00a · SourceCaption (authority · scope word · as-of). Freshness lines are 00a · FreshnessLine, and skeletons are 00d · WarmingSkeleton. Draw each of them exactly as named, and do not redesign them here.
C12 · Focus on web. Keyboard focus is a 2px border.focus outline with a 2px offset around the focusable element (a row, a strip or a button). In dark mode the outline is #38BDF8.
C13 · Micro-geometry. A few offsets below are deliberately off the 4px spacing scale: 6pt, 3pt, 2pt and 1pt, used for cell padding, bar offsets, mark shifts and numeral nudges. They are intentional micro-geometry inside an instrument. Draw them as written, and do not use them for layout spacing anywhere else.
C14 · Standalone words. Widgets and the OG card are standalone surfaces, so they print the provenance word next to each mark or include an in-block legend line. Every widget block below says how it does this.
