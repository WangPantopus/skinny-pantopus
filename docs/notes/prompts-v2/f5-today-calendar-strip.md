LAYOUT & VISUALIZATION: The card is surface.base with radius lg and shadow sm. Top to bottom:
1. Header: "Next 14 days at this address" in overline style, with the summary caption in text.secondary under it. One status line at a time sits under the summary (removed date, or saved outside the window), as a polite status, never a modal.
2. Strip: 14 equal cells across the content width. On iOS that is about 23.5pt pitch, 2pt gaps and cells 56pt tall. "Today" sits above cell 1 and the week-two date ("Mon 26") above cell 8. A weekday initial sits beneath each cell. Each cell is surface.sunken with a 1.5px text.secondary baseline along its bottom edge; weekend cells use the same fill at 50% opacity, with the baseline and marks at full ink; the S S initials carry the meaning. The instrument skeleton has no baselines and no initials, so ready or empty cells never read as loading.
3. Marks: each cell stacks up to 3 XS ProvenanceMarks (12pt) top-down in one ink, text.secondary. A cell draws one mark per pickup stream and one per other row, in row order, pickup streams first. "+N" in text.strong counts the marks not drawn; the summary counts rows. Shape is the only per-mark code. Kind is carried by the KindGlyph on the rows. Each kind uses its own KindGlyph from Foundations (including lease ends, notice deadline, insurance renews, warranty ends, HOA dues, property-tax), never a generic calendar icon. A combined pickup day has two marks and a garbage-only day has one.
4. Region rule: a statewide or county rule is a full-height ink bar filling its cell (cell width minus 6pt, radius xs) with no mark inside. When that day also has address items, the bar narrows to 4pt at the cell's left edge and the marks sit to its right. Draw this narrowed form in context in artboard 15, and as a 4× cell callout beside artboard 05. The bar reads as "a whole region", never as a claim about this house.
5. Tonight's pickup: the cell for tomorrow's pickup (cell 2 on Mon 19) gets a 1.5px text.primary outline, an emphasis, never a banner.
6. Holiday move: the mark sits on the moved day. The usual day shows a struck ghost ring at 50% opacity. The row carries the caption.
7. Pickup marks after the last published city calendar (Dec 2026) use the projected style. Record this on Notes only; it is outside every drawn window.
8. Legend line under the strip, once, with S marks: "● Official · ○ On record, not confirmed · ✓ You added this".
9. DateRows in date order, each at least 56pt. Annual or self-entered rows (HOA dues, renters insurance, a pickup schedule the person set) get a 2px identity.home left rule plus their recurrence caption; seeded city pickup rows never get the rule. At a claimed home, a date the viewer kept private shows the ScopeChip person glyph plus "Only you" at the end of its SourceCaption line. A seeded pickup row whose frequency is Not set carries a visible 44pt text button "Set your pickup day" under its caption (first such row only; later rows offer it in their actions). The button opens the DateSheet in create mode with the Pickup day kind selected and Thursday prefilled. The same action appears in that row's custom actions and in its view-seeded DateSheet. The region chip on civic rows uses the DateRow region-chip slot: ScopeChip pill geometry with a map-region glyph (never the person or house glyph), spoken "applies across Washington" / "applies across Clark County", never "Visible to …". It is not a visibility chip.
10. Foot row: "+ Add a date" (at least 44pt, primary.700), with the scope sentence under it.
When data is missing: empty after a successful check shows drawn empty cells with their baselines, then "Checked — nothing in the next 14 days", then "+ Add a date". A failure draws no strip; an InlineErrorRow sits in the card. The card shows its own FreshnessLine only when the calendar is older than the rest of Today ("Calendar updated 2h ago · Retry calendar"). Otherwise the location row covers freshness.

INTERACTION, MOTION & HAPTICS: On native, cells are not targets. The whole strip is one adjustable element. iOS: swipe up or down to move a day, then double-tap to scroll to that day's first row and highlight it. Android TalkBack: the strip is one focusable node with a state description and the custom actions "Next day" / "Previous day". A plain tap on the strip scrolls to the rows. Web 1440: cells share the content width (about 47px each with 2px gaps, never under 44px), and only cells that hold items are buttons; each moves focus to its first row. The focus ring is a 2px border.focus ring with a 2px offset, distinct from the 1.5px tonight outline and from the highlight wash. Web 390: the strip is decorative and the rows carry everything. A row tap opens the DateSheet. Every row action is reachable without gestures: inside the DateSheet (Remind me, Mark done, Edit, and a "Where this comes from" row that swaps the sheet's content with a Back control, never stacking a second sheet). Screen readers also get them as custom actions: Edit, Remind me, Where this comes from, Mark done; seeded rows drop Edit, and an unset seeded pickup row adds "Set your pickup day". On web 1440 each row also has a 44px overflow button with the same actions. On a push or widget arrival, scroll once, fade in the DateRow highlighted wash in under 300ms, let it decay, and move focus to the row. Cells fill with a 150ms cross-fade when data lands. Under Reduce Motion: static highlight, no shimmer. Haptics: none here; the DateSheet save carries the one light tick.

EDGE CASES: Long titles wrap to two lines beside the glyph. Many items: more than 3 marks on one day show "+N" in the cell, and every item still gets a row. Zero items: the empty specimen. A date saved beyond the window: the place-file status line, never a silent toast. Slow network: the instrument skeleton reserves the final height and holds no text. Offline: the cached strip, the location row's "You're offline · as of 4:10 PM", and "+ Add a date" and "Set your pickup day" disabled with their reason. Permissions: by default a member such as Sam can add dates. A member whose calendar editing the owner turned off (or a Guest) sees rows but no add row, and a LockedActionRow names who can act. A viewer without bill access sees phase-2 money rows redacted. A saved place shows "Only you will see this." and never uses household words. At a home, the add-row sentence states the DateSheet's default ("Everyone in this household will see this."); the DateSheet lets the person keep a date to "Only you", and such rows show the private caption (f1 artboard 13 draws it). The voter row never states or implies registration status. Its "Mark done" is person-scoped ("You marked this done", Only you), and the household never sees it. The tax appeal never shows as a single seeded date; it is a conditional rule in the DateSheet and the place file. Camas has no Saturday bulk pickup.

INSTEAD OF:
- Instead of four class-coloured dots, draw one-ink marks told apart by shape — because hue alone fails greyscale and 3:1.
- Instead of 14 tappable cells on phones, make the strip one element and the rows the targets — because 23pt cells are below 44pt and 48dp.
- Instead of a dot, or a ScopeChip with a house glyph, for the voter deadline, draw a full-height bar and a region chip with a map glyph — because either would read as a claim about this house.
- Instead of "in 11 days" alone, write "in 11 days · Fri 30 Oct" — because people act on deadlines by the date.
- Instead of an even Thursday rhythm through Thanksgiving week, draw the moved mark and a struck ghost — because the usual day is wrong that week.
- Instead of an empty strip when loading fails, draw no strip and a named Retry — because drawn empty cells with baselines mean "checked, nothing found".
- Instead of a toast that vanishes after saving a far-off date, show the "is in your place file · See it" status line — because a saved date must never disappear from view.
- Instead of a seeded pickup row that only opens a locked sheet, draw the "Set your pickup day" button in the row — because the first-week step needs a visible path.
- Instead of a "+ Date" header toggle, keep "+ Add a date" as the last row — because a list gets a list row, not a mode switch.
