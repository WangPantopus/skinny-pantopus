Use the Pantopus house style pasted above. This project is the Foundations board: States, feedback and asks.

TYPE: NEW (a component specimen board). This project draws no screens. It draws eight components as specimens: QuietDayReceipt, WarmingSkeleton, InlineUndo, DestructiveConfirm, LandingBannerSlot, NotificationAsk, PushCopy and ReminderLeadControl. Later projects (59 surfaces, each in a separate project with no shared memory) will use these components by exact name. When the founder accepts a component, it is published into the Pantopus design system. What you draw here is exactly what every later screen will get, so every size, token, string and state below is literal.

ATTACH:
1. A screenshot of the shipped mailbox-arrival pill at the top of the Place tab (iOS, Android and web-390). LandingBannerSlot copies its geometry exactly. If this screenshot is missing, use the fallback geometry given under LandingBannerSlot and list "mailbox-arrival pill screenshot missing; fallback geometry used" on 00d · Notes.
2. Optional, reference only: docs/screenshots/hub-v2-skeleton-ios.png, hub-v2-skeleton-android.png and hub-v2-skeleton-web.png (the existing shimmer tone). Treat these three as tone references and nothing more. Their layout is not a spec.
No other component on this board exists in the shipped app, so nothing else is attached.

WHY THIS BOARD EXISTS. These eight components cover the moments when the product has to be honest about time and consent: nothing is wrong (QuietDayReceipt), something is still loading (WarmingSkeleton), something can be taken back (InlineUndo), something can't be taken back (DestructiveConfirm), something is waiting for you (LandingBannerSlot), the product wants to interrupt you (NotificationAsk, PushCopy), and the product will interrupt you on dates you chose (ReminderLeadControl). Each has a quiet default and none of them nags.

BOARD CONVENTIONS (these apply to all 44 artboards in this project)
====================================================================
C1. Canvas and names. Each artboard is a 1600px-wide spec board on surface.app (dark twins: dark app), with height as needed. Above each frame, print the artboard name exactly as the ARTBOARDS manifest gives it, in label 13/18 text.strong. Name format: "00d · <ComponentName> · <NN-what> · <light|dark>". This board's name format replaces the house-style artboard format ("<surface-id> · <platform> · <NN-state> · <light|dark>"); the platform goes in each specimen's C13 name instead. The Notes artboard is named exactly "00d · Notes", with no light/dark suffix.
C2. Specimens are drawn at 1x at their real platform width: iOS 393pt, Android 412dp, web-390 390px, web-1440 1440px (crop web-1440 to the relevant column and label the crop "web-1440 crop"). Print the platform tag (ios / android / web-390 / web-1440) under each specimen in caption 12/16 text.secondary.
C3. Callouts. Draw callouts as numbered markers: 16pt squares with radius xs, a text.strong 1px outline and a caption numeral. Connect each marker to its part with a 1px text.secondary leader line. The key sits beside the specimen: "1 · 16pt tick glyph · text.secondary". Callout markers are always squares, never circles, so they can't be mistaken for provenance marks. Dimension rulers are 1px text.secondary with caption numbers (for example "44pt").
C4. Grids. Each "variants × states" artboard is a table. Rows are variants and columns are states. Headers are label 13/18/600 text.strong in sentence case, written as C13 gives them. Each cell holds one specimen. If a combination doesn't apply, the cell shows the caption "Not applicable: <reason in one clause>" on surface.sunken in text.strong. Every "Not applicable" on this board carries its reason; never leave a cell blank and never print a bare "Not applicable".
C5. Dark tokens and the contrast table. Dark twins redraw their grid with the house-style dark tokens, plus these dark tokens the house style doesn't list: dark text.strong #F1F5F9, dark text.muted #64748B (disabled controls only), dark text.inverse #111827, dark border.strong #374151. Dark surface.sunken equals dark base, so it can't separate anything. In dark mode a card's edge comes from the fill step (dark raised on dark app), not from border.default. Dark cards have no shadow. Each dark grid below names its container surface.
This is the only contrast table on the board. Every section refers to it as "(C5)"; print the matching figure beside the part on the board.
  Light, on surface.base: text.secondary 4.83:1 · semantic.error 4.83:1 (glyphs and borders only) · primary.700 5.93:1, and text.inverse on a primary.700 fill 5.93:1 · semantic.warning 3.19:1 (glyphs only) · text.muted 2.54:1 (disabled labels only, never readable text).
  Dark, on dark raised #1E293B: dark text.secondary #94A3B8 5.71:1 · link #38BDF8 6.83:1 · semantic.error #DC2626 3.03:1 (glyphs only) · semantic.warning #D97706 4.59:1 (glyphs only) · dark border.default 1.00:1 (never an edge).
  Dark, on dark base #0F172A: #94A3B8 6.96:1 · semantic.error #DC2626 3.70:1 (glyphs and borders only) · dark text.inverse #111827 on a #38BDF8 fill 8.28:1.
C6. Every "ax5-200-greyscale" artboard has three required columns: (a) iOS at AX5 (body 53pt), (b) Android at 200% font scale with Android 14 non-linear scaling (body 16sp renders at about 28sp; caption 12sp renders at 24sp), (c) a greyscale copy (100% desaturated) of the light default and of the most state-heavy light cell, captioned with what still tells the states apart (a word, a glyph shape, a weight). At AX5 and 200%, pairs stack, text wraps instead of truncating, sheets may scroll, and no string ever ends in an ellipsis unless this prompt says it does (only PushCopy's collapsed tray does).
C7. Every "in-context-and-rules" artboard has five panels in this order: IN CONTEXT (two or three mini crops, each 393pt wide for iOS or 390px wide for web-390, cropped to about 300pt tall, each labelled with its consuming surface id and captioned "host illustrative, not a spec" in caption text.secondary; under the crops, one line "Also consumed by: <ids>" listing the rest of the component's consumers), ACCESSIBILITY (spoken label pattern, non-colour encoding, target rule), COPY (the rule plus real strings), PLATFORMS (iOS vs Android vs web), INSTEAD OF (each line reads "Instead of X, draw Y — because Z", with a tiny struck-through thumbnail of X beside a thumbnail of Y). C8. Targets on every specimen. Draw the hit area of every control as a dashed 1px text.secondary rectangle with its size: 44×44pt on iOS, 48×48dp on Android with 8dp gaps, 44×44px on web. A small glyph is never a target by itself; its row or button is.
C9. Focus vs selection. Wherever a focus state is drawn, focus is a 2px border.focus ring (dark #38BDF8) with a 2px offset. Selection is always the same treatment on this board: primary.50 fill, 1.5px primary.700 border, a 16pt check glyph in primary.700 and a 600-weight label in text.primary (dark: dark raised fill, 1.5px #38BDF8 border, #38BDF8 check, dark text primary label). C10. Glyphs. Use the existing Pantopus icon set only, at 16pt unless stated otherwise, with a 1.5px stroke. The tick used in receipts and chips is a plain check stroke, never a filled or hollow circle, because filled vs hollow means provenance and nothing else. No control on this board uses a radio circle.
C11. Every string on this board comes from this prompt. Any string you have to add goes on 00d · Notes.
C12. Contrast values printed on the board are the ones in the C5 table. Don't invent new ratios.
C13. Specimen names. Print under every specimen, in caption text.strong, its published name: "<Component> / <variant name in lower case> / <state name in lower case> / <platform>", for example "InlineUndo / removed row / pending undo / ios". Grid row headers read "V1 · Removed row" and column headers read "S1 · Pending undo". The same rule applies to specimens outside grids. Where a section lists exact names, use those. Where a specimen has no state column, use its drawn state name (for example "NotificationAsk / primer ios / never asked / ios").
C14. Components from other Foundations boards. Use FourteenDayStrip, YearBand, KindGlyph, AqiBand, ScaleStrip, PickupCard and MemberRow from the published Pantopus design system. If one is not published yet, draw a labelled box at the size below in surface.sunken with the component name in caption text.strong, and list it on 00d · Notes. Fallback sizes: FourteenDayStrip 361×40pt · YearBand 361×120pt with four 24pt lanes · AqiBand 361×48pt · ScaleStrip row 361×48pt · PickupCard 361×196pt · MemberRow 361×56pt · KindGlyph a 16pt or 42pt square as stated. The same fallback applies to the Pantopus app icon (a 20pt box labelled "Pantopus app icon"). Host screens in IN CONTEXT crops that haven't been drawn yet are drawn only as far as the crop needs, and captioned per C7.
C15. Disabled controls. Every disabled control on this board stays focusable and reads its reason aloud ("<label>, unavailable, <reason>"). There are no silent dead buttons. Disabled labels use text.muted (dark #64748B) and are never the only place the reason is written: the reason also appears in readable text (text.primary, or text.secondary on base/raised, or text.strong on sunken).

2. WarmingSkeleton
====================================================================
PURPOSE. Content-shaped placeholders sitting in their final slots during cold loads of 1–10 s, so nothing jumps when the data arrives.

ANATOMY (00d · WarmingSkeleton · 01-anatomy · light, iOS 393, with callouts)
1. Shapes are filled surface.sunken (#F3F4F6) on surface.base cards. They match the final geometry: strip cells, track shapes, row bars, avatar circles, and for a facts count a number bar plus pill-shaped chip bars (the count is an integer plus chips; there is no ring, so there is no ring skeleton). Each shape uses the same radius as the part it stands in for. Shapes are filled, not outlined, because an outline-only skeleton reads as an empty state.
2. The skeleton reserves the exact final height. Draw a height ruler beside each skeleton and a second ruler beside the loaded version, and caption both "same height".
3. No text, no glyphs and no "checking…" anywhere inside a skeleton.
4. Shimmer: a soft band 30% of the shape's width, surface.base at 60% opacity, sweeping left to right over the shapes. One pass takes 1.2 s. The shimmer stops after 4 passes (under 5 s) and holds static.
5. Container: one accessibility element labelled "Loading" (callout).
6. Dark: shapes are dark raised #1E293B on dark base. On a dark raised card, shapes are dark border.strong #374151. Skeleton shapes are decorative and have no contrast requirement; print that on the board.
