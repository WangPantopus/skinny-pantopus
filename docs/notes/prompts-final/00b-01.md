PURPOSE (print it): The four-layer reading instrument. It draws one row per authority on that authority's own ordered bands, and it never adds up to a grade.
USED IN (print it): f8-scale-strips, f6-place-section-details, f8-compare-arrival-header, f8-compare-reveal, f8-compare-sheet, f8-og-compare-card.
WEB-1440 CARD: 640px wide, with 608px of content.
SPECIMEN CLOCKS
- Ready Air rows, including the compare "You" row, are drawn at 7:10 AM on Mon 19 Oct, so "observed 7:00 AM" counts as fresh. Print "specimen time 7:10 AM" in the gutter.
- Stale Air rows use TODAY, 6:10 PM.

ANATOMY (01-anatomy; put a callout on every part)
- Row order is fixed as Flood · Wildfire · Air · Radon, and rows are never re-sorted, because people compare rows by position.
- A row contains, from top to bottom:
  - the layer name, in label 13/18/600 text.strong
  - the value line "Word · number", in bodyMedium 16/24/500 text.primary
  - the track zone
  - the band names
  - 00a · SourceCaption
- Row padding is 12pt top and bottom. Gaps: name → value 4pt · value → track zone 8pt · track zone → band names 4pt · band names → caption 8pt.
- Each row is at least 48pt tall and is a single target. Markers and pointers are never targets.
- Layout:
  - Below 560pt (phones and web-390), the row is stacked, with a 326pt track.
  - At 560pt and wider (web-1440), the row has two columns: left 237px, gap 16px, right 355px. The left column stacks the name, the value line and the SourceCaption. The right column holds the track zone and the band names, top-aligned with the value line.
  - AX5 and 200% are stacked.
  - Part 2 defines the OG column.
- Track zone: 24pt tall, with a 12pt track centred in it.
- Track:
  - equal-width bands per layer (the layers don't share an axis)
  - filled surface.raised
  - 1.5px text.secondary separators (4.63:1 on raised)
  - a 1px text.secondary keyline with radius xs 4 around the whole track
  - the least hazard on the left
- Occupied band: a 2px text.primary outline inside its edges.
- Marker: a vertical capsule, 4 × 20pt, radius pill.
  - Core: text.primary. Halo: 2px surface.base, which makes it 8 × 24pt overall. The halo is #0F172A in dark.
  - It is centred in the occupied band and extends 4pt beyond the track top and bottom.
  - It is never a disc (G4).
- Band names, in caption 12/16 text.secondary:
  - Up to 2 lines each, never truncated.
  - Flood and Radon print all their names. Wildfire prints the two end names plus the occupied one.
  - The occupied name is set in captionMedium 12/16/500 text.primary, directly under the marker. If it comes within 8pt of an end name, it replaces that end name.
  - Air prints "0–50" · "51–100" · "101–150" · "151–200" · "201–300" · "301+", with the occupied range in captionMedium text.primary.
- Bands:
  - Flood, by annual chance: "Minimal (X)" · "0.2% a year (shaded X)" · "1% a year or more (A/V zones)". The value line prints the actual zone code. These FEMA annual-chance names and "1% chance each year" are the band names G2 allows.
  - Wildfire (USFS): "Very low" · "Low" · "Moderate" · "High" · "Very high".
  - Radon: "Zone 3 · low potential" · "Zone 2 · moderate potential" · "Zone 1 · highest potential". Never "N of M".
  - Air: the AqiBand instrument-row variant. It is the only coloured track. The next component defines AqiBand; draw this row exactly as follows:
    - six equal-width 12pt segments in the house-style EPA ColorVision Assist hues
    - 2px gaps in the host surface
    - a 1px text.secondary keyline on each segment, with radius xs 4 on the outer corners
    - on the occupied segment, a 2px text.primary inner outline
    - the same 4 × 20pt capsule, placed by value within its segment
    - no reading: segments in surface.sunken with their keylines, and no marker
    - the value line leaves out the pollutant
- Fixture rows (HOME A). All are Official; "●" stands for the drawn Official mark.

  | Row | Label | Value | Occupied | Caption |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | Flood | "Flood zone" | "Zone X — minimal flood hazard" | band 1 | "● FEMA · area zone · effective Sep 2021" |
  | Wildfire | "Wildfire hazard potential" | "Moderate · 3 of 5" | band 3 | "● USFS · quarter-mile area · 2023" |
  | Air | "Air quality index (AQI)" | "AQI 42 · Good" | 84% of Good | "● AirNow · nearest monitors · observed 7:00 AM" |
  | Radon | "Radon zone" | "Zone 1 — highest potential (county)" | band 3 | "● EPA · county-wide estimate — only a test tells you about this home" |

- Summary. Print this one sentence under the 01 fixture instrument: "Flood Zone X, minimal flood hazard; Wildfire Moderate, 3 of 5; Air AQI 42, Good; Radon Zone 1, county-wide."
- Specimen value: a Flood row with "Zone AE — 1% chance each year (about 1 in 4 over 30 years)" and band 3 occupied.

VARIANTS [row keys]
- V1 Flood [flood] · V2 Wildfire [wildfire] · V3 Radon [radon] · V4 Air [air].
- V5 compare [compare]: two identical labelled pointers replace the capsule. Filled vs hollow means provenance only.
  - Track zone, 60pt tall, from top to bottom:

    - the 8pt "You" pointer

    Band names sit 4pt below the track zone.
  - Pointers: triangles 12 wide × 8 tall, in text.primary with a 2px surface.base halo.
    - The sender pointer points down, with its tip on the track top. The "You" pointer points up, with its tip on the track bottom.
    - Each pointer is centred in its band. On Air, it is placed by value.
  - Each occupied band is outlined; a band both places share gets one outline. The two pointers share an x position on the non-Air layers, and on Air only when the values are equal.
  - Value lines: "Dana: <value>" and "You: <value>". When the band is shared, print one line, "Both: <value>".
  - Chip: "Same band" or "Different band", right-aligned on the layer-name line.
    - A 24pt pill in surface.sunken, 8pt side padding, captionMedium text.strong, no glyph and no hue.

- V6 no data [no-data] (Flood, Wildfire, Radon): bands in surface.sunken, keyline and separators unchanged, no marker and no outline, value "Not on record".

- V8 non-burnable (Wildfire): the same greyed track, with the value "Non-burnable land cover (USFS)".
- V6–V8 must each look different from Wildfire "Very low", because missing data is not a low reading.
- V9 frozen [frozen]:

STATES [column keys]
- loading [loading]: 00d · WarmingSkeleton.
  - Only the track keyline and separators show.
  - The label and value become surface.sunken bars of 60×12 and 140×16.
  - The exact row height is reserved.
- ready [ready].
- partial, loading [partial-loading]: some rows are ready and others are loading.
- partial, failed [partial-failed]: the Air row's slot shows 00c · InlineErrorRow "We couldn't reach AirNow just now" with Retry. The other rows are ready.

  - The other rows are unchanged.

COPY
- Use these verbatim: "Zone X — minimal flood hazard" · "Zone AE — 1% chance each year (about 1 in 4 over 30 years)" · "Wildfire hazard potential: Moderate · 3 of 5" · "Radon Zone 1 — highest potential (county)".
- Pair a word with a number on every layer except Radon.
- Sender rows describe places. Outbound actions start with a verb.
- "N of M" appears only in Wildfire readings, in "category 1 of 6", and in the compare summary's "3 of 4 bands".
- Never write: "100-year floodplain", "minimal risk", "wildfire risk", "better", "worse", "wins".

PLATFORMS: web draws it as SVG; iOS (SwiftUI) and Android (Compose) draw it natively. The geometry is identical on all three; only the target minimums differ by platform.

INSTEAD OF
- Instead of a grade, star, radar chart, cross-layer ramp or shared 0–100 axis, draw each authority's own bands, because the layers never add up to a verdict.
- Instead of arrows, trophies or better/worse words, print "Different band" or "Same band", because a difference between places is not a ranking.
