Component 00b-06: PublicPointMap. Follow the board rules from the setup message.

PURPOSE (print it): Shows exactly where neighbours see a post, drawn at true scale from the server's stable public point.
USED IN (print it): f9-privacy-mirror, f9-nearby-cells-map.

FRAMES: 02 cells are drawn at iOS 393 geometry (326pt content). Controls reserve 96pt (148pt for V3). The Android values, 104dp (160dp for V3), apply on 03.

ANATOMY (on 01-anatomy, with a callout on every part; the address is HOME A and the viewer is Maya Chen)
- V1 stack, top to bottom: map thumb · 8pt · caption · 8pt · controls. In context only, the hidden-from-neighbors row sits between the caption and the controls, with 8pt above and below it.
- Map thumb: 16:9, 326 × 183pt, radius md 8, 1px text.secondary edge. The frame is centred on the public pin.
  - Base map, neutral tokens only, no brand tint: land is surface.sunken; streets are 4pt surface.base lines with 1px text.secondary casing.
  - Street labels: caption 12/16 text.strong with a 2px surface.base halo: "NE 18th St", "NE 138th Ave", "NE 23rd St".
- Public pin: one 24pt teardrop in text.primary, 2px surface.base halo, 6pt surface.base centre.
- Pin label chip, above the pin: a pill in surface.base, 1px text.secondary edge, shadow md, reading exactly "What neighbors see" in label 13/18 text.primary.
- Scale bar, bottom left, on an 80% surface.base plate: a 1.5px text.primary line with end ticks; labels "0", "0.1", "0.2 mi" in caption 12/16 text.primary on the plate. The thumb spans about 0.8 mi, so 0.2 mi = 81pt.
- Caption, bodySmall text.primary, the map's one-sentence summary, used exactly: "Neighbors see your posts at this spot, the same spot every time, about 0.3 mi from your address."
- Hidden-from row (f9-privacy-mirror; draw only in context): "Hidden from neighbors: your street address, your home on the map".
- Controls:
  - Each button is 44 × 44pt on iOS and web and 48 × 48dp on Android: radius md 8, surface.base fill, 1px text.secondary edge, 20pt text.primary glyph. The accessible names are the words below.
  - One two-row cluster at every size. Left edge of the content width: a zoom column, "Zoom in" (+) above "Zoom out" (−), 8pt apart. Right edge: a 3 × 2 arrow pad with 8pt gaps. Top row: "Move map up" (↑) in the middle slot. Bottom row: "Move map left" (←), "Move map down" (↓), "Move map right" (→).
  - Size: the pad is 148pt wide on iOS and web (3 × 44 + 2 × 8) and 160dp on Android. The cluster needs at least 208pt (224dp) with a 16pt minimum gap between column and pad, so it fits 326pt. It is 96pt tall on iOS and web and 104dp on Android.
  - Disabled buttons: fill unchanged, still focusable, with a reason. Light: glyph and edge in text.muted. Dark: glyph and edge in #475569 (an added dark value, listed on Notes D), so disabled stays distinct from the enabled #94A3B8 edge. Disabled controls are exempt from the contrast minimums; the reason text carries the meaning.
  - Focus order: Zoom in, Zoom out, Move map up, Move map left, Move map down, Move map right, then List cells when present.
- Cells-map parts (V3):
  - V3 stack, top to bottom: map 326 × 326 (the same base map at the same zoom, with streets and street labels, no pin, no chip, scale bar bottom left) · 8pt · summary line · 4pt · FreshnessLine · 8pt · controls · 8pt · List cells.
  - Grid: three columns and three rows of 108/110/108pt, no gaps; neighbouring cells share one 1px text.secondary outline. Each cell has a 70% surface.base fill over the base map.
  - Each cell prints its bucket label centred in label 13/18 text.primary: "0", "1–5", "6–15" or "16+".
  - The user's cell gets a 2px primary.600 inner outline inside its rectangle (the contract calls it a ring; #38BDF8 in dark mode), and "Your cell" in caption text.primary, centred 4pt below its bucket label.
  - "List cells": a text button (primary.700; #38BDF8 in dark mode), left-aligned, 44pt tall (48dp on Android). The V3 controls block is therefore 148pt tall on iOS and web (96 + 8 + 44) and 160dp on Android (104 + 8 + 48).

INSTEAD OF
- Instead of a 150 m ring around the home, draw the single public pin, because the real offset area is about 12 times larger and a ring centred on the home reveals it.
- Instead of a home marker "for orientation", show street labels only, because a screenshot must never locate the home.
- Instead of a blurred, fogged or locked map or list, draw everything and state the requirement in words, because a hidden map is a carrot, not information.
- Instead of a lightness-only density ramp, print the bucket label in every cell, because adjacent ramp steps are only 1.16 to 1.66:1 apart.
- Instead of one long controls row, draw the zoom column and arrow pad, because six 48dp buttons do not fit in 326pt.
- Instead of pinch and drag only, give every movement a labelled button, because gestures alone exclude switch and keyboard users.
