INSTEAD OF
- Instead of a "Remove this place?" dialog for a cheap delete, draw the V1 line — because routine actions are reversible and dialogs teach click-through.
- Instead of "Undo (4s)" with a countdown, draw "Undo" with no timer — because undo stays until the person leaves the screen.
- Instead of a toast as the only trace, draw the in-row line, with the toast only as an echo — because toasts vanish before some people can reach them.
- Instead of sending "marked paid" to the household at once, hold it until the undo line closes — because a notification can't be taken back.
