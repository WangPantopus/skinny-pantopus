ANATOMY (01-anatomy-and-levels: V1 S2 on iOS 393 with callouts, then the levels table)
1. The tray preview is an in-app illustration: a surface.raised inset card (radius lg 12, padding 12) on a surface.sunken panel (radius lg, padding 16). It is never drawn as a system alert, over a wallpaper, or with system chrome. In the grids, the panel spans the full device width (393pt on iOS, 412dp on Android), so the inset is the real notification width: 361pt on iOS (337pt of text) and 380dp on Android. Inside a host sheet (IN CONTEXT, and the NotificationAsk primers) the panel fits the sheet's content width and the inset shrinks with it.
2. Inside the inset, the top row: the Pantopus app icon from the design system at 20pt (R15; if it isn't published, use the R15 fallback box and log it on Notes C), then "Pantopus" in caption 12/16 text.strong, then "now" in caption text.strong, right-aligned. (Secondary words on the raised inset inside a sunken frame use text.strong.) R10 has no light-raised row, so print no contrast figure for these words. On Android, the row uses the Android small-icon form of the same app icon, in the same illustrated style.
3. Title: bodySmallMedium 14/20/500 text.primary, 30 characters or fewer, caveat first. Under the string, draw a ruler marked in characters: one small tick per 5 characters, a labelled tick at 30, and the part after the last character shaded as the remaining room.
4. Body: bodySmall 14/20 text.primary, 40 characters or fewer on its first line. Draw the same character ruler, with the labelled tick at 40.
5. Place label: the street name only ("Larkspur Loop"), and only when the person has more than one place. Callout: "drop ' · Larkspur Loop' for someone with one place".
6. Caption under the panel: "This is the whole thing." in caption text.strong (it sits on the sunken panel, R10: 9.37:1).
7. Actions (expanded only): title-case text buttons inside the inset in bodySmallMedium primary.700, separated from the body by a 1px border.default rule (decoration). Print no contrast figure for them (no light-raised row in R10). They are drawn for illustration; real tray actions belong to the system.
8. Levels table, on the same artboard: four rows, each printed with its name from KEYS.
   - pickup, briefings, dates, bills: iOS Active / Android DEFAULT.
   - household: iOS Passive / Android LOW, one thread per home.
   - air at AQI 151 or above, and NOAA warnings: iOS Time Sensitive / Android HIGH.
   - air below AQI 151: iOS Active / Android DEFAULT.
   Under the table, print: "Never Time Sensitive for pickup." and "Android: the air group holds two channels, one HIGH (AQI 151 or above, and NOAA warnings) and one DEFAULT (below 151). Every other group has one channel."

ACCESSIBILITY
- The in-app illustration is read as one element: "Example notification. Unconfirmed: pickup tomorrow. Recycling + garbage · city schedule. This is the whole thing." (each variant reads its own title and body).
- No house numbers and no full addresses in any string. Actions are title case.
- Non-colour encoding: levels are labelled in words on the board.
- Targets: the illustration is not interactive. Real tray actions belong to the system.

COPY: all strings are given in ANATOMY, VARIANTS and STATES; use them exactly. Never "Open Pantopus to…". No keeper name or voice. No marketing, Founding or referral pushes. No pickup reminder alongside the briefing for the same pickup.

INSTEAD OF
- Instead of "Recycling and garbage tomorrow — not yet confirmed", draw "Unconfirmed: pickup tomorrow" — because at 30 characters a caveat at the end is the part that gets cut.
- Instead of "Bins out tonight. 2418 NE Larkspur Loop.", draw "Bins out tonight · Larkspur Loop" — because a house number doesn't belong on a lock screen.
- Instead of "Sam paid Clark PUD $142.18", draw "Sam marked Clark PUD paid" — because amounts stay inside the app, and "marked paid" is the product's word.
- Instead of the notification over a phone wallpaper, as a system alert, or with a copied Time Sensitive badge, draw the inset illustration captioned "This is the whole thing." — because an imitation of system UI misleads.
- Instead of "Open Pantopus to see today's pickup", draw the fact itself — because the notification is the whole message.
- Instead of Time Sensitive for a pickup reminder, draw Active — because pickup is never Time Sensitive.
