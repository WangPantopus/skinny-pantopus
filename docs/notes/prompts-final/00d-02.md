ANATOMY (01-anatomy: V1 at S1 on iOS 393 with callouts, plus V3d at S1 beside it, named "WarmingSkeleton / instrument-skeleton-bill-trend / shimmer / ios / light")
1. Shapes are surface.sunken fills on surface.base cards, matching the final geometry: strip cells, track shapes, row bars, avatar circles. A count of facts is a number bar plus pill chip bars (the count is an integer plus chips, so there is no ring skeleton). Each shape takes the radius of the part it stands in for. Shapes are filled, never outlined, because an outline-only skeleton reads as an empty state. The avatar circle is placeholder geometry and carries no meaning (R13).
2. The skeleton reserves the exact final height. Beside it, draw a height ruler; beside a loaded version, a second ruler; caption both "same height". The loaded version is a review aid, not published: AqiBand (AQI 42 Good), PickupCard for HOME A (recycling and garbage, Tue 20 Oct) and the V3a loaded strip below, as R15 components or fallback boxes.
3. No text, no glyphs and no "checking…" inside a skeleton.
4. Shimmer: a soft band 30% of the shape's width, surface.base at 60% opacity, sweeping left to right. 1.2 s per pass; it stops after 4 passes (4.8 s) and holds static. This is an idle loop under the 5 s rule, not a 300ms transition (Notes 2.4). Draw the band mid-sweep with the callout "1.2 s per pass · stops after 4 passes".
5. Container: one accessibility element labelled "Loading" (callout).
6. Print on the board: "Skeleton shapes are decorative and have no contrast requirement."

INSTEAD OF
- Instead of a full-page spinner, draw skeletons in their final slots — because a spinner hides the wait and the page jumps.
- Instead of an empty card frame, draw filled shapes — because a frame-only skeleton looks empty.
- Instead of "Checking air…", draw text-free shapes — because a skeleton must not claim a check is running.
- Instead of a ring for a count, draw a number bar and chip bars — because the count is an integer plus chips.
- Instead of a skeleton for the founding meter, the Earn row or LandingBannerSlot, draw nothing — because they fail closed.
- Instead of an endless shimmer, stop after 4 passes — because idle loops stop within 5 s.
