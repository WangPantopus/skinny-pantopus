PURPOSE (print it): The one officially coloured scale. It shows the EPA AQI with its category name and is drawn the same way everywhere air appears.
USED IN (print it): f1-today-air-band, f8-scale-strips, f6-place-section-details, f7-today-widget, f7-widget-gallery, f7-widget-howto-sheet, f7-widget-tap-landing, x-provenance-sheet, f8-og-compare-card, f8-compare-reveal.
WEB-1440 CARD: 640px wide, with 608px of content.

SPECIMEN CLOCKS
AirNow readings are hourly, so the age of a reading decides its state. On every artboard that shows one of these specimens, print its specimen time once in the gutter.
- Ready and offline specimens: 7:10 AM on Mon 19 Oct, so "observed 7:00 AM" counts as fresh.
- Alert and still-above specimens: 4:10 PM.
- Stale specimens: TODAY, 6:10 PM, which gives "— 11 hours ago".

ANATOMY (01-anatomy; put a callout on every part)
- Segments: six equal-width category segments in the EPA ColorVision Assist hues. These are the only non-token colours in the product.
- Swatch row: show the hues as a row, with each name and RGB value:
  - aqi.good: Good 158,255,145
  - aqi.moderate: Moderate 255,201,5
  - aqi.usg: Unhealthy for Sensitive Groups 255,130,5
  - aqi.unhealthy: Unhealthy 240,34,0
  - aqi.veryUnhealthy: Very Unhealthy 137,9,151
  - aqi.hazardous: Hazardous 100,0,21
- Segment height: 16pt in the detail variant (Today), 12pt in the instrument row, and 8pt in the widget micro variant.
- Gaps: 2px between segments, in the host surface. That is surface.base on light cards and surface.raised (dark) on dark cards.
- Keylines: each segment gets its own 1px text.secondary keyline, because some hues nearly vanish against their surface (Good is 1.22:1 and Moderate 1.54:1 on white; Hazardous is 1.08:1 on dark raised). The outer corners of the first and last segments have radius xs 4.
- Detail segment width: (326 − 5 × 2) ÷ 6 = 52.7pt. On web-1440: (608 − 10) ÷ 6 = 99.7px.
- Range labels: centred under each segment, in caption 12/16 text.secondary: "0–50" · "51–100" · "101–150" · "151–200" · "201–300" · "301+".
  - The occupied label switches to captionMedium text.primary.
  - The occupied segment gets a 2px text.primary inner outline.
- Marker: the ScaleStrip capsule, 4pt wide.
  - Core: text.primary. Halo: 2px surface.base. In dark mode the halo stays the fixed dark tone #0F172A.
  - Height is the segment height + 8pt: 24pt in detail, 20pt in the instrument row, 16pt in widget micro.
  - It is never a disc (G4).
  - It passes 3:1 on every hue in both themes, because one of its two tones always contrasts.
- Marker position:
  - Position within the segment = (value − segment low) ÷ (segment high − segment low).
  - For position only, the "301+" segment spans 301–500.
  - AQI 42 sits at 84% of Good. AQI 118 sits at 35% of Unhealthy for Sensitive Groups.
  - Values above 500 clamp to the right end of "301+", and the actual number is still printed.
- Marker contrast table. Print it on 01: core and halo contrast against each hue.

  | Hue | Light core | Light halo | Dark core | Dark halo |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | Good | 14.50 | 1.22 | 1.01 | 14.59 |
  | Moderate | 11.50 | 1.54 | 1.25 | 11.57 |
  | USG | 7.14 | 2.49 | 2.01 | 7.18 |
  | Unhealthy | 4.17 | 4.26 | 3.44 | 4.19 |
  | Very Unhealthy | 2.15 | 8.24 | 6.65 | 2.17 |
  | Hazardous | 1.31 | 13.52 | 10.92 | 1.32 |

- Value line: above the band, in bodyMedium text.primary.
  - Fixture: "AQI 42 · Good · PM2.5". Alert: "AQI 118 · Unhealthy for Sensitive Groups · PM2.5".
  - The category and number always sit on the card in text.primary, never on a fill (text.primary on Hazardous is only 1.31:1).
  - The value line is the visible summary.
  - Inside ScaleStrip, the pollutant is left out.
- Health statement: below the band, in body 16/24 text.primary, verbatim from EPA.
  - Good: "Air quality is satisfactory, and air pollution poses little or no risk."
  - Unhealthy for Sensitive Groups: "Members of sensitive groups may experience health effects. The general public is less likely to be affected." Then add the PM2.5 line: "People with heart or lung disease, older adults, children, and people of lower socioeconomic status are the groups most at risk."
- PANTOPUS NOTE: Pantopus context, labelled as ours. It appears on the alert and still-above specimens only.
  - It is a separate block below the EPA statement. The EPA statement stays above it, unchanged.
  - The block holds the overline "PANTOPUS NOTE", then, in bodySmall text.primary, "Your alert level is 101. You can change it in Notifications."
  - On still-above, the block holds a second line directly below that one, also in bodySmall text.primary: "Still above your alert level (101) since 4:00 PM. No new alert until it drops below 101 and rises again."
- Threshold rule: drawn at the user's chosen threshold (101, 151 or 201), never hard-coded.
  - A 1.5px dashed vertical rule (3 on, 2 off) in text.primary, extending 6pt beyond the band top and bottom.
  - When the threshold equals a segment's low value, centre the rule in the 2px gap before that segment. 101 sits in the Moderate/USG gap; 151 sits in the USG/Unhealthy gap.
  - The threshold number sits above the rule, in caption text.primary ("101").
  - "Crossed 101 at 4:00 PM" appears in bodySmallMedium text.primary, with a leading 16pt alert glyph in semantic.warning (on the glyph only).
  - The fixture threshold is 101, the server default. Also draw one specimen at 151, labelled "threshold 151 (not crossed)".
  - In the instrument row, the threshold number sits to the right of the rule's top end, and the track zone grows from 24pt to 40pt, in the alert cell only.
- 00a · SourceCaption: "● AirNow · nearest monitors · observed 7:00 AM". On alert, it reads "observed 4:00 PM". "●" stands for the drawn Official mark.

ACCESSIBILITY
- The summary is the value line. The band is one element. Spell out "Air quality index (AQI)" once per surface.
- "category N of 6" is allowed in spoken labels only.
- Print these labels under their 02 cells:
  - detail ready: "Air quality index 42, Good, category 1 of 6. AirNow, observed 7 AM."
  - detail stale: "Air quality index 42, Good, category 1 of 6. AirNow, observed 7 AM, 11 hours ago."
  - detail no-reading: "Air quality: No reading for this address right now."
  - detail alert: "Air quality index 118, Unhealthy for Sensitive Groups, category 3 of 6. AirNow, observed 4 PM."
  - detail still-above: "Air quality index 118, Unhealthy for Sensitive Groups, category 3 of 6. Still above your alert level, 101, since 4 PM. AirNow, observed 4 PM."
  - detail offline: "You're offline. Air quality index 42, Good, category 1 of 6. AirNow, observed 7 AM."
  - instrument-row ready, stale and alert: the matching detail label followed by " Official." (the ScaleStrip Air-row form)
  - instrument-row no-reading: "Air quality: No reading for this address right now." (no "Official.", because no reading is shown)
  - widget-micro ready: "Air quality 42, Good. Official, AirNow, 7 AM."
  - widget-micro no-reading: "Air quality: no reading. Official, AirNow."
  - widget-micro alert: "Air quality 118, Unhealthy for Sensitive Groups. Official, AirNow, 4 PM."
- Separators and keylines keep the band readable in greyscale. In tinted widgets, the printed name carries the meaning.
- The band is not a target. Retry and row actions are 44pt / 48dp / 44px.

COPY
- EPA names and health text are verbatim: "Good", "Moderate", "Unhealthy for Sensitive Groups", "Unhealthy", "Very Unhealthy", "Hazardous".
- "Sensitive groups" appears only in widgets.
- Never write: "satisfactory for everyone", "air score", "pollution level".

PLATFORMS: the geometry is identical on iOS, Android and web. The widget differences are defined on 03, in part 2.

INSTEAD OF
- Instead of a proportional 0–500 track, draw six equal segments ending in "301+", because proportional widths squeeze 0–100 and can't place readings above 500.
- Instead of brand blue or an invented ramp, use the EPA hues, because this is the authority's own scale.
- Instead of "Air quality is satisfactory for everyone", print EPA's exact sentence, because a paraphrase is an EPA reporting error.
- Instead of hiding the band on a good day, draw it at AQI 42 with "Good", because a quiet reading is still a reading.
