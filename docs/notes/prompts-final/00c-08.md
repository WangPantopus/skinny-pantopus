# Foundations 00c-08 — TextActionRow
id: 00c-08 · clean: False · artboards: 6

Component 00c-08: TextActionRow. Follow the board rules from the setup message.

PUBLISH NAME: TextActionRow. This message is the complete TextActionRow specification.

PURPOSE (print at the top of 01). Tertiary actions that spread the product: share, compare and outbound links. They never outrank the primary action.

USED IN (panel): f8-native-share-compare · f8-compare-sheet · f8-seasonal-aha · f6-place-section-details · f8-compare-reveal.

ANATOMY (01-anatomy)
Draw V1 at 2x, and V2b at 2x below it.
① Two text buttons side by side: "Share this address" and "Compare with a friend". Each is bodySmallMedium primary.700 (dark: dark border.focus), with a 44pt / 48dp / 44px target, 8dp apart, no fill and no border. Draw both dashed targets and the 8dp gap.
② Caption under the buttons: caption 12/16 text.secondary, "The card shows your city and your readings — never your address, and no name."
③ Outbound variant: a bordered row, 48pt minimum, radius md 8, padding 12pt vertical and 16pt horizontal, with a 1px text.secondary edge (4.83:1 on base; a border token would fail as the only edge). It holds the destination text in bodySmall text.primary and a trailing 16pt external-link glyph "↗" in text.secondary. The whole row is the target. The "↗" at the end of each variant string below is this glyph: draw it once, as a vector, not as a text character.
④ Web menu callout: "no custom actions; no row menu button".

VARIANTS (rows; label each "<code> · <key>")
- V1 · share-compare: ① plus ②.
- V2a · outbound-test-kits: "Free test kits · Washington Dept of Health ↗".
- V2b · outbound-votewa: "Check or update at VoteWA ↗".

STATES (columns, in this order: S1, S2, S3, S4, S5, SF)
- S1 · idle.
- S2 · minting: on "Compare with a friend", a 12pt spinner (dark: D6) replaces the start of the label, which reads "Making a link…". Not re-tappable. Under Reduce Motion the spinner is removed and the words stay.
- S3 · copied: the caption "Link copied" (role=status) sits between the buttons and the ② caption, and stays until the user leaves the screen.
- S4 · cancelled: nothing changes. Draw the idle row again with the annotation "share sheet dismissed: neutral, no message".
- S5 · offline: "Compare with a friend" is disabled per R8, with the reason "You're offline. Compare needs a connection." "Share this address" stays enabled (the system share sheet works offline). Outbound rows are disabled: the destination text turns text.secondary (glyph unchanged), the edge stays, and "You're offline. You can open this when you're back." sits under the row.
- SF · focused: the ring 2px outside the V2b row.

GRID CELLS (02 and 05; 16 cells)
- V1: S1, S2, S3, S4, S5 (5).
- V2a and V2b: S1 and S5 (4).
- V2a and V2b in S2, S3 and S4: "n/a — outbound links open the browser" (6).
- V2b: SF (1).

DARK (05): rows on dark surface.base. Buttons use dark border.focus. The outbound edge is a 1px dark text.secondary keyline (6.96:1 on dark base). Spinners use D6. Add the Increase Contrast caption (R5).

AX5 / 200% (03): draw V1 and V2b in both columns. The two buttons stack, each full width with its own target. The caption wraps. The outbound row wraps its destination text; the glyph stays trailing, top-aligned.

GREYSCALE (03 captions): actions = verbs and position, not colour · outbound = text.secondary edge + destination name + ↗ glyph · minting = "Making a link…" words · copied = "Link copied" words · offline = reason captions.

ACCESSIBILITY (panel)
- Links name their destination: "Check or update at VoteWA, opens in browser".
- "Link copied" is a status message.
- Targets are 44pt / 48dp / 44px, with 8dp between the two buttons.
- The minting button is announced as "Making a link" and cannot be re-activated.
- Disabled offline controls stay in the tree with their reason as a hint.

COPY RULES (panel)
- The V1 buttons start with a verb. Outbound rows name the destination as the contract gives it.
- The caption states what the card reveals.
- Use "Compare with a friend", never "challenge" or "vs".
- Offline reasons open with "You're offline".

PLATFORMS (panel)
- Only the system share sheet is used. iOS supplies LPLinkMetadata; Android supplies EXTRA_TITLE to the system chooser; web uses the browser's share, falling back to copying the link (S3).
- System UI is never redrawn by us: in 04 it is a surface.sunken frame with a 1px text.secondary keyline, labelled "system UI" in caption text.secondary.

INSTEAD OF (panel)
- Instead of placing these actions inside the sticky WallBar, draw them on the page surface under the content card. The wall's single primary action must stay single.
- Instead of an app-drawn Android chooser, draw the system chooser frame.
- Instead of filled or bordered buttons for share and compare, draw text buttons, so they never outrank the primary.
