# Foundations 00b-04 — YearBand
id: 00b-04 · clean: False · artboards: 10

Component 00b-04: YearBand. Follow the board rules from the setup message.

PURPOSE (print it): Twelve months of dated rules in four source lanes. It shows the same data as FourteenDayStrip at a longer zoom.
USED IN (print it): x-place-file, f7-today-widget, f7-widget-gallery.
WEB-1440 CARD: 880px wide (848px of content).
TYPE (two styles this component adds to the house scale; use them only as named):
- captionMedium 12/16/500. iOS AX5 → 43, Android 200% → 24.
- widgetCaption 11/16/400, widgets only. At AX sizes the widget drops to its text fallback (05), so widgetCaption has no AX5 or 200% size.

ANATOMY (on 01-anatomy-phone, with a callout on every part)
- Scope: the 00a · ScopeChip "Your household" (HOME A), above the summary header.
- Summary header, in label 13/18 text.strong: "Next 12 months: 10 dates. Next, in 4 days: Clark Public Utilities bill due Fri 23 Oct." The pickup rhythm is not counted as dates.
- Top line (16pt): "Today" in caption text.primary above the today rule, and "2027" above "Jan".
- Month columns: 12, current month first, in caption text.secondary: "Oct Nov Dec Jan Feb Mar Apr May Jun Jul Aug Sep". Phone: 326pt, so each column is 27.2pt.
- Lanes, in order: "You" · "Your city" · "Your county" · "Your state".
  - Phone stack per lane: a 16pt label line (captionMedium text.strong) · a 24pt lane · a 4pt gap. At 768px and wider, the labels sit in a 128px left column.
  - Lane track: surface.base fill, a 1px text.secondary keyline, radius xs 4, and 1px text.secondary month separators.
  - Paint order in each lane: lane fill · window wash · keyline and separators · rhythm ticks · today rule · marks, bars and numerals.
  - Marks, bars and numerals carry a 1pt halo that matches the fill beneath them, so the today rule stops short of them. On phones the rule (≈16.7pt) would otherwise cut across the Oct You tick (7.6–19.6pt). Callout this.
- Marks on phones: one group per month per lane.
  - Each group is an XS 12pt ProvenanceMark centred in the column. When the group has 2 or more items, add a count numeral in captionMedium text.primary, 1pt to the right of the mark.
  - A mixed-provenance group shows the hollow mark, and the rows list each item.
- Rules: county and state rules are solid 4pt bars, full lane height, in text.strong (the same rule as FourteenDayStrip). On phones a bar is centred in its month column, with a numeral when there are 2 or more.
- Collision rule (768px and wider only; exact-day placement happens only there):
  - Use drawn extents: a mark is 12px wide and a bar is 4px wide. Once a cluster holds 2 or more items, its numeral adds 1px plus the numeral's width (about 7px for one digit in captionMedium).
  - Work left to right within each lane. An item joins the current cluster if its left edge is less than 2px from the cluster's right edge (its mark or bar, plus the numeral once there is one). Otherwise the item starts a new cluster.
  - A cluster is drawn as one item at its earliest day, with its count numeral 1px to the right. Mixed provenance shows the hollow mark.
  - Rhythm ticks are not items and never merge.
  - Item centres: x = 128 + 60 × (month index + (day − 0.5) ÷ days in month), where Oct is month index 0.
- Today rule: 1.5px text.primary through all four lanes at 19 Oct (19/31 into Oct).
- Shaded 14-day window: 19 Oct–1 Nov across all lanes.
  - Wash: light surface.sunken; dark raised #1E293B (dark lanes are base #0F172A, so a sunken wash would vanish).
  - The wash carries the window, and the legend line under the band names it: "Shaded: next 14 days".
  - A 1px dashed text.secondary edge marks the end of 1 Nov. On phones that edge sits about 1.8pt past the Oct/Nov separator, so it also rises 4pt above the top lane as a tick, to tell it apart.
- Pickup rhythm, "Your city" lane:
  - Label line: "Your city", an XS hollow mark, and the caption "Pickup every Tuesday · city schedule" in caption text.secondary. At 768px and wider these sit in the label column under "Your city".
  - Ticks: 1.5 × 8pt in text.secondary, one per service week, centred vertically.
  - After the last published city calendar (Dec 2026), the Jan–Sep keyline turns dashed (3 on, 2 off). The caption "Projected after Dec 2026 (last published city calendar)" sits on its own 16pt caption line directly under the Your city lane.
  - Holiday moves on phones: that week's tick is 11pt tall with a 1.5px strike.
  - Holiday moves at 768px and wider: if the moved day is at least 14px from the usual day, the tick moves to the real day and a struck ghost (same shape, full ink) sits on the usual day. Otherwise use the phone form.
- The band reserves its full height while loading.
- Caption under the band, in text.secondary: "The same dates are listed below." Then come 00c · DateRow and 00c · FactRow rows, which are the table.
- Fixture data, HOME A:
  - You (all ticks): Oct "2" (Clark Public Utilities bill Fri 23 Oct; City of Vancouver water bill Wed 28 Oct) · Nov "2" (HOA dues Sun 1 Nov; Comcast bill Mon 2 Nov) · Dec (water-heater warranty ends Sat 12 Dec) · Mar "2" (lease notice deadline Mon 1 Mar; lease ends Wed 31 Mar).
  - Your city: the weekly Tuesday rhythm, on record, not confirmed. There is no holiday move in the published window.
  - Your county: a bar in Nov (property tax 2nd half due Mon 2 Nov; the row may add "moved from Sat 31 Oct").
  - Your state: bars in Oct (online or mail voter registration must arrive by Mon 26 Oct) and Nov (in person until 8:00 PM Tue 3 Nov).
  - Total: 10 dates (7 + 1 + 2).

ACCESSIBILITY
- The summary sentence is the label. The rows are the table, and the caption says so.
- Native: one element with adjustable month cells (readouts on 01).
- Web: one image, with the summary as its text alternative.
- Marks are never targets. Provenance is shown by shape, rules by bar.

COPY: Lane labels are exactly "You · Your city · Your county · Your state"; widgets use place names. Deadlines say "must arrive by". Dates show the weekday, and summaries and widget lines add the relative count beyond tomorrow.

PLATFORMS: Phones group items by month; at 768px and wider, items sit on exact days. Everything else is identical.

INSTEAD OF (contract doNot, plus the bar rule)
- No heatmap: marks with count numerals in labelled lanes, because intensity hides what the dates are and who set them.
- No collapsing the lanes: the source of a rule matters as much as its date.
- No day-accurate dots on phones: one group per month with a count, because 27pt columns cannot place days.
- No dot for a county or state rule: a full-lane bar, because the strip and the band must encode the same data the same way.
