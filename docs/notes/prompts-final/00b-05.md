ANATOMY (on 01-anatomy, with a callout on every part)
- Heading "Founding Neighbor" in bodyMedium text.primary.
- Segments: five bounded capsules, each 40 × 12pt, radius pill, with 4pt gaps (216pt in total). Taken segments come first, from the left.
  - Taken: solid primary.600 fill with a 1.5px primary.700 edge (#38BDF8 dark, per C6).
  - Open: surface.base fill with a 45° diagonal hatch (1.5px lines every 4pt) in text.secondary, and a 1.5px text.secondary edge.
  - Fixture: 2 taken, 3 open.
- Numeral label, 8pt right of the segments, in label 13/18 text.primary: "3 of 5 open". This count of open slots is the one sanctioned "N of M" here. It counts what is left and never measures progress.
- Elapsed bar, 8pt below the segments: 4 × 216pt, radius pill.
  - The elapsed part is solid text.secondary. The remaining part is an unfilled 1px text.secondary outline (never a border token).
  - The window opened Mon 5 Oct and closes Tue 27 Oct. TODAY is Mon 19 Oct, so the elapsed part is 138pt. Callout: "elapsed 138pt of 216pt; decorative".
  - The bar is decorative and hidden from assistive tech; the date pill carries the meaning.
- Date pill, 8pt below the bar: a neutral 24pt pill with a surface.sunken fill, holding a 12pt calendar glyph and "Closes Tue 27 Oct" in label 13/18, both in text.strong.
- RankBadge (part of this specimen, published only with SlotMeter). It appears only in V2 (HOME A), because a Block Founder rank cannot exist on an unclaimed place.
  - It always sits below the meter, at least 24pt away, never in the same row or sentence as the tier.
  - It is a separate squared shape: 128 × 64pt, radius md 8, surface.sunken fill, a 1px text.secondary edge, 12pt horizontal padding (104pt of text width) and 8pt vertical padding.
  - It holds the overline "BLOCK FOUNDER" in overline 11/16/600 caps +0.5 text.strong, then "#3" in h2 24/32/600 text.primary.
  - At default size the overline sits on one line (it measures about 91–100pt, inside 104pt). It breaks as "BLOCK / FOUNDER" only at AX sizes, where the badge grows vertically.
- R6 contrast table on 01: primary.600 fill on base, primary.700 on base, text.secondary on base, text.strong on sunken.

ACCESSIBILITY
- The meter is one element. Its spoken label, printed on 01: "Founding Neighbor slots: 3 of 5 open, closes Tuesday 27 October."
- The RankBadge reads "Block Founder number 3".
- Taken vs open is shown by solid vs hatched, plus the numeral.
- The meter is not a target. Any card action is 44pt / 48dp / 44px.
- Absent states are silent: nothing is announced.

COPY
- Never write "permanent" unless the data model guarantees it.
- Rank ("Block Founder #3") and tier ("Founding Neighbor") never share a sentence.
- Closing dates are dates, never "closes in 6 days" or hh:mm.

PLATFORMS: identical on iOS, Android and web; only the target minimums differ.

INSTEAD OF
- Instead of hollow segments for open slots, draw hatched segments with an ink edge, because hollow means "on record, not confirmed".
- Instead of a warning-tinted pill, draw a neutral surface.sunken pill, because a badge deadline is not an alarm.
- Instead of an hh:mm or "closes in 6 days" countdown, print "Closes Tue 27 Oct", because live countdowns manufacture urgency.
- Instead of a border-token fill for the remaining time, draw a text.secondary outline, because border tokens are never the only edge of a segment.
- Instead of a 2px elapsed bar, draw a 4pt bar, because a 1px outline cannot be drawn around a 2px shape.
- Instead of an empty meter at 0 open, or a meter after a failed lookup, render nothing, because the meter must never promise a slot the system cannot honour.
- Instead of a disc or ring for the rank, draw the squared RankBadge, because discs and rings mean provenance.
