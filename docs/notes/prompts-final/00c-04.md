ACCESSIBILITY (panel)

COPY RULES (panel): notifications say "marked paid", not "paid" · "Paid by <first name> · <weekday day month>" · future dates beyond tomorrow add the relative count; past dates show the date only ("Due Thu 15 Oct", "Paid by Sam · Thu 1 Oct") · former member "Sam (no longer here)" · the header names who can see · never monitoring language ("still hasn't paid").

PLATFORMS (panel; added by this board): the overflow is native: iOS pull-down menu, Android M3 DropdownMenu, web menu button with keyboard support.

INSTEAD OF (panel)
- One-tap "Mark paid" on the row or dashboard → in the overflow; a mis-tap must not notify the household.
- Avatar without a name → "Paid by Sam"; the name is the point.
- Budget meter → the header total.
- Truncated attribution → truncate the provider.
