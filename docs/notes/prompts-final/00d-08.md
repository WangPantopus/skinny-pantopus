PASSED-LEAD RULE (stated once here; everything below refers to it)
TODAY is Mon 19 Oct 2026. A lead whose date is before TODAY has passed. A passed lead gets exactly one of two treatments:
- Passed (never chosen): disabled per R16. Label in text.muted (disabled only), no check, still focusable, read as "<N> days, unavailable, <N> days before was <date>, which has passed." Its reason is also written in readable text: the passed-lead line, 8pt below the control and above the timeline, in bodySmall 14/20 text.secondary (R10: 4.83:1; dark #94A3B8, R10: 6.96:1). It names only never-chosen passed leads: "60 days has passed for this date." (V1) or "60 and 30 days have passed for this date." (V2). With no such lead, there is no line. Activating a passed segment shows the explanation line and suggestion instead of selecting it.
- Chosen-then-passed (the lead was selected, then the date was edited so it has now passed): a primary.50 fill, a 1.5px primary.700 border, NO check, the label in bodySmall 14/20 at weight 400 in text.primary, and the word "passed" under the label in caption 12/16 text.primary; both lines fit inside the 44pt segment. Dark: a primary.900 fill, a 1.5px #38BDF8 border, no check, label and word in dark text primary. Read as "<N> days, selected, passed. <N> days before was <date>, which has passed." It keeps this treatment, with the explanation line and suggestion under the control, until the person picks another lead. This is the one extra treatment R12 allows.
The suggestion is always the longest lead still ahead. Nothing is saved until the person taps the suggestion or picks a live segment. The timeline never draws a tick for a passed lead.

ACCESSIBILITY
- Spoken: "Remind me, 30 days before, selected, 2 of 4." The segment's accessibility label intentionally expands "30 days" to "30 days before". Passed and chosen-then-passed segments read as the rule says; the passed-lead line is plain text read in order after the control. The timeline is one element with a summary: V1 "Reminders on Thu 12 Nov, Fri 11 Dec and Sat 12 Dec." · V1 one day "Reminders on Fri 11 Dec and Sat 12 Dec." · V2 "Reminders on Wed 21 Oct, Tue 27 Oct and Wed 28 Oct." · V3 "Reminders on Sat 30 Jan, Sun 28 Feb and Mon 1 Mar." The same facts appear as a list at AX sizes. The S2 explanation line is announced politely when it appears.
- Non-colour encoding: selection is a text label plus a fill change plus a check, never colour alone.
- Targets: segments (disabled ones included) are 44pt on iOS, 48dp on Android and 44px on web. The suggestion button is 44pt. Ticks are not targets.

COPY: all strings are given in the rule, ANATOMY, VARIANTS, STATES and AX rows; use them exactly. Dates always show the weekday.

PLATFORMS: the same on all platforms apart from target sizes and the large-text layouts above. On web-1440, the control sits in the date sheet's 480px side panel (04 extra row).

INSTEAD OF
- Instead of a single "Remind me 30 days before" row, draw all three reminders with their real dates — because three will fire, and the person should see each one.
- Instead of anchoring the lease reminder to Wed 31 Mar 2027, anchor it to the notice deadline Mon 1 Mar 2027 — because the notice is what the person must act on.
- Instead of silently accepting, hiding or greying a passed lead, apply the PASSED-LEAD RULE, with its written passed-lead line — because a reminder dated in the past never fires, and silent dead controls leave people guessing.
- Instead of keeping the check on a lead that passed after a date edit, draw the chosen-then-passed segment — because a check says a reminder will fire.
- Instead of radio circles at large text, draw selectable rows with a check — because filled vs hollow circles mean provenance.
- Instead of showing selection by colour alone, draw fill + check + weight — because colour alone fails in greyscale and for colour-blind people.
- Instead of a timeline drawn to scale, draw fixed tick positions with a "not to scale" break — because a to-scale 60-day line squashes the last two ticks together.
