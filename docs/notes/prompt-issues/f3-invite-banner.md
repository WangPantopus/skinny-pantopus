# Last review findings for f3-invite-banner (round 2). The fix after this round was applied but not re-verified.

- [major] (substance) The rule that a banner is never inserted after first paint defeats the surface's one job and contradicts both its own acceptance criterion and the LandingBannerSlot spec. On a cold start, where the invitations call often returns after first paint, a brand-new invitee sees no invitation until the next load or a pull to refresh. The board instead lets the arriving banner move content down once, in 300ms or less.
  - evidence: Prompt: "It is never inserted after first paint." and "If the invitations call has not returned by first paint, the slot shows the next banner by precedence (or nothing), and the invitation appears on the next load or pull to refresh. Content never moves down." vs DONE WHEN "A person with no home sees their invitation on first landing, without opening any homes list." prompts-v3/00d-05.md anatomy 8(iii): "banner arrives: content below moves down once, 300 ms or less (Reduce Motion: no animation)" and Notes 5.7 "Only a pending, failed or empty fetch is guaranteed not to shift layout."
  - fix: Adopt the board rule: while the fetch is pending the slot is 0pt; when an invite or reissue banner arrives it replaces any lower banner and moves content down once (300ms or less, no animation under Reduce Motion); a failed or empty fetch never shifts anything. Update frames 6 and 7 and the Motion bullet to match.
- [major] (substance) The new durable 'Invitations waiting for you' list has no loading, error or offline states. The source proposal requires an inline retry that must not take down the rest of the page. Without one, a push that lands on a dead row, or a 'Review' tap from the count banner, can reach an empty or broken list.
  - evidence: derive-proposals.md f3-received-invitations states: "loading · none (section absent — never an empty box) · rows · expired or revoked invite row … · error: the invitations fetch fails — inline retry that must not take down the rest of My Homes · offline". Prompt defines only "No invitations: the section is absent", a fetch-failure rule for banners only, and an offline state for the banner ("cached banner, Review disabled").
  - fix: Add list states: a row-skeleton WarmingSkeleton; an InlineErrorRow such as "We couldn't load your invitations. · Retry" used when the list is reached from a push or from Review (with no error on a plain landing); and an offline list with each Review disabled and a reason. Add one list error/offline artboard and list the strings on Notes.
