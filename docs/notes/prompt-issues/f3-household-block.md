# Last review findings for f3-household-block (round 2). The fix after this round was applied but not re-verified.

- [major] (substance) The notification ask understates what it turns on. "Turn on" switches on the whole Household activity group, but the ask only offers join news and promises it is all quiet. The brief puts joins, leaves, bill_paid and calendar edits in that group, and bill_paid is Active (it makes a sound) for some members. So "Hear when they join? Quiet notifications, no sound." is not true for the thing being switched on.
  - evidence: ux-research-brief.md §3: "Household activity | Joins, leaves, bill_paid, calendar edits | Passive (bill_paid Active only for members who still see the bill as unpaid and due within 3 days)". component-contract.md NotificationAsk copyRules: "Body lists the real defaults". Prompt: "'Hear when they join? Quiet notifications, no sound.' ... It turns on Household activity".
  - fix: Make the ask list what the group actually sends, for example: "Hear when someone joins, marks a bill paid or adds to the calendar? Mostly quiet, no sound." Or narrow what "Turn on" enables to join and leave notices only, and say so on Notes. Either way, drop the flat "no sound" promise unless bill_paid is excluded.
- [major] (substance) The "You'll get" preview breaks the push rules and doesn't match the canonical join row. It puts a raw email address in a 43-character line, over the 30-character title limit, and that address could show on a lock screen. It also leaves out the canonical wording "— can see the calendar and bills".
  - evidence: house-style-v2.txt PUSH: "Titles are 30 characters or fewer". per-surface-changes/f3-household-notifications.md: "Add quiet rows: 'Tova joined Larkspur Loop — can see the calendar and bills'". Prompt: "the caption \"You'll get: 'sam.ortega@example.com joined Larkspur Loop'\"".
  - fix: Show the canonical tray preview: a title of 30 characters or fewer, like "Sam joined Larkspur Loop", with the in-app row "Sam joined Larkspur Loop — can see the calendar and bills". While the name is unknown, use a generic title such as "Someone joined Larkspur Loop" rather than the email address. Add the string to Notes.
- [minor] (substance) The prompt never says when Maya's address was verified. Frame 01 (Mon 5 Oct) shows her the verify banner. Foundations says her address is already confirmed by TODAY, and the member dashboard offers "Ask Maya Chen to confirm" on TODAY. It is unclear whether frames 09 and 16–17 show the banner.
  - evidence: prompts-v3/00c-09.md V4: "Maya's address is already confirmed". Prompt frame 01: "with the verify banner above". Frame 09 is Maya on TODAY, and the banner is not mentioned.
  - fix: Add a fixture delta: Maya was not address-verified on Mon 5 Oct and was confirmed by postcard before TODAY. State that frames 09, 16 and 17 show no banner, or state the opposite.
- [minor] (substance) The NotificationAsk row pairs "Turn on" with "Not now" but has no lasting decline, which the glossary requires.
  - evidence: component-contract.md glossary: "Not now — a deferral that never re-asks on its own. Always paired with a permanent decline (Just me / Skip / No thanks)."
  - fix: Either add "No thanks" beside "Not now", or say that this row's "Not now" closes the slot for good and the setting stays available in Notifications settings.
- [minor] (substance) The derive proposal's densest content for this card includes the home nickname, but the card never names the home.
  - evidence: derive-proposals.md f3-who-lives-here-card: "densest realistic content = home nickname + privacy sentence + 2 buttons + dismiss".
  - fix: Either state that the People section header already names Larkspur Loop and put that on Notes, or add the home label to the worst-case frame.
