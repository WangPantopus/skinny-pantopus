USED IN (print): f1-email-verify-handoff, f1-save-confirmation, f1-add-place-sheet, f8-compare-arrival-header.

ANATOMY (callouts on the held chip for Place B)
① Pill, radius pill, fill surface.raised, with a 1px border.default edge as decoration only (1.24:1 on the base card, 1.18:1 on the chip fill). Surface.raised on base is about 1.05:1, so on a base card this edge is in practice the chip's only visible outline; the pin and text carry the chip, as in ⑨. Meaning never depends on the edge.
② 16pt pin glyph in text.strong, 8pt gap. Its round hole is a sub-part (B8).
③ Street "1107 NE Birchfield Ct": bodySmallMedium 14/20/500, text.primary.
④ City "Camas, WA 98607" on the line below: caption 12/16, text.secondary (4.63:1 on raised). On a selected fill the city turns text.primary (see R1 selected in send 2).
⑤ Padding 12 horizontal, 8 vertical; minimum height 44pt.
⑥ At 390 wide, street and city each wrap and are never truncated.
⑦ Optional "Save it", only when the chip is a selectable suggestion row: a text button in label 13/18/600 primary.700, with a target (B13).
⑧ SCOPE SENTENCE RULE (defined only here; every other mention points to ⑧): "Only you will see this." sits 8pt below every held, recovery and duplicate chip. The sender label (R4) is the one exception: it describes someone else's place, so it carries no sentence, and the sentence goes under the recipient's own address field instead. Print "no scope sentence: not your place" on the exception.
⑨ DARK EDGE RULE: fill dark surface.raised. Dark border.default on dark raised is 1.00:1, so dark mode has no border.default edge; the pin and text carry the chip. The selected state keeps its 1.5px primary.400 (dark link) edge. Draw a light and a dark default chip side by side, labelled "dark: no border". The light chip sits on a surface.base card; the dark chip sits on its own dark surface.base card (radius lg 12), never directly on the light artboard.

ACCESSIBILITY (print)
- Reads the full address: "1107 Northeast Birchfield Court, Camas, Washington 98607". Selected: "…, selected". Sender: "Dana, Camas, Washington". Expired: "1107 Northeast Birchfield Court, Camas, no longer held, Search again, button".
- Non-colour: selected is the check plus the edge; expired is stated in words.
- The chip itself is a label. Its actions ("Save it", "Use for Today", "Search again") are targets (B13).

PLATFORMS (print): no platform differences. Draw the same chip on all three platforms.

INSTEAD OF (print)
- A blank retype field when the server holds the address → the held AddressChip, because retyping loses people between steps.
- A map or coordinates → the street and city as text, because a label is enough and a map leaks location.
- An alarm colour for an expired hold → a neutral sunken chip with "Search again", because expiry is not an error.
- A held or selectable chip without its scope sentence → add it (⑧), because a held address must say who sees it.
- The sentence under a sender's chip → under the recipient's own field (⑧), because it would be false about the sender's place.
- A text.secondary city on a selected fill → text.primary (dark: dark text.primary), because dark text.secondary on primary.900 is 3.69.
