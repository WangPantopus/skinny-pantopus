ANATOMY (01-anatomy)

① Row: minimum 64pt on surface.base; padding 12pt vertical, 16pt leading (before the dot column) and 16pt trailing. The whole row is the target: draw its dashed target.
② A fixed 12pt leading column holding a 6pt unread dot in primary.700 (dark: D6), labelled "Unread". Read rows keep the empty column, so text never shifts. The dot is not a provenance mark and not a target.
③ A 28pt initials avatar: surface.sunken circle, initials in label 13/18 text.strong (dark: D4). System rows have no avatar; their text keeps the same leading alignment.
④ Line 1: body 16/24 text.primary, a sentence with the actor first.
⑤ Line 2: caption 12/16 text.secondary, joined by middots: date and time · home label (only for readers with more than one home) · for reminders, a trailing ProvenanceMark S.
⑥ Grouped row: a chevron disclosure that expands to the individual rows. The chevron is part of the row target. Expanded rows have no avatar, are indented to the text column, and each has line 2 "Today · 5:40 PM".
⑦ Web menu callout: "no custom actions; no row menu button".

COPY RULES (panel)
- Amounts appear in-app only, never in pushes.
- Use "marked paid", not "paid". The actor comes first.
- Dates show the weekday; beyond tomorrow the relative count comes first: "in 7 days · Mon 26 Oct".
- A landing whose object is gone says "That bill was removed."
