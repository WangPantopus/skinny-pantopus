ANATOMY (01-anatomy)
Draw the three sizes side by side at 2x, then V1 in S2, S3, S4 and SX at 2x below them, then V5 with the rail collapsed.
① Thumbnail sizes, all radius md 8: triage 56×56pt · drawer 40×52pt · privacy list 32×32pt. The thumbnail sits 12pt (spacing.3) before the row text.
② Photo content: a neutral envelope on a grey table, with no legible name, address or amount.
③ No photo: the rail collapses. The text moves to the leading margin, with no empty square.
④ Uploading: a determinate ring inside the thumbnail, 2pt stroke, primary.700 on a border.strong track (4.03:1; dark: D6), plus the text "Uploading 2 of 3" beside the row in caption text.secondary. This is transfer progress, not a score, and it never carries a percentage.
⑤ Failed: a 16pt retry glyph in text.strong, centred on a surface.sunken thumbnail (dark: D4), plus the row caption "Upload failed · Retry". That caption is InlineErrorRow's upload-failed variant, drawn as specified in the previous message.
⑥ Deleted: a quiet 16pt document glyph in text.secondary on surface.sunken (dark: D4), plus the words "photo deleted Mon 12 Oct" in caption text.secondary.
⑦ Selected: a primary.50 fill behind the row plus a leading check glyph (dark: D5). Below it, a visible action row of three text buttons, each with a 44pt / 48dp target, 8dp apart: "Retake" · "Delete" · "Same letter as previous". Draw their dashed targets. The progress ring never sits under or next to the Delete target.
⑧ Web menu callout: "the visible action row holds the actions; no row menu button".

ACCESSIBILITY (panel)

- No action is long-press-only or hover-only.
- Upload progress is a polite status (live region).
- Targets are 44pt / 48dp / 44px with 8dp gaps.

COPY RULES (panel)

- Upload progress reads "Uploading 2 of 3", with no percentage.

PLATFORMS (panel): web converts HEIC to JPEG before rendering; add a callout on the web crop. iOS and Android render natively.

INSTEAD OF (panel)
- Instead of a broken-image icon, draw the ⑥ deleted glyph with words, or the ⑤ retry glyph.
- Instead of placing the progress ring under or near the Delete target, keep the ring inside the thumbnail and the action row separate, so a tap meant for Delete never lands on progress.
- Instead of an empty square when there is no photo, collapse the rail.
- Instead of a percentage beside the ring, draw "Uploading 2 of 3". Percentages are banned by house invariant 6.
