# Foundations 00c-06 — InviteRow
id: 00c-06 · clean: False · artboards: 6

Component 00c-06: InviteRow. Follow the board rules from the setup message.

PUBLISH NAME: InviteRow. This message is the complete InviteRow specification.

PURPOSE (print at the top of 01). A pending invitation or invite link, with its expiry and a one-tap fix.

USED IN (panel): f3-members-roster · x-place-file · f3-invite-composer · f3-invite-banner.

ANATOMY (01-anatomy)
Draw V1 at 2x under its "PENDING" overline.
① Row: minimum 56pt, in a "PENDING" group (overline 11/16 text.secondary) that only managers see. Quieter than people rows: no avatar on any variant.
② Leading mail glyph, 16pt, text.secondary.
③ Line 1, the recipient: bodySmall 14/20 text.primary, "priya@example.com" or a username such as "@priya".
④ Line 2, the caption: caption 12/16 text.secondary, "Member · sent Mon 12 Oct · Expires in 7 days · Mon 26 Oct". It runs the full text width under ⑤ and ⑥ and wraps at a middot when it has to.
⑤ "Resend" text button, bodySmallMedium primary.700 (dark: dark border.focus), trailing and aligned to line 1, with a 44pt target. Draw its dashed target.
⑥ Overflow button (44pt / 48dp) holding Cancel · Extend, trailing, 8dp after Resend. Draw its dashed target.
⑦ Web menu callout: "the ⑥ overflow is the web row menu button; rows without an overflow use their visible buttons; no second menu".

VARIANTS (rows; label each "<code> · <key>")
- V1 · pending-email: as in the anatomy.
- V1u · pending-username: V1 with the recipient "@priya".
- V2 · expiring (fixture delta: shown on Sun 25 Oct): V1 with the caption "Member · sent Mon 12 Oct · Expires tomorrow · Mon 26 Oct". The expiry is words in text.secondary, not tinted.
- V3 · needs-reissue: a 16pt warning glyph replaces the mail glyph (hue on the glyph only; dark: D7). Line 1: the recipient, with ⑥ trailing. Line 2: "Needs reissue — your household's invite rules changed" in bodySmall text.primary, wrapping within the text width. Line 3: the caption "Expires in 7 days · Mon 26 Oct" leading, and the "Reissue" text button trailing with a 44pt target.
- V4 · declined (fixture delta: Priya declined): the 16pt mail glyph; line 1 the recipient; line 2 a StatusChip "Declined · Thu 15 Oct". ⑥ holds only "Remove". No Resend.
- V5 · link-active: a 16pt link glyph; line 1 "Invite link" in bodySmall text.primary; line 2 the caption "Works once · Expires in 7 days · Mon 26 Oct". "Turn off link" is a trailing text button with a 44pt target. No overflow.
- V6 · awaiting-approval (Priya joined by link, so the link is used and no expiry applies): the 16pt mail glyph; line 1 "priya@example.com"; line 2 the caption "Waiting for your OK". "Approve" and "Remove" are equal-weight text buttons, trailing, 8dp apart, each with its own 44pt target. No overflow.

  - Follow-on (V1 only): inside the V1·S2 cell, under the sending row, draw a second stacked frame sub-labelled "then": the caption gains " · Sent" at the end and the button returns to "Resend". "Sent" is a role=status region whose accessible text is "Invitation resent". It stays one cell.

The ⑥ overflow stays enabled and opens; Cancel and Extend inside it are disabled with "You're offline. You can change this invite when you're back." On V4, Remove inside it uses the remove reason.

GREYSCALE (03 captions): expiring = "Expires tomorrow · Mon 26 Oct" words · needs reissue = warning glyph shape + "Needs reissue" · declined = chip word "Declined" · link = link glyph + "Works once" · sending = "Sending…" words.

ACCESSIBILITY (panel)
- Pattern: "Invitation to priya@example.com, member, expires in 7 days, Monday 26 October. Resend. Button."
- A successful resend is announced as "Invitation resent" (the visible word is "Sent").
- Expiry is always in words. Targets are 44pt / 48dp / 44px with 8dp gaps.
- Overflow items are also custom actions. Disabled offline actions stay in the tree with their reason as a hint.

COPY RULES (panel)
- The expiry is shown on every pending invite (including expiring and needs-reissue rows) and on every active link. The awaiting-approval row shows none, because its link is already used.
- Dates show the weekday; beyond tomorrow, the relative count comes first: "in 7 days · Mon 26 Oct".
- The link warning ("Works once") appears at the point of choice.
- Every email uses @example.com. Each offline reason names the action it blocks.

PLATFORMS (panel): No platform differences.

INSTEAD OF (panel)
- Instead of a "Remind them" action on a declined invite, draw only "Remove". A decline is an answer.
- Instead of a real email domain, draw @example.com.
- Instead of a warning-tinted expiring row, draw the expiry in text.secondary words. Urgency comes from the date, not colour.
