Component 00b-03: FourteenDayStrip. Follow the board rules from the setup message.

PURPOSE (print it): The next two weeks at this address, drawn as a shape. The strip sits above list rows, and those rows are the real tap targets.
USED IN (print it): f5-today-calendar-strip, f7-today-widget, f7-widget-gallery, f7-widget-howto-sheet, f7-widget-tap-landing, f1-today-tab, x-place-file.
WEB-1440 CARD: 648px wide (616px of content), so each cell is 44px.
TYPE (two styles this component adds to the house scale; use them only as named):
- captionMedium 12/16/500. iOS AX5 → 43, Android 200% → 24.
- widgetCaption 11/16/400, widgets only. At AX sizes the widget drops to its text fallback (05), so widgetCaption has no AX5 or 200% size.

ANATOMY (on 01-anatomy, with a callout on every part)
- Header: the 00a · ScopeChip "Your household" (HOME A), then the summary label "Next 14 days: 6 items" in label 13/18 text.strong. The summary is the strip's one-sentence summary.
- Strip: 14 equal cells across the content width, today on the left. At 326pt the pitch is 23.3pt.
  - One track: radius sm 6, a 1px text.secondary keyline, and 1px text.secondary separators between cells.
  - Cell height 76pt, top to bottom: 6pt padding · up to 3 XS (12pt) ProvenanceMarks stacked with 4pt gaps (44pt) · a 4pt gap · a 16pt line reserved for "+N" · 6pt padding.
  - "+N" appears when a cell has 4 or more mark items (3 marks, then "+N"). It is in caption 12/16 text.strong (#F1F5F9 dark), because text.secondary on sunken is only 4.39:1. Otherwise the reserved line stays empty.
  - All marks use one ink, text.secondary at full opacity, centred in the cell. Marks never dim, in any state (C11).
- Cell fill: weekdays surface.sunken at 100%, weekends surface.sunken at 40%. Opacity marks the weekend, never hue, and the initials also name it (or "Sat–Sun" where there are no initials).
- Civic, state or county rule: a solid 4pt bar, full cell height, in text.strong. The bar is centred if the cell has no other marks. Otherwise it sits 2pt from the cell's left edge and the marks shift 3pt right. A bar never counts toward the 3-mark limit or "+N". A rule is never a dot. YearBand uses the same bar rule.
- Holiday move: the mark sits on the moved day. The usual day gets a struck ghost: the same shape at full text.secondary ink, crossed by a 1.5px diagonal strike in text.primary (#E5E7EB dark). The strike carries the meaning, and the DateRow names the move in words.
- Labels under the strip:
  - Row 1: weekday initials under every cell, in caption text.secondary: "M T W T F S S M T W T F S S". Draw them only where the pitch is 20pt or more. They are never spoken.
  - Row 2: anchors in captionMedium text.strong: "Today" left-aligned under cell 1 and "Mon 26 Oct" left-aligned under cell 8.
- Below the strip: one 00c · DateRow per item. The foot is a persistent 48pt row "+ Add a date", with the glyph and label in primary.700 (#38BDF8 dark).
- Fixture window, HOME A, Mon 19 Oct–Sun 1 Nov:

  | Cell | Day | Item (DateRow title) | Mark |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | 2 | Tue 20 Oct | Recycling and garbage | hollow (city schedule, not confirmed) |
  | 5 | Fri 23 Oct | Clark Public Utilities bill | tick |
  | 8 | Mon 26 Oct | Voter registration, online or mail, must arrive by | state bar |
  | 9 | Tue 27 Oct | Garbage only | hollow |
  | 10 | Wed 28 Oct | City of Vancouver water bill | tick |
  | 14 | Sun 1 Nov | HOA dues | tick, weekend cell |

  DateRow line 2 dates (R11): "Tomorrow"; "in 4 days · Fri 23 Oct"; "in 7 days · Mon 26 Oct"; "in 8 days · Tue 27 Oct"; "in 9 days · Wed 28 Oct"; "in 13 days · Sun 1 Nov".
- Two extras for 01. Each is a separate specimen outside the window, labelled "specimen" in the gutter:
  - Overflow cell [extra key overflow-cell], Mon 2 Nov: the county rule "Property tax 2nd half" as the bar 2pt from the left, plus four tick items (Comcast bill, "Swap furnace filter", "Library books due", "Drop off old paint"). Draw 3 marks shifted 3pt right, then "+1". Callout: "Bar = county rule, not counted in +N; 4 marks → 3 + '+1'; 6 + 44 + 4 + 16 + 6 = 76pt".
  - Holiday-move pair [extra key holiday-move], PLACE B: the Thanksgiving notice moves the usual Thu 26 Nov pickup to Fri 27 Nov. Draw a hollow mark on Fri 27 Nov and a struck ghost on Thu 26 Nov. DateRow: "Thanksgiving — moved to Fri 27 Nov · City of Camas 2026 calendar", line 2 "in 39 days · Fri 27 Nov".

INSTEAD OF (contract doNot)
- No class hue as the only cue: one ink, with the DateRow KindGlyph carrying the class.
- No 6pt dots with ticks: 12pt XS marks, the smallest size where the shapes stay distinct.
- No 14 tiny tap targets on phones: 23pt cells fail 44pt / 48dp (04).
- No statewide or county dot: a full-height bar, because a rule is not an item at this address.
- No even rhythm through a holiday week: the moved day plus a struck ghost.

NOTES (last batch: append a FourteenDayStrip part to each section of 00b · Notes)
- A. Invented strings: list every quoted string above that is not in the fixtures or the contract.
- B. Assumptions:
  - The cell is 76pt (6 + 44 + 4 + 16 + 6).
  - The widget cell is 60pt, with up to 2 marks, then "+N" (N = items − 2), within 100pt of the 158pt widget. The other 26pt is f7-today-widget's header line, which carries the label and the "As of" text.
  - The widget fill is base in light and raised in dark. The widget legend keys only the shapes drawn, and a bar is always keyed "Official rule".
  - PLACE B's frequency is Not set, so only Thu 22 Oct is drawn. The Thanksgiving move is drawn because the City of Camas notice covers every Thursday route.
  - The overflow cell is outside the window.
  - The strip counts pickups as items; YearBand does not.
  - The dark window wash is raised #1E293B, because dark sunken equals the dark lane fill.
  - In the window crop, the today rule paints under marks, bars and numerals, which carry a 1pt halo. The wash carries the window, and on phones the dashed edge also rises 4pt above the lanes.
  - captionMedium 12/16/500 and widgetCaption 11/16/400 are added to the type scale for this component.
- C. Normalisations: the contract's "Next 14 days: 9 items" → the fixture count "6 items". The empty line follows QuietDayReceipt ("next two weeks").
- D. Deviations: stale marks and holiday ghosts stay at full ink (C11); the contract's 50% opacity is not used.
- E. Open question for 00d: QuietDayReceipt uses a bare stroked check, while grammar rule 4 reserves the ticked disc for "You added this". Should the exemption stay (bare stroke, no disc, always followed by "Checked"), or should its CheckItems get a glyph that is not a check?
