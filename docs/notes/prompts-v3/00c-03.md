PUBLISH NAME: FirstWeekRow. This message is the complete FirstWeekRow specification.

PURPOSE (print at the top of 01). The single pointer on Today to the next first-week ask. It comes from a bounded set of steps that the person has already started.

USED IN (panel): f1-today-tab · x-place-file · f4-briefing-optin-card. (f4-briefing-optin-card is not cropped on 04: it only hosts the row, so it adds nothing beyond the f1-today-tab crop.)

FIXTURE DELTA (applies to every FirstWeekRow specimen). The specimens show Place B (Jordan Lee) on Tue 13 Oct 2026, 3 days after the Sat 10 Oct save. The 7-day activation window runs Sun 11 Oct through Sat 17 Oct and closes after Sat 17 Oct. The hidden variant (V2) shows Mon 19 Oct, when the window has closed. Place B's pickup day is known (Thursday, City of Camas); its pickup frequency is Not set.

ANATOMY (01-anatomy)
Draw V1a at 2x.
① One row, 48pt high at default sizes: padding 16pt horizontal and 12pt vertical (12 + 24 + 12 = 48), on surface.base. At default sizes the step text is one line; if it wraps, the row grows and the dismiss button stays top-aligned.
② Text in bodyMedium 16/24, text.primary: "Next: set your pickup day →". The arrow is part of the link. The whole row except the dismiss button is the link target: draw its dashed target.
③ Trailing dismiss button: a 16pt close glyph in text.secondary inside a 44pt / 48dp target, with 8dp between it and the link target. Draw its dashed target too.
④ Callout listing the bounded set: "Save (already done) · Pickup day · Reminder or widget · One date or person".
⑤ Callout: "Only steps reachable at the current tier are counted. Verify, profile and photo are never steps."
⑥ Callout: "Shown for 7 days (the activation window)."
⑦ Web menu callout: "the visible dismiss button is the only extra action; no row menu button".

VARIANTS (rows; label each "<code> · <key>")
- V1a · next-pickup: "Next: set your pickup day →". For Place B this step means confirming the known day and setting the frequency.
- V1b · next-widget: "Next: put today on your home screen →" (invented). It may wrap at 361pt; if it does, apply the ① rule.
- V2 · hidden (set complete, dismissed, or window closed): draw an outline of the Place B Today section stack (ScopeChip, pickup section, air section) with no FirstWeekRow and no gap where it would be, plus the annotation "hidden: set complete, dismissed, or 7 days passed".

STATES (columns, in this order: S1, SFl, SFd, S2)
- S1 · default.
- SFl · focused-link: the focus ring around the link target only.
- SFd · focused-dismiss: the focus ring around the dismiss button only.
- S2 · dismissed (stored on the server): the row collapses in place to the InlineUndo "Hidden from Today · Undo" (invented). Undo has a 44pt target and stays until the user leaves Today. There is no countdown.

ACCESSIBILITY (panel)
- The row is one link: "Next: set your pickup day. Link."
- Dismiss is a separate button, "Dismiss next step" (invented), and is also offered as a custom action on the link.
- Undo is announced with role=status and is focusable.
- Targets: the link row is 48pt; the dismiss button is 44pt / 48dp / 44px, 8dp from the link.

COPY RULES (panel)
- Name only the next step, in sentence case, verb first. Never show a count or fraction of steps.
- The row shows for 7 days, matching the activation window.
- Step strings come from the set: "set your pickup day", "put today on your home screen" (invented), "add one date that matters" (invented).

PLATFORMS (panel): No platform differences.

INSTEAD OF (panel)
- Instead of "3 of 6" or any denominator, draw "Next: <step> →". Naming the next step moves people without pressure.
- Instead of counting verify, profile or photo, draw only the four set steps reachable at the viewer's tier. A step you cannot do is not a step.
- Instead of a checklist card, draw this single 48pt row. The full list lives in the place file.
