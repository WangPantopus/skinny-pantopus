# Bill detail (new web route)
id: f3-bill-detail-web · platforms: web · isNew: True · artboards: 27

Use the Pantopus house style pasted above and the Foundations components (boards 00a–00d), by exact name.

SCREEN: Bill detail · f3-bill-detail-web

TYPE: NEW. Web only. Web has no page for a single bill today, so every money notification lands on a list. Match the attached Bills list and native bill detail. Use no new card style.

ATTACH: the web Bills list at 1440 and 390; the web Place tab with the left sidebar at 1440; the iOS bill detail screen (for content order only).

PLATFORMS & VIEWPORTS: web 1440x900 with the left sidebar (Place selected); web 390x844 with the four-tab bottom bar (Place selected). Draw no native frames; iOS and Android keep their own bill detail.

WHERE IT LIVES & HOW PEOPLE ARRIVE: Place tab › Larkspur Loop › Bills › one bill. URL /app/homes/{home}/bills/{bill}. Arrivals:
1. A single "marked paid" row in Notifications (/app/notifications). The page opens in the Paid state. A grouped "Maya marked 3 bills paid" row lands on the Bills list instead (/app/homes/{home}/bills?segment=paid&highlight={bill1},{bill2},{bill3}).
2. A bill reminder row, sent the day before and the day of.
3. A Bills list row (the whole row is the link). Back returns to the list, restores its scroll position and puts focus on that row, with no wash.
4. The Today 14-day strip money row (phase 2).
5. "Added to bills" on a Mail piece (phase 2).
Web gets no push. A phone push opens the native bill detail, and the in-app row is the web twin. A notification row for a bill that has since been removed opens this URL and shows this page's removed state (frame 17); browser Back returns to Notifications. What happens next: Mark paid → Undo stays open → Maya leaves the page → Sam gets one "marked paid" notification → Sam taps it and lands here in the Paid state.

WHO AND WHEN: Maya Chen, owner of HOME A, at her laptop on Mon 19 Oct at 6:10 PM. She just paid Clark Public Utilities on the utility's website and opened the bill from its row in Bills, so Sam doesn't pay it too. Maya is the only person at HOME A who can mark bills paid. Second viewer: Sam Ortega, a member who can see bills but cannot mark them paid. He opens the same bill from "Maya marked Clark Public Utilities paid". Jenna, a guest until Tue 20 Oct, cannot see bills.

THE ONE JOB: Give every bill notification, reminder and calendar row one exact page that shows the amount, the due date and who marked it paid.

INTERACTION, MOTION & HAPTICS
- Mark paid: the button row becomes the InlineUndo line "Marked paid · Undo" (role=status). The chip changes to "Paid". The attribution reads "Marked paid by you · Mon 19 Oct". "Next due Mon 23 Nov" appears. Under it: "No more reminders for October's bill, for anyone." Details Reminders changes to "Sun 22 Nov · Mon 23 Nov". A caption reads "We'll let Sam know when you leave this page." The paid status is saved at once, so Sam's list and bill detail show "Marked paid by Maya" straight away. Only the "marked paid" notification waits for Maya to leave the page; if she closes the tab, it is sent then. Undo stays until she leaves, restores everything and sends nothing. Leaving sends exactly one "marked paid" notification to each other person who can see bills (today, only Sam). Marking paid also cancels October's scheduled reminders for every member and replaces any reminder already delivered with the "marked paid" notification. November's reminders stay.
- Overdue bill: the primary still reads "Mark paid" and behaves exactly as above.
- Paid a different amount: an inline amount field pre-filled with $142.18, a button that restates the value ("Mark $120.00 paid") and a "Cancel" text button beside it. Cancel or Escape closes the field and changes nothing. After committing, the amount reads "$120.00" with the caption "Bill was $142.18" under it, the attribution reads "Marked paid by you · Mon 19 Oct", and InlineUndo reads "Marked $120.00 paid · Undo". Next due, the reminders line and the notice caption behave as for Mark paid.
- Skip this month: skips only October. InlineUndo "Skipped this month · Undo" plus "Next due Mon 23 Nov". It cancels this month's reminders and sends no household notice.
- Edit opens the existing bill edit form for this bill; its design is out of scope.
- Remove: no dialog. The page collapses to "Removed Clark Public Utilities · Undo" with the caption "This removes the monthly bill and its future months.", then "Sam won't see it anymore." and "Back to bills". For a bill made from a photo, the line reads "Removed Clark Public Utilities and its photo · Undo" (the InlineUndo bill + photo variant, owned by f10-bill-provenance). A removed bill also leaves the Bills list's Upcoming segment.
- Race: a second Mark paid on a bill already marked paid shows "Already marked paid by you on your phone · 6:02 PM" with the Paid chip, and nothing is sent twice. When a different person who can mark bills paid got there first, the line names them ("Already marked paid by <name> · 6:02 PM"). HOME A has no second such person, so draw only the phone case.
- Deep-link arrival: focus moves to the h1 and the headline block fades a highlight once (300ms). With Reduce Motion, show the highlight without the fade. Escape closes the overflow menu. Web has no haptics.

EDGE CASES: Wrap the longest provider name to two lines; the amount never truncates, and $3,412.56 fits the amount line at 390. The attribution line wraps and never truncates before the name ("Marked paid by Maya · Mon 19 Oct"). A Paid landing is reassurance, not an error: neutral chip, no error or success slab; it occurs only after a "marked paid" notification or in a race. No reminder for a paid month can reach anyone. A member (Sam) sees no Mark paid, Skip, Edit or Remove, only LockedActionRow "Maya can mark this paid." An Only-you bill never sends a "marked paid" notification and shows no "We'll let Sam know" caption. A guest without bill access sees the denied page with no provider or amount. A removed bill opened from a stale notification shows "That bill was removed." Offline keeps the cached values and disables the actions, each with its reason. Due today is drawn as if viewed on Fri 23 Oct.

INSTEAD OF
- Instead of putting the provenance block or the trend above the amount, draw them beneath it, because they are evidence for the number the reader came to see.
- Instead of paired bars or a second peer series, draw one reference line with its count of homes, because paired bars suggest one household competing with another.
- Instead of a confidence percentage on the read, draw the sentence "You confirmed the amount on Sat 3 Oct", because a percentage makes people distrust a value they already checked.
- Instead of a confirm dialog for Mark paid, draw the InlineUndo line and hold the household notice until Maya leaves, because undo prevents mis-taps without adding a step.
- Instead of a second label such as "Already paid" on an overdue bill, keep "Mark paid", because one job gets one button name.
- Instead of a greyed Mark paid for a member, draw LockedActionRow naming Maya, because Sam needs to know who can record it.
- Instead of "Reminders are off for this bill" on a repeating bill, say October's reminders stopped and list November's dates, because the series continues.
- Instead of a 404 or blank page for a removed bill, draw "That bill was removed." with Back to bills, because a stale notification must never dead-end.

DONE WHEN: Within five seconds, Maya can say what she owes, when it is due and what to press. After she presses Mark paid, the page says Sam will be told, and he is told only once, when she leaves. Sam lands on a calm, loop-phase Paid page naming Maya, with no provenance or trend. No October reminder can reach Sam after the bill is paid, and November's reminder dates are visible. The visibility line is true on every frame. Every arrival resolves to this bill or to a designed fallback, never a list or a blank page.
