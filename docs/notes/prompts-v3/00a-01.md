ANATOMY (on 01-anatomy: each silhouette at L 56pt with callouts, then all sizes in a row)
- OFFICIAL: a solid disc. The diameter equals the size.
- UNCONFIRMED: a ring with the same outer diameter and a transparent centre. Stroke width: XS 12 → 1.5px, S 16 → 2px, M 24 → 3px, L 56 → 7px. Never thinner than 1.5px.
- YOU ADDED: a solid disc with a tick KNOCKED OUT of it. The tick is a true cut-out that shows the surface behind, not a second colour painted on top. The tick spans the middle 56% of the diameter. Its stroke width equals the ring stroke for that size (XS 1.5, S 2, M 3, L 7), with round caps and joins. This drawing survives one-colour tinting.
- Ink: text.secondary (4.83:1 on base) or darker in light mode; dark text.secondary in dark mode. Add a crossed-out swatch row labelled "never border.* · never text.muted" (border.default is 1.24:1, text.muted is 2.54:1).
- Sizes, each with its use printed:
  XS 12pt / 12dp / 12px: the smallest size at which the three shapes can still be told apart (strip cells, widget, tray previews).
  S 16pt: list rows and captions.
  M 24pt: the sheet legend.
  L 56pt: the provenance sheet header.
  OG 28px at 1200 wide: the share-card minimum.
- Below 12pt, no mark is drawn. Show a row labelled "10pt → no mark" that prints the words instead: "Pickup day · Thursday · On record, not confirmed".
- Spacing: 8pt (spacing.2) between the mark and its text, shown with a dimension line.
- LEGEND specimen (at M 24pt, stacked): [drawn official mark] "Official" · [drawn unconfirmed mark] "On record, not confirmed" · [drawn you-added mark] "You added this". Where a legend is written "● Official · ○ … · ✓ …", those characters stand for the drawn marks: always render the vector mark, never a font character.

ACCESSIBILITY (print on 01-anatomy · light)
- Spoken names are fixed: "official", "on record, not confirmed", "you added this". The name is appended to the row's merged label:
  "Flood zone, Zone X, minimal flood hazard, FEMA, official"
  "Pickup day Thursday, City of Camas, on record, not confirmed"
  "Warranty ends, water heater, in 54 days, Saturday 12 December, you added this"
- Non-colour encoding: the shape; colour never tells the marks apart.
- Target rule: the mark is never a tap target on its own. The enclosing row or cell is the target (B13).
- Contrast: at least 3:1 in both themes, including the tinted widget. Scales with Dynamic Type or sp up to AX5 / 200%.
- Each surface shows a legend or a first-occurrence word once.

COPY (print on 01-anatomy · light)

INSTEAD OF (print on 01-anatomy · light)
- Instead of filled vs hollow for any other meaning (sender vs you in compare, taken vs open founding slots, density), draw labelled pointers, or solid vs hatched segments plus a numeral — because filled vs hollow means provenance and nothing else.
- Instead of a confidence percentage, bar or star, draw one of the three marks plus its word — because the product never grades certainty.
- Instead of a hollow mark on an official public dataset (EPA radon zone, USFS wildfire hazard), draw the filled mark and put the limit in the caption's scope word ("county-wide estimate") — because hollow means "not confirmed", not "coarse".
- Instead of a tick painted in a second colour on top of the disc, knock the tick out — because a second colour disappears under one-colour widget tinting.
- Instead of a light tinted widget in primary.500, use primary.700 on surface.base — because primary.500 is 2.77:1 on white and marks need 3:1.
- Instead of dimming a tinted widget mark for stale, keep the full tint and add the age words — because a dimmed tint drops below 3:1.
- Instead of making an 8px dot the tap target, make the enclosing row or cell the target — because a small mark cannot be hit reliably.
- Instead of a bare "Unverified" suffix on every row, show the word once per surface in a legend — because repetition is noise and "unverified" is forbidden.
- Instead of text.secondary at 50% for stale, use text.primary at 50% — because the first drops to 1.98:1.
- Instead of one age suffix on every row, give each fact its own stale caption — because an effective date or your own entry does not age.

NOTES ENTRIES (subheading "01 ProvenanceMark")
