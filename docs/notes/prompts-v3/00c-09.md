ACCESSIBILITY (panel)
- The reason is at 4.5:1 or better (text.strong on sunken 9.37:1; text.secondary on base 4.83:1).
- The link names its destination: "Verify address, opens Verify this address".
- The disabled button is exposed with its reason as a hint: "Message your neighbors, dimmed. Address verification needed to message your neighbors."
- The offline link is exposed as dimmed, with its reason as a hint.
- Row and link targets are 44pt / 48dp / 44px.

COPY RULES (panel)
- The pattern is "Address verification needed to <specific action>". Any extra context (V5) follows as a second sentence.
- Never write a generic "Verification required".
- When someone else can act, name them ("Maya can add bills here").
- Dates beyond tomorrow carry the relative count (V4).

PLATFORMS (panel): No platform differences.

INSTEAD OF (panel)
- Instead of red or amber colouring, draw the neutral lock glyph in text.secondary. Nothing is wrong; something is not yet available.
- Instead of text.muted for the reason or the disabled link, draw text.strong on sunken or text.secondary on base, because both must be read.
- Instead of a present but inert control with no explanation, draw the control with this row under it.
- Instead of an "Address verification needed…" reason for a saved-place user on a home-only feature, draw V7 "Claim this address first". Verification means nothing before a claim.
