THE VISUALIZATION DECISION: kind picker is a two-column grid of glyph tiles — one tap, no wizard step — above a native date control that must accept ~2 years out (31 Mar 2027 is an ordinary entry, not an edge case). Then optional title. Then "Remind me" as a four-segment control, 60 / 30 / 7 / 1 days, where the chosen lead draws a short leader line from the segment back up to the date and labels the actual day ("Mon 30 Nov 2026") — so the reminder is visibly earlier than the event instead of an abstract number. Seeded mode puts the hollow provenance mark beside the date with the authority as a caption, locks the fields, and keeps Remind me and the dispute control live. The collision warning renders the conflicting row inline, with its own title, date and provenance mark.

WHY IT IS SHAPED THIS WAY: pickup day is a KIND with a recurrence branch, not its own control — that is what gives a saved-place user any pickup control at all and gives the pickup push's caveat a one-tap destination. 60 days must exist in Remind me: it is the only lead anyone ever picks for a lease notice.

STATES TO DRAW, each its own frame: create (kind picker, nothing chosen); create with pickup day and the recurrence branch open; view-mine (edit / remind / delete); view-seeded read-only (tax appeal, then voter registration with its per-method dates and "I did this"); collision — "Water heater warranty — Rheem 40-gal · 12 Dec 2026 is already on file", with Replace / Add as a separate calendar event / Cancel; saving; saved; validation (past date 1 Sep 2026; and a 60-day lead chosen on HOA dues 15 days away); permission-denied (household member without calendar.edit — fields visible, disabled, reason stated); deleted with an Undo toast; offline — save disabled, "You're offline"; bill mode with the OCR value unreadable and the manual fallback.

DO NOT: do not turn this into a multi-step wizard — the kind is one tap, not a step; do not infer the recycling date from the trash day, because "Not set" is a real shippable value; do not use a destructive confirm dialog for delete — delete immediately with an Undo toast; do not let a seeded row look editable or a user-entered row look official; do not cap "Remind me" at 30 days; do not draw a separate sheet per kind.
