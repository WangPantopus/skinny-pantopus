Use the Pantopus house style, honesty rules and platform specs from the top of this pack.

SCREEN: the positioning line and the contrast line in the hero — a typographic spec applied across five existing surfaces.
PLATFORMS: web 1440×900 and 390×844 (plus a 320px check), iOS 393×852, Android 412×915, and a 1200×630 OG footer crop. Light and dark.
THIS IS: an EXTENSION of existing designed screens — "/start funnel hero", "Marketing homepage hero", "Place launch (iOS)", "Place launch (Android)" and "Place OG share card". These already exist and are already designed in the Pantopus design system. Open each, keep everything, and add only one new line of type plus its placement rule.
WHERE IT LIVES: Place tab → launch screen on native (this screen is the Place tab's root for a user with no place); on web /start it is signed-out and has no tab bar; the marketing homepage sits outside the app shell entirely.
HOW THE USER GETS HERE: taps a friend's /start?vs= compare link, scans a mailed postcard, or lands on pantopus.com cold.
THE ONE JOB: say in one sentence what Pantopus is and, directly under it, what it is not, so a stranger arriving from a friend's card knows in two lines why this is not another neighborhood app.

CONTENT (exact strings — use verbatim, these are single shared constants):

Web /start lede: "Wildfire and flood risk, today's air, radon, water, who represents you, and the neighbors who've proven they're real. Free, no account."

Marketing eyebrow, unchanged: "Your address, answered · Early access"
NEW contrast line, under the lede on all three platforms and in the OG footer: "Nextdoor is what your neighbors say. Pantopus is what's on record about your address."
Below it, unchanged: the address autocomplete field, the "See your place" primary, and the privacy proof line.

THE VISUALIZATION DECISION: three distinct typographic ranks stacked with nothing between them. H1 at 31/37/700 on mobile and 42/48/700 at 1440, text.primary. Lede at 15.5/23/400, text.secondary, max 46ch. Contrast line at label or caption weight (13/18 or 12/16), text.muted — and the contrast is carried by WEIGHT alone inside the line: clause one "Nextdoor is what your neighbors say." at 400 in text.muted, clause two "Pantopus is what's on record about your address." at 500–600 in text.strong. One line at 640px and above. It must read as a caption on the lede, never as a second paragraph, or the hero becomes three competing blocks of prose and the address field falls below the fold. The OG footer crop uses the same two-weight treatment scaled up, full width under both columns.

DO NOT: do not set the contrast line at lede weight as a second paragraph. Do not put it in a pill, badge, card, callout or blockquote, and do not add quote marks, an icon or a divider rule.
