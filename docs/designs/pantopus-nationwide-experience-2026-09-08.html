01 / START WITH SOMETHING REAL

One useful finding. One next step.

Intake makes a reviewed private note. A source-backed discovery earns the next tap. The bill view shows reproducible arithmetic with explicit inputs; it does not invent a reason for higher usage.

02 / KEEP THE BOUNDARIES VISIBLE

Your home is not your browsing radius.

Home is private to Jamie. Nearby can explore another area without claiming residency or gaining household access. Public source information, personal eligibility and live availability remain separate facts.

03 / RETURN FOR A REAL CHANGE

Saved should mean dependable.

The event source changes from 6 PM to 7 PM. Jamie’s saved time stays at 6 PM until accepted. Turning phone notifications off never removes the change from Inbox. Nothing is sent to a device.
