The rounded square that carries a section's Lucide glyph.

**Provide:** `icon`, `tone` (`home` default: the Place accent on `color-identity-home-bg`; `muted` for empty, unavailable and locked; `sky` for informational or selectable tiles), `size` (34 default, 32 inline, 42 on hero cards).

The glyph is 56% of the tile at stroke 2; the radius is an off-scale 9px at every size. (The 42px tiles on HeroCard and AhaCard are their own spans with `radius-xl`.) Source: `frontend/apps/web/src/components/archetypes/place/primitives.tsx`.
