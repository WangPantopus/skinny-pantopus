The quiet note that closes a detail screen or explains a status.

**Provide:** `children` (one or two sentences), optional `tone` (`neutral` default, `warning`, `sky`) and `className`.

12.5/18 text with an info glyph, `radius-xl`, and a 1px border (`app-border` for neutral, the tone's `-light` token otherwise). Use it for disclaimers and privacy promises: "Screening, not a diagnosis.", "Your exact address is never shown to neighbors." Source: `frontend/apps/web/src/components/archetypes/place/detail.tsx`.
