A segmented progress bar for wizards, with a "Step N of M" readout.

**Provide:** `step` and `totalSteps`, optional `hideReadout`.

Segments are 4px tall pills 6px apart: filled `color-primary-600`, empty `app-surface-sunken` (3.72:1 between them in light). The readout is 11px/600 uppercase `app-text-secondary`. Native: `SegmentedProgressBar` (6pt segments, 4pt gap). Source: `frontend/apps/web/src/components/archetypes/primitives/ProgressSegments.tsx`.
