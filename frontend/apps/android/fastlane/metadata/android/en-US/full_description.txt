Real life, connected.

Pantopus is one app for the place where your life happens: your home, the people you live with, the plans you make, the mail you get and what’s going on around you. Pan means all. The octopus is one mind with many arms.

There’s more to your address than a pin
Type any U.S. address to see air and weather right now; flood, wildfire, earthquake and radon; who supplies your water, and its record; and the governments you live inside. Every fact names its source and its scale. Free to look, no account needed.

A little less to keep in your head
Today draws the sky over your home and puts what’s due inside right beside it. Confirm your pickup day once and get a reminder the evening before. Turn a radon zone into a test-kit task. Briefings come only when there’s something useful.

You don’t have to be the household’s memory
Invite the people you live with. Give a task an owner and a due date, keep documents where the right people can find them, and see when something’s done. For the place you own, rent or share.

Good company can start with a small plan
Walk and coffee. A pickup game. A watch party. A study group. Find one nearby, ask to join, and get the exact spot once the host says yes. Invited by a friend? Say you’re in, get directions, add it to your calendar and talk in the plan’s group chat. Playdates have extra rules: a public place and the host’s approval.

Feel the pulse around you
Pulse is what’s happening around you, as a list or a live map: questions, useful updates and nearby news. Calm by design, with no crime or suspicious-person alerts.

Mail, reinvented
Mail arrives each morning at 7, stamped and sealed, and opens like a real envelope. This season, send a Christmas card that stays sealed until Christmas morning.

Ask Pantopus
AI that knows your place. It answers with sources, says so when it doesn’t know, and turns an answer into a task or a plan that waits for your tap.

Support Trains
Organize meals or practical help for someone you care about, share a schedule, and coordinate volunteers.

Clear boundaries
Other people on Pantopus see a first name and a street, never your house number or unit. Looking up an address shows public information; it never opens a household’s tasks or documents. Verification unlocks the features that need it, like messaging verified residents near you. Your address is never sold and never used for ads.

Home features are U.S.-only for now, and what’s available varies by location.
