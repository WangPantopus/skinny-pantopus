Know what matters for your home, and stay on top of it.

Pantopus keeps the things that come around again and again at your home in one calm place, and reminds you before they slip.

Today, for your address
See today's weather, air quality and active alerts for where you live, with the source of each fact.

Trash night, handled
Tell Pantopus your pickup days once. The evening before each pickup you get one reminder, with a "Bins out" button. Evenings without a pickup stay quiet.

Radon, checked
Pantopus shows whether radon testing matters where you live. If your home hasn't been tested, it becomes a dated task with a reminder.

Home tasks for the whole household
Invite the people you live with. Assign tasks, get a reminder the morning they're due, and hear when someone finishes one.

Support Trains
Organize meals or help for a friend or neighbor: share a schedule, let people sign up for a slot, and Pantopus reminds every helper.

Nearby
See what's happening around you, from neighbors and clearly labeled local updates.

Private by default
Your address stays private, and you choose what neighbors can see.

Pantopus is starting with first-time homeowners in Vancouver, Camas and Washougal, Washington, and works for any address in the United States.
