Know what matters for your home, and stay on top of it.

Pantopus brings your place, household tasks, and nearby community together.

Today, for your address
See weather, air quality, and local information with sources, plus useful reminders for your household.

A home you can manage together
Keep household tasks and home information in one place. Invite the people you live with, assign tasks, and keep track of what needs doing.

Pickup days and home reminders
Save your trash and recycling schedule, manage notification preferences, and make a plan for recurring home tasks such as radon testing.

Nearby plans and conversations
See nearby posts and local updates, organize a small plan, request to join, and message people directly. Exact meeting details are shared according to the plan’s privacy and approval settings.

Support Trains
Organize meals or practical help for someone you care about, share a schedule, and coordinate volunteers.

Privacy and control
Choose what you share, manage notification settings, and control access to your household information.

Pantopus is available across the United States. Local information and service availability vary by address.
